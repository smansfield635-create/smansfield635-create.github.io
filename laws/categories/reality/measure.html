Measurement Contract Law

Observer Invariance Law

Primary relationship

The number and its meaning cannot be separated.

The value, unit, denominator or reference, uncertainty, and decision rule form one measurement object.

MEASUREMENT = value + unit + reference + uncertainty + decision rule
ValueObserved quantity
UnitDeclared scale
ReferenceBaseline or denominator
UncertaintyError or variability
Decision ruleThreshold and interpretation

Engineering controls

Declare units, calibration, sampling frame, normalization, aggregation, thresholds, error handling, and zero or missing-value behavior.

Failure behavior

If observers can change classification by silently changing scale, reference, calibration, or threshold, the measurement contract is incomplete.

Reading layers

Agreement, construction, and evidence remain distinct.

Measurement is agreement.

State what is being measured, how and when, what counts as enough, and what the number means for the real system.

Measurement requires coordinate definitions and error bounds.

Calibration, sampling, normalization, uncertainty, and threshold behavior remain explicit.
