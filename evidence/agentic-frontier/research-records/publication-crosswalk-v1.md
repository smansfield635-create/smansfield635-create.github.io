## Status vocabulary

- `SUPPORTED` — a bounded empirical result passed its frozen decision rule.
- `CONFIGURATION_BOUNDED` — an observed comparative result is valid only for the tested configurations and cannot be generalized to all configurations or causal mechanisms.
- `NOT_DISTINGUISHED` — the tested candidate did not meet the frozen separation criterion against its comparator.
- `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE` — a frozen support, invariance, verification, or identifiability gate prevented the substantive hypothesis from being lawfully adjudicated.
- `FORMAL_PROOF` — a mathematical/formal result holds under explicitly stated axioms/model class; it is not empirical validation.
- `FORMAL_COUNTEREXAMPLE` — a universal formal claim is rejected within the stated model class by a valid counterexample.
- `PREREGISTERED_NOT_EXECUTED` — protocol/decision rule is frozen but the empirical outcome has not been opened.
- `METHODOLOGICAL_DIAGNOSIS` — a result concerns experimental design/geometry rather than direct support for the candidate theory.
- `HISTORICAL_CONTEXT` — retained context that may explain provenance/history but is not a current headline scientific result.
