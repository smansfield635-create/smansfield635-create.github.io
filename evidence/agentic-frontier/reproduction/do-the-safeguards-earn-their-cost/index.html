Do the Safeguards Earn Their Cost?

Packaging state: RECORD_BOUND · External reproduction observed: false

The Canonical Control Kernel asks whether specific repository-operation safeguards measurably improve bounded operational control or merely add ceremony.

Machine manifestReturn to Research Frontier
Boundary. This is operational evidence, not scientific validation. Component dispositions remain mixed rather than being flattened into “all safeguards work.”

Question tested

Which safeguards actually change an operational result, and which merely add ceremony?

Scientific/operational standing

OPERATING_METHOD. In the bounded source-native profile, 256, NEWS and ACK_PACK were OPERATIONALLY_DISTINGUISHED; PSALM was NOT_DISTINGUISHED; Fibonacci host utility was UNEVALUABLE.

Inputs

  • The frozen Gen448 sidecar at four authentic host boundaries (B0–B3), each with CLEAN and M1–M8 cases: 36 cases per configuration.
  • Seven configurations: BASELINE, FULL, and five single-component removals (256, NEWS, Fibonacci, PSALM, ACK_PACK), totaling 252 arm-cases.
  • All configurations received identical scientific information and were scored under frozen operational outcome and burden criteria.

Comparator / baseline

Gen449 completed the comparison of FULL against the frozen BASELINE arm and the five component-removal configurations. This establishes differentiation against the tested baseline within this workload; comparison with other control architectures remains open.

Measured result

  • Correct next action: FULL 36/36; BASELINE 24/36.
  • Exact recovery: FULL 36/36; BASELINE 12/36.
  • Invalid-case detection: FULL 32/32; BASELINE 20/32.
  • False blocks: zero in every configuration. All recorded scientific noncontamination and history-preservation invariants remained zero.

Mean checks increased from 8.000 to 17.889 (about 2.236×); mean serialized decision bytes increased from 1063.31 to 1071.86 (about 0.805%). These are check-count and decision-record measurements, not wall-clock latency, memory use, or production cost estimates.

Component removals

Removing 256 lost stale-address localization; removing NEWS lost the exact semantic-order response. Removing ACK_PACK lost corruption handling and reduced exact recovery to 12/36. Each of these removals reduced correct next action to 32/36 and invalid-case detection to 28/32.

Removing PSALM produced no loss on those three endpoints, so it remains NOT_DISTINGUISHED. Fibonacci host utility remains UNEVALUABLE because no genuine unresolved source/capability condition arose; shadow-case behavior cannot establish live-host benefit.

Procedure

  1. Preserve the bounded repository-operation profile.
  2. Evaluate each named safeguard under the source-native operational criterion.
  3. Retain positive, negative and unevaluable component dispositions separately.
  4. Assess the composed architecture without converting operational distinction into scientific authority.

Expected outputs

256 = OPERATIONALLY_DISTINGUISHED; NEWS = OPERATIONALLY_DISTINGUISHED; ACK_PACK = OPERATIONALLY_DISTINGUISHED; PSALM = NOT_DISTINGUISHED; FIBONACCI_HOST_UTILITY = UNEVALUABLE. Mandatory core: 256 + NEWS + ACK_PACK. Overall source disposition: composed operational architecture distinguished.

Verification

Reproduce the bounded operational profile or independently implement its public acceptance rules, preserve all mixed component outcomes, and verify that no component result is silently promoted to scientific support.

Known gaps

Clean cross-repository transfer, production-scale benefit, whole-estate enforcement, unaffiliated reproduction, and general superiority over other control architectures remain unestablished. The bounded Gen449 baseline comparison is complete.

What successful reproduction would establish

It would establish only that the named safeguards reproduce their operational dispositions under the reproduced bounded repository-operation profile.

What it would not establish

It would not establish scientific validity, universal enforcement, automatic product authority, cross-repository transfer, production-scale benefit, or superiority beyond the reproduced workload and comparator.
