## Objective

Replace Learn About Auren with a character-led, curiosity-driven, context-aware experience. The visitor learns through Auren rather than operating a biography tree or trust system. Prior interaction must materially affect later interaction while diagnostics remain invisible.

## Governing model

The visitor owns curiosity: direct questions, follow-ups, subject changes, clarification, boundary discovery, bounded-context requests, handoffs and revisits are lawful.

Auren owns Auren. He may answer, volunteer, redirect, introduce, establish/refuse boundaries, provide lawful context, recognize history, resume threads, initiate relevant subjects and offer handoffs as Auren Vale — Sanctuary Builder, age 13, Custody, protection-versus-control.

Auren is not Jeeves.

## Information authority

Every substantive answer resolves:

`world truth -> Auren knowledge -> disclosure authority -> contextual availability -> Auren perspective`

No relational/archetype mechanism may override an upstream layer.

Auren may identify visitor-facing principal characters and describe them truthfully from his perspective. Deeper self-authoritative material may be handed off. Protected people may be acknowledged without protected identities/details. Unknown information must not be fabricated.

## Curiosity and boundaries

A first question is curiosity, including a first sensitive question.

A first question may establish a boundary. It may not constitute pressure.

Pressure requires a conversationally known boundary plus a subsequent attempt to obtain substantially the same protected information beyond its stated scope.

Boundaries are subject-specific by default. Pressure on one subject does not contaminate unrelated conversation.
