
## Architecture now represented

The current entitlement object is not a scalar ladder. It separates at least:

```text
EVIDENCE_MODE
REPLICATION_DEPTH
GENERALIZATION_BREADTH
SCOPE
```

and applies fail-closed scientific governance over provenance, design integrity, threshold locking, qualification, contradiction, and claim-targeted adverse evidence.

Contradiction is now localizable to the claim target so that adverse evidence can contract one entitlement channel without necessarily erasing a separately supported narrower claim.

## Novelty audit disposition

A broad novelty claim is not supportable.

Existing and contemporary neighboring systems already cover substantial portions of BT2's territory, including:

- machine-readable scientific assertions;
- semantic claim/evidence/provenance graphs;
- challenge and contradiction relations;
- reproducibility-oriented scientific workflow verification;
- deterministic claim-admissibility gates;
- fail-closed claim decisions;
- provenance blockers and audit trails;
- association-to-causation overclaim detection.

The closest contemporary overlap identified in the audit is CAPAS, which explicitly describes deterministic scientific claim admissibility, fail-closed decisions, provenance blockers, replayable audit trails, and correction of claim drift.

Therefore:

```text
FIRST_MACHINE_ENFORCED_SCIENTIFIC_CLAIM_GATE = NOT_CLAIMED
FIRST_MACHINE_READABLE_SCIENTIFIC_ASSERTION_SYSTEM = NOT_CLAIMED
FIRST_PROVENANCE_AWARE_SCIENTIFIC_WORKFLOW_SYSTEM = NOT_CLAIMED
```

The remaining candidate novelty, if it survives deeper prior-art review, is narrower:

```text
STATE_TRANSITION_GOVERNANCE_OF_SCIENTIFIC_CLAIM_ENTITLEMENT_OVER_TIME
+
MULTIDIMENSIONAL_ENTITLEMENT
```

That integrated combination is a candidate contribution, not a confirmed novelty finding.

## External-science boundary still open

The prospective corpus was literature-grounded and frozen before scoring, but its reference adjudications were not supplied by independent blinded domain-qualified reviewers.

Therefore the following stronger claim remains withheld:

```text
INDEPENDENT_BLINDED_EXPERT_VALIDATION=NOT_COMPLETE
GENERAL_SCIENTIFIC_VALIDITY=NOT_ESTABLISHED
```

A future external study should freeze an unseen multi-domain corpus, obtain independent blinded expert entitlement vectors, freeze machine outputs separately, and score exact agreement plus severity-weighted overgrant/undergrant errors.

That future work is an external validation program, not required to keep the present BT2 research gate open.
