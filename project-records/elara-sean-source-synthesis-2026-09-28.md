## Post-interview corrections

### Dignity — SEAN_SOURCE

Dignity is an **internal frame of reference**.

Sean accepts as constitutive of Dignity:
- recognition of one's own value,
- respect for oneself,
- recognition of the value of others.

Dignity should not be reduced to self-worth alone because its internal frame also preserves recognition of other human value.

### Humility / Dignity correlation — SEAN_SOURCE

Humility and Dignity should not be represented as a simple causal arrow.

Sean clarified the relationship as more like a **correlation**:
- **Dignity** is inward-facing: the internal frame of reference.
- **Humility** has an outward-facing relational expression: what others receive externally.

Do not claim “Humility produces Dignity” unless Sean later establishes that stronger causal relationship.

### Free Will — SEAN_SOURCE

Free Will is better understood as an **operational standard**, not merely a summit reached late in a climb.

Sean's relational formulation remains central:

> By one's own free will, one's actions should enable the free will and liberty of others.

The mature exercise of agency should expand rather than diminish another person's capacity to choose and become themselves.

### Self-Control — SEAN_SOURCE

Self-Control operates across the other summits/qualities as a **moderator**.

Sean's sharper post-interview formulation:

**Self-Control is the barometer for Free Will.**

Therefore Self-Control should not be forced above or below Patience as though it were simply another rung. It helps indicate the condition in which choice and the other qualities are being exercised.

### Love — SEAN_SOURCE

Love is relational and governing rather than merely emotional.

Free Will is an operational expression/standard within Love: freely exercised agency reaches its strongest relational form when it enables rather than constrains the liberty and free will of others.

Do not reduce Love to a final badge of completion.
