Why the Compass exists

Diamond Gate Bridge brings products, worlds, measurement tools, standards, experiments, and stories into one estate. The Compass keeps those destinations findable without forcing them into one menu or one kind of page.

Each direction groups rooms that answer a different kind of question. North helps you get oriented. East opens worlds and maps. South leads to measurement and governing standards. West leads to things being built and tested.

You do not need to understand the architecture before using it. Bring a question, rotate the estate, and follow the room that makes sense.

How this estate is built

The pages you see are tested as one connected system.

Before a public page is merged, the estate checks whether it works in a real browser, whether its interactions still mean what they are supposed to mean, and whether the result still meets the site's visual and navigational standards.
