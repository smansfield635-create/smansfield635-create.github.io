Approved editorial graphics may be used only as authored context.

Every newly normalized asset must pass the frozen treatment law.

### Shirts

Automatic fail:

## 21. Candidate disposition

Once exact technical checks pass, the candidate enters physical/perceptual review.

Allowed owner dispositions:

`KEEP | ONE_REPAIR | REJECT`

### KEEP
Candidate becomes eligible for later merge/release steps.

### ONE_REPAIR
Exactly one bounded repair pass may be authorized against the same frozen acceptance framework.

The repair may not expand scope.

### REJECT
Candidate is not accepted.

Rejection does not invalidate the frozen baseline, scope, source manifest, treatment law, content hierarchy, or implementation boundary unless the owner explicitly changes one of those decisions.

## 22. No self-approval law

Construction completion is not acceptance.

The builder may prove:
- exact diff;
- exact identities;
- static/runtime conformance;
- responsive evidence.

The builder may not convert those proofs into owner perceptual acceptance.

Human disposition remains distinct.
