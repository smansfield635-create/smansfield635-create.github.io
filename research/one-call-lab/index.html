The 12-month stabilization framework · plan, not historical results

Three phases. One measurable coaching loop.

01
Months 1–3
32–35%

Identify volatility. Review price-anchor timing, surface objections early, and record what interrupted decision clarity.

02
Months 4–6
33–36%

Build repeatability. Reinforce first-call confidence, practice responses to recurring objections, and track when follow-up is needed.

03
Months 7–12
34–38%

Hold consistency. Confirm buying agreement clearly before commitment and monitor cancellations after the sale.

Closing-rate percentages above are phase targets drawn from the stabilization-plan screenshot. No achieved phase results, recordings, Rilla scorecards, or CRM denominators were supplied.

Measurement rule: define the appointment population and time window before comparing periods. A weekly close rate, a monthly net close rate, and an annual revenue total are different measurements.
