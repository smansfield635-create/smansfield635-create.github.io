# 1. Purpose

The 3×3 is a semantic model, not a requirement to render nine equal text boxes in one viewport.

The refined Meaning paragraphs in publication crosswalk v2 are the semantic source. They are **not** the required first-view typography.

The public Meaning experience must turn those paragraphs into editorial contrast:

`SETUP -> CONTRAST -> PRACTICAL SIGNIFICANCE -> VISIBLE BOUNDARY -> OPTIONAL DEPTH`

The contract exists to prevent the successor page from degenerating into a dashboard of long prose blocks.

# 3. Page-scale chapter model

The nine Meaning objects are organized into three substantial chapters:

1. `DISCOVERY` — **What we are learning** — G11, G12, G13
2. `RESEARCH_METHOD` — **How the work is done** — G21, G22, G23
3. `INDEPENDENT_PROGRAM` — **How this was built** — G31, G32, G33

Each chapter should read as one coherent editorial sequence rather than three unrelated widgets.

On a sufficiently wide desktop canvas a chapter may present three Meaning cards in one row. That is a maximum, not a requirement.

If three columns would compromise the contrast beats, support copy, boundary visibility, or readable line length, the chapter must collapse to two columns or one column before typography is reduced.

The successor must not force all nine objects into a single viewport merely to preserve the visual appearance of a matrix.

---

# 4. Shared Meaning-face anatomy

Every Meaning face contains five semantic roles:

1. **Human object title** — the stable crosswalk object name.
2. **Immediate beats** — the dominant editorial contrast, normally two or three short statements.
3. **Support line** — one compact plain-language sentence explaining practical significance.
4. **Visible boundary** — an always-visible statement of what is not yet established.
5. **Progressive depth** — optional fuller Meaning explanation and explicit path to Evidence.

The immediate view must not require reading a paragraph before the central idea becomes intelligible.

The boundary is not secondary legal copy. It is part of the Meaning proposition and receives its own reserved visual region.

---

# 5. Shared spatial rules

- Use whitespace as part of the explanation, not as leftover space.
- Every Meaning face must have at least one strong contrast zone that can be understood at a glance.
- Dominant beats should remain short enough to function as display language. If they wrap excessively, expand or collapse the layout rather than shrinking them into body copy.
- Support copy should remain a compact readable measure; target roughly `42–62ch` where the surface permits.
- No body copy is truncated with ellipsis merely to preserve a card height.
- The boundary receives a distinct footer, rail, field, or equivalent persistent region and remains visible before expansion.
- Equal card heights are subordinate to semantic legibility. A chapter may use controlled height variation if the visual system remains coherent.
- Visual hierarchy is shared; composition geometry may vary by object.
- Scientific, Agentic, Formal, Engineering, and Operating Context remain Evidence/domain tags, not Meaning-side layout categories.

---

# 6. Composition archetypes

## `SPLIT_CONTRAST`

Two propositions are given independent spatial weight. The visual relationship may be side-by-side, stacked opposition, or separated by a clear axis. Neither side may disappear on narrow layouts; narrow layouts stack the same semantic order.

## `NOT_EQUAL_LOGIC`

A false equivalence is broken explicitly. The `≠` glyph may be visually prominent, but accessible text must state the inequality in words. The symbol is never the sole carrier of meaning.

## `QUESTION_NEGATIVE_SPACE`

A genuine unresolved question receives deliberate room. Empty space is used to signal an open boundary, not to imply missing implementation. The answer may not be visually fabricated with speculative charts or pseudo-results.

## `SEQUENCE_CHALLENGE`

The Meaning is expressed as a short sequence in which one stage is deliberately challenged by another. Sequence does not imply that passage through the stages proves scientific correctness.

---

# 7. Progressive-disclosure contract

## Immediate Meaning view

Must expose, without expansion:

- human object title;
- immediate contrast beats;
- one support line;
- visible boundary;
- explicit control to inspect Evidence.

## Optional Meaning depth

A control such as `Why this matters` may reveal the fuller refined Meaning paragraph or a semantically equivalent expansion.

The expansion may add context but may not introduce a stronger causal, novelty, generality, or validation claim than the crosswalk source.

## Evidence transition

`Evidence` is an explicit state/control, not a hidden hover behavior. The Evidence face may remain technically dense and is governed by publication crosswalk v2 rather than this composition contract.

The Meaning composition must not consume technical Evidence space merely to make the Meaning face look more impressive.

# 8. Nine source-bound editorial treatments

## G11 — Seeing fragility before failure

- **gridId:** `G11`
- **humanObject:** `Seeing fragility before failure`
- **archetype:** `SPLIT_CONTRAST`
- **immediateBeats:**
  - `IT STILL LOOKS HEALTHY.`
  - `BUT ITS ROOM TO RECOVER IS SHRINKING.`
  - `Can that loss be measured before failure becomes obvious?`
- **supportLine:** This research asks whether losing future options can improve prediction before ordinary signs of failure dominate; on one frozen power-system benchmark, it did improve prediction.
- **visibleBoundary:** `Still unresolved: whether the same signal works beyond that setting, and why.`
- **progressiveDisclosure:** `Why this matters` may expose the full refined G11 Meaning source. `Evidence` opens the LVTG / RTS24 technical record and its Gen429 non-identifiable boundary.
- **spatialRule:** Give the first beat a broad/open field and the second a visibly tighter or more constrained field. The contrast may suggest shrinking option-space, but must not present a live health gauge, fabricated measurement, or universal failure curve.
- **motionRule:** Optional subtle contraction may reinforce the second beat. Static rendering must communicate the complete contrast. Reduced motion removes the contraction without loss of meaning.
- **accessibilityText:** `A system can appear healthy while its room to recover shrinks. The research tests whether that hidden loss of options can improve failure prediction. One frozen benchmark improved, but generality and mechanism remain unresolved.`
- **sourceMeaning:** A system can still look healthy even as its room to recover is shrinking. This research asks whether that hidden loss of options can be measured early enough to improve what we can predict about failure. On one frozen power-system benchmark, it did improve prediction. What remains unresolved is whether the same signal works beyond that setting, and why.

## G12 — Making coherence precise

- **gridId:** `G12`
- **humanObject:** `Making coherence precise`
- **archetype:** `SPLIT_CONTRAST`
- **immediateBeats:**
  - `“COHERENT” IS EASY TO SAY.`
  - `SCIENCE NEEDS SOMETHING IT CAN TEST.`
- **supportLine:** The work turns parts of an intuitive idea into mathematical objects that can be proved, challenged, contradicted, and exhaustively checked.
- **visibleBoundary:** `Formal results hold inside their stated models. Literature priority and physical usefulness remain open.`
- **progressiveDisclosure:** `Why this matters` may restore the fuller distinction between intuitive language and formal objects. `Evidence` opens the formal theorem, Diamond Operator, counterexample, and non-identifiable records.
- **spatialRule:** Oppose an ordinary-language field with a formally structured field. The formal field may use definitions, relation marks, or restrained geometric structure, but must not use physics/cosmology imagery in a way that implies empirical validation.
- **motionRule:** Optional transition from an imprecise word field to a formal relation field is allowed. Static labels and structure must remain sufficient.
- **accessibilityText:** `The word coherent is intuitive. This work makes parts of that intuition mathematically precise enough to test formally. The formal results are model-bounded; novelty and physical usefulness remain unresolved.`
- **sourceMeaning:** Words like “coherent,” “fractured,” or “holding together” are intuitive, but science cannot test an intuition until it becomes precise. This work turns parts of that language into mathematical objects that can be proved, challenged, and exhaustively checked. The formal results hold inside their stated models. Whether the objects are genuinely new in the literature—or useful descriptions of the physical world—still has to be established.

## G21 — Good reasoning is not permission to act

- **gridId:** `G21`
- **humanObject:** `Good reasoning is not permission to act`
- **archetype:** `NOT_EQUAL_LOGIC`
- **immediateBeats:**
  - `THIS APPEARS CORRECT`
  - `≠`
  - `AUTHORIZED TO CHANGE THE RECORD`
- **supportLine:** Before governed state changes, state identity, scope, and authority still have to agree.
- **visibleBoundary:** `Authority separation is demonstrated operationally; a reliability advantage over simpler workflows is not yet established.`
- **progressiveDisclosure:** `Why this matters` may explain stale state, incomplete review, and persuasive mistakes in ordinary language. `Evidence` opens canonical AI Entry / intake identities and the bounded Compass example.
- **spatialRule:** The inequality is the organizing axis. The two phrases must retain independent visual weight. The symbol must be accompanied by a textual equivalent in DOM/accessibility content.
- **motionRule:** Optional arrival from opposite sides is decorative only. Static inequality remains complete. Reduced motion uses no directional transition.
- **accessibilityText:** `Appearing correct is not the same as being authorized to change the record. State, scope, and authority must still be checked. The separation is operationally demonstrated, but its benefit over simpler workflows is not yet measured.`
- **sourceMeaning:** A system can reach a convincing conclusion and still be wrong, stale, incomplete, or unauthorized. Diamond Gate therefore separates “this appears correct” from “this is allowed to change the record.” The practical aim is simple: a persuasive answer should not quietly become reality until the relevant state, scope, and authority have been checked.

## G22 — Construction cannot grade itself

- **gridId:** `G22`
- **humanObject:** `Construction cannot grade itself`
- **archetype:** `SEQUENCE_CHALLENGE`
- **immediateBeats:**
  - `BUILD IT.`
  - `THEN LET SOMETHING ELSE TRY TO BREAK IT.`
- **supportLine:** Making, continuity checking, fracture testing, authorization, and recording are separated so disagreement and missing evidence can surface before completion is declared.
- **visibleBoundary:** `Role separation is implemented in bounded contexts. Its added benefit has not been isolated or scientifically validated.`
- **progressiveDisclosure:** `Why this matters` may explain independent challenge and false-confidence detection. `Evidence` opens the bounded PSALM / ACK_PACK / role-separated assurance record and its advisory ceiling.
- **spatialRule:** Use visibly distinct `BUILD` and `CHALLENGE` lanes or stages. The challenge stage must not be visually subordinate to construction. Completion is shown only after the challenge path, but passage must not be presented as proof of scientific truth.
- **motionRule:** Optional sequence progression is allowed only if every stage is also present in static/reduced-motion form. No auto-advancing sequence may hide the challenge stage.
- **accessibilityText:** `The process that builds something is not the only process that judges it. Diamond Gate separates construction from continuity and fracture checks. That separation is implemented, but its added reliability benefit has not yet been isolated.`
- **sourceMeaning:** The same process that builds something should not be the only process deciding whether it worked. Diamond Gate separates making, checking continuity, looking for fracture, authorizing execution, and recording the result. The purpose is not ceremony. It is to create places where disagreement, missing evidence, and false confidence can surface before completion is declared.

# 9. Chapter-to-chapter contrast

The three chapters should not feel identical.

## DISCOVERY

Primary visual tension: **what appears true now vs what the research is trying to expose or formalize**.

Recommended atmosphere: observational, spacious, question-driven.

## RESEARCH METHOD

Primary visual tension: **reasoning/construction vs independent authority/challenge**.

Recommended atmosphere: sharper logic, explicit separations, visible gates and inequalities.

## INDEPENDENT PROGRAM

Primary visual tension: **unusual operating context vs the limits of what context can prove**.

Recommended atmosphere: broad system view, provenance, transfer, restrained scale.

The visual system may share typography, spacing tokens, controls, and card mechanics while varying internal composition geometry across these chapter modes.

# 10. Responsive behavior

Responsive behavior is semantic before geometric.

1. Preserve reading order: title → contrast → support → boundary → depth/Evidence controls.
2. Collapse a three-card chapter row to two or one before display typography is reduced into body-text scale.
3. If a dominant beat exceeds roughly three visual lines at the active width, prefer more width or a stacked composition rather than smaller type.
4. Do not force matched card heights if doing so truncates support or boundary text.
5. On narrow screens, split contrasts stack in semantic order; inequalities become vertical only if their accessible reading remains unambiguous.
6. `QUESTION_NEGATIVE_SPACE` retains meaningful whitespace on mobile but must not create excessive dead scrolling.
7. Every Meaning boundary remains visible without opening a drawer.
8. The Evidence control remains reachable by touch and keyboard without requiring a hover state.

# 11. Accessibility contract

- Every visual contrast has a complete textual equivalent in the DOM.
- `≠`, arrows, connectors, spatial contraction, and sequence direction are reinforcement only.
- No distinction depends only on color.
- No required information appears only on hover.
- Keyboard and touch users can enter Meaning depth, inspect Evidence, and return without losing context.
- Reduced-motion mode preserves identical semantic order and all visible boundaries.
- Auto-advancing text is prohibited on the Meaning face.
- Focus order follows the semantic reading order, not decorative spatial position.
- Screen-reader labels distinguish `Why this matters` from `View technical evidence`.
- Decorative diagrams that could be mistaken for measured data must be marked/presented as conceptual or omitted.
