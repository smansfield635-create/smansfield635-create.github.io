## CANONICAL_ARCHETYPE_LAW

The live Coherence Diagnostic explicitly defines exactly four public archetypes:

1. **Strategist** — Structure / Sequence / Decision
2. **Builder** — Action / Construction / Visible Progress
3. **Mitigator** — Stability / Risk Reduction / Continuity
4. **Auditor** — Evidence / Contradiction / Proof

### Strategist

Short definition: organizes direction, priority, timing, and decision structure.

Pressure question: **What is the correct order, and what decision governs the rest?**

Healthy expression:
- clarifies direction;
- sets sequence;
- protects structure;
- makes decisions under pressure;
- prevents scattered execution.

Risk expression:
- over-controlling the field;
- waiting for perfect structure;
- compressing people into the plan;
- mistaking authority for coherence;
- becoming rigid when adaptation is needed.

Primary meaning: leading through order, direction, decision, and structure.

Support meaning: using planning, sequencing, and decision structure to reinforce the primary pattern.

### Builder

Short definition: turns possibility into working form through action, testing, and construction.

Pressure question: **What can we make, fix, test, or move right now?**

Healthy expression:
- creates momentum;
- converts ideas into action;
- builds proof through real attempts;
- learns from working models;
- resists stagnation.

Risk expression:
- moving before structure is ready;
- building around unresolved contradictions;
- treating motion as proof;
- starting more than can be finished;
- frustration with slow review or mitigation.

Primary meaning: leading through action, construction, testing, and visible progress.

Support meaning: using building, testing, or prototyping to reinforce the primary pattern.

### Mitigator

Short definition: reduces harm, stabilizes tension, protects continuity, and keeps the field usable.

Pressure question: **What needs to be stabilized so this does not break?**

Healthy expression:
- reduces harm;
- stabilizes conflict;
- preserves continuity;
- protects people and systems from overload;
- keeps the field usable under pressure.

Risk expression:
- delaying needed decisions;
- over-accommodating instability;
- preserving a failing structure too long;
- softening accountability;
- confusing peace with coherence.

Primary meaning: leading by stabilizing, preserving, reducing harm, and keeping the field intact.

Support meaning: using risk reduction, continuity, and regulation to reinforce the primary pattern.

### Auditor

Short definition: tests claims, detects contradictions, checks evidence, and protects truth under pressure.

Pressure question: **What is the evidence, and where is the contradiction?**

Healthy expression:
- detects contradiction;
- protects truth;
- finds weak points;
- prevents false claims;
- tests systems before trust is granted.

Risk expression:
- over-challenging;
- becoming adversarial;
- slowing action through excessive doubt;
- applying pressure without closure;
- treating flaws as the whole picture.

Primary meaning: leading by testing, verifying, challenging, and protecting evidence.

Support meaning: using proof, contradiction detection, and pressure testing to reinforce the primary pattern.

## EXISTING_IMPLEMENTATION — COHERENCE DIMENSIONS

The diagnostic measures six explicit coherence subscales:

1. **Sequence Integrity**
2. **Evidence Discipline**
3. **Action Realism**
4. **Field Stability**
5. **Correction Capacity**
6. **Contradiction Resistance**

These dimensions are distinct from archetype identity. Archetype-aligned responses carry different subscale profiles and may express stronger or weaker coherence.

The implementation therefore does not establish that selecting an archetype-associated response is automatically coherent.

## EXISTING_IMPLEMENTATION — ASSESSMENT STRUCTURE

The technical face states that the instrument contains:

- two intake anchors;
- ten randomized real-world pressure scenarios.

The intake anchors are:

1. self-rated coherence;
2. claimed primary and support patterns.

The pressure scenarios then provide observed decision evidence.

## EXISTING_IMPLEMENTATION — RESPONSE WEIGHTING

For each pressure scenario:

- first move = **×2**;
- support move = **×1**.

Both moves contribute to:

- archetype totals;
- coherence scoring;
- subscale totals;
- pressure-break calculations.

This weighting belongs to the existing Coherence Diagnostic assessment. It does **not** automatically authorize the same weights for future C3 conversation.

## EXISTING_IMPLEMENTATION — REVEALED PATTERN

The implementation accumulates a complete vector across all four public archetypes.

For each archetype it can retain:

- weighted points;
- weighted coherence;
- percentage of total response weight;
- primary-response count;
- secondary-response count.

The vector is ranked by weighted points, then weighted coherence, then name as a deterministic tie-break.

The highest supported result becomes the revealed primary pattern and the next becomes the revealed support pattern.

The diagnostic also calculates:

- dominance margin;
- dominance disposition;
- confidence band;
- primary distribution;
- secondary distribution.

Therefore mixed and near-tied patterns are already representable. The architecture does not require every person to be treated as an absolute single-type identity.

## EXISTING_IMPLEMENTATION — SELF-IDENTIFICATION VS OBSERVED BEHAVIOR

The participant claims a primary and support archetype before completing the pressure scenarios.

The diagnostic independently derives revealed primary/support patterns from scenario behavior.

It then compares claimed and revealed patterns through claim alignment.

This establishes an important existing distinction:

**self-description is evidence, but it is not identical to observed/revealed behavior.**

The diagnostic can therefore preserve disagreement between claimed identity and observed pattern instead of forcing one to overwrite the other.

## CANONICAL DISTINCTION — ARCHETYPE VS PRESSURE BREAK

The diagnostic separately recognizes fragmentation / pressure-break responses.

Explicit pressure-break classes include:

- avoidance;
- unsupported certainty;
- comfort over accountability;
- blame displacement;
- motion without proof;
- collapse under pressure.

The technical face explicitly states that these are treated as **decision signals rather than personal identities**.

They are not fifth/sixth archetypes and are not personality labels.

The implementation separately records:

- weighted fragmentation;
- primary fragmentation count;
- secondary fragmentation count;
- pressure-break rate;
- fragmentation band;
- fragmentation types;
- interpretation.

This separation must survive any later C3 integration.

## EXISTING IMPLEMENTATION — CALIBRATION

The diagnostic distinguishes:

- self-rated coherence;
- revealed scenario score;
- calibration gap;
- calibration accuracy;
- claimed archetype blend;
- revealed archetype blend;
- claim-pattern alignment.

This means the system already has precedent for comparing what a participant says about themselves against behavioral evidence without declaring either input to be a fixed personality identity.

## EXISTING IMPLEMENTATION — RESULT BOUNDARY

The public page describes itself as a **self-reflection and decision-pattern instrument**.

It explicitly states that it is not a medical, mental-health, legal, employment, intelligence, or official personality-type diagnostic.

The archetypes must therefore remain decision-pattern constructs within this instrument's stated authority.
