## 2. Contextual rules

1. **Achievement before biography.** Creator provenance may magnify scale but may not substitute for evidence or lower the standard of judgment.
2. **Scale before explanation.** The visitor should understand the magnitude of the estate before being asked to parse internal mechanisms.
3. **Claim before jargon.** Public-facing claims must remain intelligible without repository vocabulary. Technical terms belong behind OPEN and must be attached to a concrete accomplishment.
4. **Top and bottom may share objects, not editorial jobs.** The top states why the work matters. The bottom tells the chapter case and houses films.
5. **Avoid headline duplication when a chapter-specific formulation can carry the same truth.** Repetition must add a new lens, not merely restate the same sentence.
6. **Readers prove; they do not merely describe.** An opened top card must explain what was built, why the claim is true here, how it connects to the estate, and what boundary remains.
7. **Chapter readers identify the award case.** Each lower reader should state what this chapter is evaluating before presenting film/correspondence.
8. **Current state and future ambition stay separate.** No future gameplay, AI, platform, research, award, or product state may be implied as already complete.
9. **Provenance is amplification, not evidence substitution.** “Independently built” changes the scale of the accomplishment, not the standard of proof.
10. **Commercial conversion is tertiary inside the Awards narrative.** Continuation labels should favor inspection, evidence, construction method, and live work over direct sales language.
11. **Claim boundaries stay visible but subordinate to the achievement.** Transparency should strengthen credibility without becoming the emotional conclusion of a section.
12. **Integration does not mean homogenization.** Estate language must preserve distinct roles, maturity, authority, and runtime boundaries among systems.
13. **Evidence routes must return to inspectable work.** Every major claim should have a direct path to the live surface, product, Governance, or another appropriate proof route.
14. **The Award page is a significance surface.** Its local role is `BODY_OF_WORK -> SIGNIFICANCE -> TRANSPARENT_CLAIM_BOUNDARY`; it must not become the global estate directory or a generic Build landing page.
