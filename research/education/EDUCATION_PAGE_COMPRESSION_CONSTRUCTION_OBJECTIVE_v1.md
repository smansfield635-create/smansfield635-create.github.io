# EDUCATION PAGE COMPRESSION CONSTRUCTION OBJECTIVE v1

STATUS: FROZEN CONSTRUCTION OBJECTIVE
SCOPE: Education public page information order and vertical compression
BASE AUTHORITY: qualified program-wide placement + expression runtime
PRODUCT LOGIC MUTATION: PROHIBITED

## Objective

Reduce time-to-action on the Education page by eliminating duplicated priority before placement.

The required first-use path is:

HERO
→ SIX-CARD INSTRUMENT
→ YOUR STARTING POINT / PLACEMENT

A first-time learner must be able to understand the proposition, inspect the six-card system if desired, and reach placement without passing through a second explanation of the product.

## Governing method

Vertical density MUST be solved primarily through information sequencing, relocation, and removal of redundant explanation.

It MUST NOT be solved merely by shrinking typography, compressing controls, reducing touch targets, or making primary content visually insignificant.

No new decorative graphic system is authorized by this objective.

## Protected authorities

The following are frozen and must not be changed by this construction cycle:

- D1-D7 diagnostic matrix;
- Capstone matrix and admission;
- scoring and adaptive thresholds;
- four-phase Receive → Place → Use → Own mapping;
- placement / curriculum availability / access separation;
- Phase-1 availability and access rules;
- carousel drag, snap, flip/open, and interaction authority;
- EN/ES/ZH help-language evidence boundaries;
- qualified placement result semantics.

## Required read-only inventory

Before mutation, inventory every visible block from the hero through the first placement control.

For each block record:
- function;
- approximate vertical cost;
- meaning already communicated elsewhere;
- interaction/dependency constraints;
- disposition.

Each block receives exactly one disposition:

KEEP ABOVE
MOVE BELOW
MOVE INTO CARD
COMPRESS
REMOVE REDUNDANCY

## Target-order hypothesis

The audit begins from this target hypothesis:

1. Hero
2. Carousel introduction
3. Six-card instrument
4. Immediate Starting Point / placement CTA
5. Placement runtime
6. Program position and learning availability
7. Deeper explanatory material
8. Complete-program 3–6 month journey and supporting material

The read-only inventory may refine this order when physical/DOM evidence justifies it.

## Content preservation rule

Useful content is not deleted merely because it moves below placement.

Content that primarily explains one of the six card concepts should preferentially move into that card's information surface when structurally appropriate.

Complete-program explanation, support-fading explanation, evidence boundaries, 1,001-concept architecture, productive patterns, and the 3–6 month journey may remain available below the primary placement path.

Duplicated explanation may be removed when its meaning is already preserved authoritatively elsewhere.

## Physical acceptance

The successor must be qualified at phone, tablet, and desktop widths.

Qualification must establish:
- materially shorter hero-to-placement journey;
- no new clipping or overflow;
- readable neighboring carousel cards;
- preserved touch targets;
- coherent hierarchy after content relocation;
- moved material remains discoverable and understandable;
- placement remains reachable without traversing a second full product explanation.

## Functional acceptance

The cycle must prove:
- carousel drag/snap/open/flip behavior survives;
- placement starts and completes;
- D1-D7 + Capstone authority is unchanged;
- Phase-1 eligible routing survives;
- later-phase unavailable routing survives;
- transition and uncertain results survive;
- EN/ES/ZH shell behavior survives;
- persistence behavior survives.

## Learner-path acceptance

Adversarially test:
- visitor who immediately wants placement;
- visitor who explores cards first;
- visitor who changes help language;
- Phase-1 placement;
- later unavailable-phase placement;
- Capstone route.

## Construction law

After the inventory and target-order contract are frozen, execute one bounded structural mutation.

Do not stop at every successful intermediate pass. Bounded failures are repaired and rerun within the construction cycle until qualification, merge, exact-head publication, and live verification are complete, unless a material external stop condition prevents lawful continuation.
