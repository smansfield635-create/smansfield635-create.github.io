# Diamond Gate Bridge / Consider the Energy — Phase 2 Starter Template Kit v1.0

**Status:** FROZEN  
**Version:** `Starter-Template-Kit-v1.0`  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-19  
**Parent authority:** `DESIGN_FACE_FREEZE_V1.md` / `Design-Face-Freeze-v1.0`

## Purpose

This document defines the first canonical static-social template system for Diamond Gate Bridge / Consider the Energy.

All templates inherit the Phase 1 design-face law:

- dark cinematic visual language
- blue/gold emphasis
- atmospheric depth
- structured modern typography
- restrained symbolic imagery
- subtle branding
- one clear idea per frame

No template may override the parent design-face freeze.

---

# Global template law

## Default canvas

Primary social canvas:

`1080 × 1350` (4:5)

Secondary derivatives may be created from the same composition for:

- `1080 × 1080` square
- `1080 × 1920` story/reel cover

The 4:5 master is the default authority for static feed graphics.

## Safe-zone law

Keep critical text and brand marks inside:

- left/right: 84 px minimum
- top: 96 px minimum
- bottom: 108 px minimum

No hero line may touch the frame edge.

## Grid law

Use a 12-column conceptual grid.

Default alignment options:

- centered axis
- left-biased editorial axis
- symmetric split only when the concept itself requires polarity

## Spacing law

Use generous vertical breathing room.

Minimum spacing between:

- hero and support: 36 px
- support and CTA/brand zone: 52 px
- distinct conceptual elements: 40 px

## Type hierarchy

### Hero
- dominant
- short
- high contrast
- maximum 2 lines preferred
- maximum 4 lines absolute

### Support
- one short sentence or phrase
- maximum 2 lines preferred

### Micro label / brand
- small
- consistent
- readable
- never visually dominant

## Brand zone default

Default footer treatment:

`DiamondGateBridge.com`

Alternative identifiers may be used only when the post family requires them:

- Consider the Energy
- Think & Blink
- Chapter One
- Find Your Way

## CTA law

Maximum one CTA per frame.

## Background law

Every template must use one of the three Phase 1 background families:

A. Atmospheric Dark Field  
B. Fuzzy Cinematic Field  
C. Symbolic Concept Field

# Template 01 — Statement Card

**Template ID:** `DGB-T01-STATEMENT`

## Purpose

Deliver one short, high-authority statement with maximum clarity and minimal visual noise.

## Default use cases

- philosophy lines
- brand principles
- campaign phrases
- short thought leadership
- baseline language
- Find Your Way language

## Preferred background

Primary:
`Family A — Atmospheric Dark Field`

Secondary:
`Family B — Fuzzy Cinematic Field`

## Composition

### Focus zone
Hero statement centered or slightly above center.

### Support zone
Optional one-line reinforcement below hero.

### Brand zone
Small `DiamondGateBridge.com` footer.

## Visual motif

Optional one restrained motif only:

- glow point
- thin horizon
- orb
- faint field line
- small polarity pair

## Copy constraints

Hero:
- 2–8 words preferred
- 12 words maximum

Support:
- 16 words maximum

CTA:
- optional
- one only

## Canonical examples

- FIND YOUR WAY
- BASELINE MATTERS
- BRING IT BACK TO BASELINE
- CONSIDER THE ENERGY

## Fail conditions

- more than one dominant statement
- busy illustration behind the copy
- multiple CTAs
- meme-like layout
- oversized logo competing with message

# Template 02 — Concept Graphic

**Template ID:** `DGB-T02-CONCEPT`

## Purpose

Visualize one abstract or philosophical idea through a restrained symbolic composition.

## Default use cases

- fear / love
- polarity
- symmetry
- restoration / degradation
- signal / noise
- relationships / boundaries
- words as tools / indicators / distractions
- coherence / baseline

## Preferred background

Primary:
`Family C — Symbolic Concept Field`

Secondary:
`Family A — Atmospheric Dark Field`

## Composition

### Focus zone
Symbolic visual structure carries the main concept.

### Support zone
One short phrase names or sharpens the concept.

### Brand zone
Small site or series mark.

## Symbolic vocabulary

Approved:

- opposing orbs
- radial symmetry
- network nodes
- field lines
- branching geometry
- horizon intersections
- central axis
- paired glow points
- contained circular systems

## Text constraints

Hero phrase:
- optional
- 1–5 words

Support:
- one sentence maximum

No explanatory paragraph.

## Motion-language rule

Even though static, the graphic should imply motion, field, relation, or system behavior through composition.

## Fail conditions

- infographic density
- literal clip art
- generic science wallpaper
- random decorative geometry
- five concepts in one frame
- explanation longer than the visual idea

# Template 03 — Website / Pillar Promo

**Template ID:** `DGB-T03-PILLAR`

## Purpose

Route the viewer to exactly one destination inside Diamond Gate Bridge.

## Default destinations

- Compass
- Mirrorland
- The House
- Chapter One
- Find Your Way
- Products
- Showroom
- Gauges

## Preferred background

Primary:
`Family B — Fuzzy Cinematic Field`

Secondary:
`Family A — Atmospheric Dark Field`

## Composition

### Focus zone
Destination title.

### Support zone
One short orientation sentence.

### CTA zone
One action.

### Brand zone
`DiamondGateBridge.com`

## Copy structure

Line 1:
Destination name

Line 2:
One sentence explaining why to enter

Line 3:
One CTA

## Approved CTA patterns

- Find your way.
- Enter the Compass.
- Explore Mirrorland.
- Start at Chapter One.
- Explore DiamondGateBridge.com

## Image behavior

Use a source-native visual from the destination when available.

If using a destination screenshot:
- blur/fuzzy-fill may support depth
- do not display browser chrome unless explicitly intended
- focus on the world/experience, not interface clutter

## Fail conditions

- multiple destinations in one graphic
- more than one CTA
- sales copy
- feature-list overload
- screenshot clutter
- oversized URL dominating the composition

# Template 04 — Chapter / Announcement

**Template ID:** `DGB-T04-ANNOUNCEMENT`

## Purpose

Create an event-like visual for a release, chapter, premiere, launch, or scheduled drop.

## Preferred background

Primary:
`Family A — Atmospheric Dark Field`

Secondary:
`Family B — Fuzzy Cinematic Field`

## Composition

### Top micro label
Series / category.

Examples:
- DIAMOND GATE BRIDGE
- THINK & BLINK
- CHAPTER SERIES

### Focus zone
Main title.

### Support zone
Release descriptor or date/time.

### Brand zone
Website or campaign mark.

## Typography behavior

Strongest hierarchy of all static templates.

Title may be larger than normal statement cards.

## Date/time treatment

If needed:
- one date
- one time
- one timezone only
- never dominate the title

## CTA

Optional:
- Watch tomorrow.
- Start at Chapter One.
- Find your way.

## Fail conditions

- flyer-like clutter
- multiple dates
- dense event details
- decorative badge overload
- more than one headline

# Template 05 — Video Cover / Social Thumbnail

**Template ID:** `DGB-T05-COVER`

## Purpose

Create a recognizable cover system for video posts while maintaining family resemblance across the feed.

## Default use cases

- Think & Blink
- Diamond Gate cinematic
- Chapter series
- website walkthroughs
- product/showroom clips

## Preferred background

Primary:
`Family B — Fuzzy Cinematic Field`

Secondary:
source still with restrained dark treatment.

## Composition

### Focus zone
One high-value subject or frame.

### Hero hook
Short title or concept.

### Series label
Small and consistent.

### Brand zone
Optional website mark.

## Subject law

Use one dominant subject:

- human face
- portal
- globe
- Compass
- symbolic graphic
- environmental scene

Do not collage several competing stills.

## Copy constraints

Hero:
- 2–6 words preferred
- 8 words maximum

Series label:
- 1 line

## Cover-frame law

Covers must remain legible at small mobile thumbnail size.

## Fail conditions

- tiny text
- multiple frames
- busy collage
- paragraph copy
- large CTA
- generic YouTube-style clickbait treatment

- shared palette
- shared type hierarchy
- shared footer/brand logic
- shared spacing discipline
- shared atmospheric treatment
- shared symbolic vocabulary

# Template qualification checklist

A template instance passes only if:

- parent Phase 1 law is satisfied
- one main idea is visually dominant
- type hierarchy is clear
- background family is valid
- brand zone is controlled
- CTA count <= 1
- mobile readability is preserved
- edge safe zones are respected
- no clutter is introduced
- no off-brand color family appears
