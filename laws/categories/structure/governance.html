Reading layers

No reading is open on entry. Each reading remains available without being treated as interchangeable.

Practical reading

Show the people or institutions responsible for safe, lawful, and reviewable operation.

Engineering reading

Expose permissions, separation of duties, admission gates, mutation authority, escalation, shutdown, and rollback.

Empirical reading

Show evidence of approvals, executed actions, violations, shutdown tests, and missing governance records.

Authority

Shared premise

Authority is not volume, preference, or proximity. It is the recognized right to decide, preserve, enforce, and answer for a rule.

  1. Decision right

    Story focus. This selection isolates decision right within Authority. It shows which decision changes at this layer and prevents the card-level premise from standing in for the unresolved detail.

    Practical

    Lens frame. Authority is not volume, preference, or proximity. It is the recognized right to decide, preserve, enforce, and answer for a rule.

    What this selection adds. Name who owns the rule. This selection isolates decision right within Authority. It shows which decision changes at this layer and prevents the card-level premise from standing in for the unresolved detail.

    Engineering

    Lens frame. In engineering terms, authority maps to ownership records, permission models, administrative roles, maintainers, approval rights, policy owners, and source-of-truth custody.

    What this selection adds. Separate read access from change authority. Treat decision right as a distinct control point inside Authority. Give it an owner, explicit state, transition rule, and failure output so the implementation cannot hide it inside a general label.

    Empirical

    Lens frame. For authority requires more than a title. It requires records showing that decision rights, enforcement rights, and responsibility are recognized and used.

    What this selection adds. Strong evidence: approval records, ownership registry, change authority logs, enforcement history. Evidence for decision right must identify the observed unit, context, comparison, and boundary. Without those records, Authority remains a conceptual or procedural classification at this layer. Require records showing how Authority behaved at ordinary load and at its boundary: crossings, rejected inputs, exceptions, enforcement, and resulting state.
