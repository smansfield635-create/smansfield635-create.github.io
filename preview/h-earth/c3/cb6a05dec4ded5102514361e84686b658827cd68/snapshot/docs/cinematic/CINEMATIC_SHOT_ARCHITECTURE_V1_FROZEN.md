## Architecture principle

**DERIVE THE FEWEST, STRONGEST SHOTS THAT MAKE D01–D08 FEEL LIKE ONE INEVITABLE 42-SECOND EXPERIENCE.**

The directing ledger may be complex. The viewed film should feel simple, causal, and inevitable.

This freeze establishes **five primary shot families**. They are not five mandatory hard cuts. A family is the independently governed cinematic movement that owns one causal spatial/narrative regime.

Default rule:
- one continuous shot per family is preferred;
- a seamless transition may connect families without a hard cut;
- an additional internal cut is prohibited unless later cinematography proves that semantic clarity, spatial comprehension, musical structure, or physical continuity cannot be preserved without it;
- adding a shot requires an explicit architecture amendment, not camera convenience.

Measured score events are pressure / release / emphasis evidence. They are not automatic cut points.

## Global shot-economy law

Before adding or preserving any adjacent shot boundary, apply this deletion test:

> If two adjacent proposed shots can be merged without losing semantic clarity, musical structure, spatial comprehension, or emotional escalation, merge them.

No shot exists merely to demonstrate a directing beat, a camera capability, a score accent, or a control-plane state.

## SH01 — WORLD / QUESTION

Directing contracts served:
- D01 — ESTABLISH / ANTICIPATION
- D02 — LINE 01 / QUESTION

Approximate master-audio domain:
- opening through the Line 01 release, approximately `0–8s`;
- exact entry / release frames remain subordinate to the frozen master-audio phrase map.

Canonical language event:
- `What makes something real?`

Shot purpose:
- `ESTABLISH → REVEAL`

Starting audience state:
- un-oriented but attentive; the world has not yet declared its proposition.

Ending audience state:
- oriented enough to receive a genuine question; the inquiry has become part of the world rather than an overlay.

World / art-direction state:
- restrained information density;
- strong negative-space discipline;
- enough world depth to establish physical orientation without beginning the principal ascent;
- light, atmosphere, material hierarchy, and environmental detail remain subordinate to anticipation rather than spectacle.

Editorial-space role:
- world establishes before language;
- Line 01 enters around the intended musical arrival near four seconds;
- language has spatial presence while remaining immediately readable;
- controlled depth / parallax / focus relationship may support integration;
- no conventional caption behavior;
- the line releases causally into the next proposition.

Camera purpose:
- establish viewpoint, depth, horizon / spatial orientation, and enough parallax potential for language to feel integrated;
- camera activity must not compete with the first question or determine its timing.

Why this is one family:
- anticipation and question are one causal act: the world is first experienced, then interrogated.

Transition purpose:
- the question opens possibility; the film moves from orientation into a claim about what may exist.

Prohibitions:
- no decorative opening movement;
- no inherited old opener merely because it existed;
- no subtitle-style Line 01;
- no premature spectacle.

## SH03 — EMBODIED ASCENT

Directing contracts served:
- D04 — EXPERIENCE / ASCENT LAUNCH
- D05 — ASCENT PROOF / COMPRESSION

Principal master-audio domain:
- ascent launch near `16.064000s` through summit approach around `32.053333s` / release entry around `32.896000s`.

Canonical language event:
- `The experience creates reality.`

Shot purpose:
- `TRAVERSE → PROVE`

Starting audience state:
- the world is plausible, but the proposition has not yet been physically demonstrated.

Ending audience state:
- ascent has been experienced as evidence; summit inevitability and larger-scale consequence are physically legible.

World / art-direction state:
- one continuous causal environmental rise;
- geographic information must progress legibly through `GROUND → HILL → MOUNTAIN → SUMMIT`;
- foreground depth, terrain scale, atmospheric perspective, environmental density, light, and material character must evolve so that increasing elevation and spatial consequence are visible rather than merely asserted;
- exact palette, lighting recipe, material treatment, and production-design implementation are deferred to the cinematography / art-direction specification.

Editorial-space role:
- T03 participates in the ascent field rather than floating above it;
- language density deliberately diminishes as picture assumes the burden of proof;
- editorial presence must never fragment the ascent into four explanatory cards.

Camera purpose:
- make increasing elevation, terrain depth, scale, and summit inevitability physically legible;
- preserve one causal spatial axis of advancement;
- movement exists to reveal new spatial information, not to exhibit camera capability.

Frozen internal-score treatment:
- `16.064000s`, `19.914667s`, `24.213333s`, `28.149333s`, and `32.053333s` shape escalation;
- none is an automatic cut point;
- D04 and D05 remain one shot family and one causal directing movement.

Why this is one family:
- launch and proof are not separate narrative ideas. The ascent is the proof.

Transition purpose:
- accumulated physical evidence reaches a threshold where the summit can become a question about larger reality rather than a terminal landscape image.

Prohibitions:
- no four generic four-second ascent clips;
- no planetary-horizon substitute inside the terrestrial rise;
- no independent D04 / D05 montage branches;
- no cut added solely because an intermediate score event exists;
- no technically spectacular move without semantic gain.

Camera purpose:
- convert local summit evidence into larger-world comprehension while preserving spatial identity;

Why this is one family:

## Art-direction requirement

Art direction is now a first-class downstream specification, equal in creative importance to camera direction.

For every shot family, the cinematography / art-direction pass must explicitly bind:
- light progression;
- atmosphere;
- environmental density;
- foreground / midground / background hierarchy;
- horizon / scale relationship;
- material character;
- color progression;
- negative-space behavior;
- continuity into the next family.

This architecture intentionally does not invent those exact values.

## Editorial-space proof requirement

Before propagating spatial typography through the whole film, physically prove it inside the actual master-audio timeline using:

1. Line 01 in SH01;
2. the exact `WASN'T → IS` sentence event at `10.000000s` in SH02.

These are not detached motion-graphics demos. They must be judged inside the intended cinematic compositions with the exact corresponding master audio.
