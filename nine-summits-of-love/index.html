The Nine Summits of Love · 256 Carats of Human Potential

What if the heart is not broken?

What if it is simply under pressure?

We often talk about love as though it is something we are supposed to find: the right person, the right moment, the right answer.

But what if love is not merely something we find? What if love is something we must become capable of carrying?

Pressure does not always destroy what is valuable. Sometimes pressure reveals it. A diamond does not emerge by avoiding pressure. Neither does character. Neither does trust. Neither does love.

The heart is not empty. Its value is still present—even when it is locked, guarded, exhausted, or afraid.
