Flow Track 04

Handoffs

Handoffs govern transfer between actors, modules, rooms, roles, domains, states, or stages. They ask whether meaning, timing, responsibility, state, and context survive the boundary.

Platform

Preserve continuity

A good handoff gives the next holder enough intact context to continue without distortion.

  • What is being transferred?
  • Who gives it and who receives it?
  • Does responsibility survive the transfer?
Engineering

Pass state without loss

Handoffs map to custody transfer, interface transition, state passing, queue movement, ownership change, protocol exchange, and context preservation.

  • Declare sender and receiver.
  • Preserve state and context.
  • Make acceptance and ownership transitions observable.
Evidence

Prove transfer and continuation

Evidence requires proof that transfer occurred and the receiving side could continue with the required context intact.

  • Strong: transfer receipts, custody records, sender/receiver traces, accepted state.
  • Useful: queue logs, handoff notes, transition histories, continuity checks.
Boundary

Sending is not the same as successful transfer.

A handoff is incomplete when the receiver cannot identify, accept, or continue the required state. This page does not claim scientific validation, production validation, or universal proof.
