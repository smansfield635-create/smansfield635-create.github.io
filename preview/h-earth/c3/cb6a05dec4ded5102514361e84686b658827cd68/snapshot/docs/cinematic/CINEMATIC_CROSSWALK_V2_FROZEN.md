## Film definition

**A 42-second visual interpretation of a frozen melodic ascendance, with story and cinematography synchronized to the music and executed deterministically.**

## Crosswalk law

Every relevant inherited cinematic idea must receive an explicit disposition before present-tense construction.

Legal dispositions:

- `PRESERVED` — the inherited idea remains authoritative.
- `SUPERSEDED_BY` — the underlying purpose survives, but a named successor interpretation replaces the prior implementation.
- `REMOVED_BY_LATER_AUTHORITY` — later authority explicitly eliminates the inherited idea.

No silent inheritance. No silent replacement.

Any unresolved predecessor relation is an authority gap, not permission to carry the old implementation forward.

## Preserved creative grammar

Subject to beat-level crosswalk disposition, the following predecessor ideas remain eligible for preservation:

- causal progression rather than decorative montage;
- motivated camera movement;
- spatial and cognitive orientation;
- `READ → LOOK` attention rhythm where it still serves the script;
- shot-purpose grammar such as `ESTABLISH`, `REVEAL`, `TRAVERSE`, `INSPECT`, `CONNECT`, `PROVE`, `RETURN`;
- language integrated with image rather than treated as conventional caption overlay;
- spatial / 3D editorial expression;
- strong semantic sequencing;
- causal transitions.

Preserving grammar never means preserving rejected footage or obsolete timing.

## Explicit supersession / non-inheritance

The following are not inherited merely because they existed in an older master:

- rejected source selections;
- arbitrary four-second visual blocks;
- planetary-horizon ascent;
- rejected V2/V3 footage;
- timing that conflicts with the 42-second master audio;
- conventional caption treatment;
- predecessor camera moves whose only justification is historical presence;
- any implementation carried forward without explicit crosswalk disposition.

## Shot-family doctrine

The fundamental replaceable production unit is the independently sealed shot family.

Each shot receives:

- shot identity;
- narrative purpose;
- audience attention target;
- subject hierarchy / dominance;
- composition and horizon placement;
- lens / FOV behavior;
- camera position / height;
- motion type;
- velocity / acceleration profile;
- parallax / occlusion behavior;
- reveal / withhold structure;
- beginning state;
- ending state;
- intended cut / transition;
- approximate duration derived from script + score;
- emotional / experiential intention;
- deterministic state range;
- qualification evidence;
- frozen render range;
- editorial boundary.

Optimization order:

`MEANING FIRST → COMPOSITION SECOND → MOVEMENT THIRD → DETERMINISTIC EXECUTION FOURTH`

The deterministic camera system is an execution/compiler layer beneath cinematography. It does not invent cinematography and may not determine duration because a trajectory is technically convenient.

## Beat / shot separation

Story/timing regions are not mechanically one-shot-per-region.

- one shot may span multiple beat regions;
- one beat region may contain multiple shots;
- a cut may occur inside a beat;
- a shot may continue across a beat boundary.

Shot count and cut structure emerge from the score and story.

## Editorial-space doctrine

Script language is cinematic material, not a subtitle layer.

The editorial system may use controlled depth, perspective, parallax, focus behavior, camera relationship, environmental integration, and bounded occlusion while preserving immediate readability.

Three spatial systems remain distinct:

- `WORLD SPACE` — H-Earth, Audralia, terrain, architecture, objects;
- `CAMERA SPACE` — position, lens, motion, framing, perspective;
- `EDITORIAL SPACE` — script typography, titles, semantic transitions, graphic expression.

Editorial-space timing is governed by script + audio, not by camera-move completion.

### Opening / Line 01 creative requirement

- approximately `0–4s`: visual establishment / anticipation;
- approximately `4s`: canonical Script Line 01 enters in synchronization with the intended musical arrival;
- approximately `4–8s`: integrated presence for roughly four seconds;
- approximately `8s`: controlled fade / release into the next visual state.

Exact frame binding remains subject to the immutable master-audio phrase map.

## Physical-proof doctrine

Silent proofs are permitted only for narrow spatial diagnostics such as composition, subject readability, geometry, summit recognition, occlusion, horizon placement, lighting, or render integrity.

Any proof used to judge motion, pacing, transition, reveal timing, emotional escalation, cut rhythm, or editorial quality must carry the exact corresponding interval of the immutable 42-second master audio.

Only the full 42-second AV review may qualify the film as an integrated editorial object.

Physical qualification remains deliberately non-creative: it verifies that the realized shot preserves approved geometry, framing, depth, continuity, occlusion, reveal, and endpoint behavior. Creative merit must already have been decided in the cinematography specification.
