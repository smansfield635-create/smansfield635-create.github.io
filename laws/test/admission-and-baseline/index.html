Test readings

Purpose, inputs, procedure, challenge, and record remain distinct.

Purpose

Fix what the test is allowed to judge and why the baseline matters.

Inputs

Declare inclusion, exclusion, split logic, outcome definition, baseline, comparators, and leakage controls.

Procedure

Cases pass through explicit gates; rejected inputs remain outside and visible.

Challenge

Combined AUROC 0.9394 did not exceed the burden comparator at 0.9704.

Record

Case admission, outcome, and baseline may not move after results are known.

Derivative admission method

  1. Require externally observable records sufficient to identify constraints, outcomes, and corrective actions.
  2. Exclude cases relying primarily on subjective accounts or inferred intent.
  3. Fix the temporal or event evidence window before analysis.
  4. Evaluate multiple periods separately; do not mix or retroactively adjust windows.

Output

ADMITTED_CASE_PACKET or NO_ADMISSION. Threshold categories remain distinct rather than silently collapsed.
