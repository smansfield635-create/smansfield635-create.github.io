What the battery result establishes—and what it does not.

The test used 1,653 cell-disjoint held-out evaluation cycles from three batteries excluded from development. The target was crossing below 80% retained capacity within 20 cycles. This design supports transfer-to-unseen-cell inference, not universal-law inference.

Claim-testing ladder

Held-out association: supported. Axis AUROC 0.9394.
Transfer to unseen batteries: supported within CS2_34, CS2_36, and CS2_38.
Confound independence: unresolved. Burden AUROC 0.9704 may encode conventional aging exposure.
Mechanistic specificity: unresolved pending cycle, throughput, capacity, slope, and ablation controls.
H* incremental superiority: not supported. Raw + H* AUROC 0.7770 versus raw 0.7850.
Hard MQ threshold: not supported. Sensitivity 0%; balanced accuracy 0.4994.
Universal law: not established.

Scientific meaning

The axis representation is empirically promising because it ranked impending degradation strongly on unseen cells. The strongest rival explanation is that burden re-expresses cycle age or accumulated use. The next decisive test is whether the axes retain independent predictive value after conventional degradation controls.
