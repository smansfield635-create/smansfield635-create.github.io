# Mirrorland Character Interaction Law — Immersive Relational Conversation

Status: **CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE / CROSS-ROOM RECOVERY AUTHORITY**
Date: 2026-10-02

## Purpose

This record preserves the governing interaction model for Mirrorland character experiences so future rooms, agents, or implementation sessions do not have to reconstruct owner intent from conversation history.

Auren is the first proving ground. The law is Mirrorland-wide unless a later explicit architecture record supersedes a clause.

This record does not itself mutate a character runtime.

## 1. Core experience law

Visitors arrive in Mirrorland to **engage with characters and experience part of the world**.

They do not arrive to:
- complete a personality test;
- interrogate a character biography;
- navigate an FAQ disguised as dialogue;
- collect an evaluation at the end;
- receive a diagnostic label;
- optimize a visible trust score.

During natural interaction, characters inevitably learn about the visitor just as people learn about one another through conversation and shared experience.

That learning changes the ongoing interaction.

**The journey itself is the output.**

There is no required terminal assessment, ticket, scorecard, archetype reveal, or “what this character learned about you” report.

## 2. Invisible diagnostics law

Underlying diagnostic systems are character cognition/mechanics, not conversational presentation.

Examples include:
- relationship state;
- archetype evidence;
- behavioral observations;
- session interaction history;
- contextual/world state;
- disposition-specific interpretation;
- authority/privacy boundaries.

These systems may influence what a character notices, considers, remembers, tests, avoids, offers, withholds, revisits, or does next.

They must not dictate the surface delivery in a mechanical or diagnostic voice.

**Diagnostics guide cognition and mechanics. Character + context guide delivery.**

The visitor should experience the character, not the machinery.

A visitor may realize that a character is studying them, but in the way one realizes another intelligent person is learning about them: memory, changed behavior, perceptive follow-up, disagreement, anticipation, caution, trust, curiosity, humor, refusal, invitation, or consequence.

## 3. No disguised assessment

Do not manufacture conversation primarily to collect diagnostic evidence.

If a line exists mainly because the system needs another data point, it is presumptively wrong.

Do not:
- march the visitor through archetype questions;
- force visitor beliefs/personality declarations into option text;
- make choices evenly distributed diagnostic buckets merely for scoring;
- announce hidden archetype evidence;
- expose internal classification language as conversation;
- turn character interaction into teacher/interviewer interrogation.

Evidence should arise naturally from what the visitor chooses to pursue, notice, challenge, respect, avoid, reveal, leave alone, or do within the situation.

## 4. Character disposition law

Each character is anchored by their own disposition/canon.

That disposition affects what the character naturally notices and cares about.

For Auren, Sanctuary Builder / Custody / protection-versus-control makes responsibility, boundaries, stewardship, admission, protection, control and trust naturally salient.

The character's disposition is a lens on the interaction, not a questionnaire topic.

Different characters may respond differently to the same visitor because they perceive different things as important.

No character needs to summarize that interpretation for the visitor.

## 5. Experience before exposition

Character identity should primarily be **experienced**, not explained.

Do not rely on visitor prompts such as:
- “What do you actually do?”
- “What does that mean?”
- “How do you decide?”
- “Who else is here?”
- “Tell me about your room?”

when their primary purpose is extracting dossier exposition.

A character should reveal themselves through:
- what is happening in the room/world;
- what they notice;
- what they ask;
- what they refuse;
- what they permit;
- what they protect;
- what they joke about;
- what frustrates them;
- what they volunteer;
- what decisions they make;
- what consequences follow.

Exposition is allowed when it is natural to the moment. The anti-pattern is making exposition the interaction model.

## 6. Visitor agency / option law

The character owns the character's words.

The visitor retains ownership of theirs.

User-facing options must not force elaborate beliefs, emotions, morality, identity or personality declarations into the visitor's mouth merely to feed diagnostics.

Options should represent plausible conversational/action intent in context.

Not every turn requires three options.

One, two or three choices may be appropriate under the existing maximum-three law.

The character may initiate, redirect, challenge, ask, interrupt, decline, change subject, revisit something, or move the situation forward. The visitor must not always be the interrogator.

## 7. Continuous relational adaptation

Relationship development is continuous, not an end-state evaluation.

A meaningful interaction changes what happens next.

Characters may become more or less:
- comfortable;
- guarded;
- candid;
- curious;
- skeptical;
- playful;
- protective;
- challenging;
- willing to ask for help;
- willing to offer an opportunity;
- willing to revisit a subject.

These are experiential consequences. They need not be named to the visitor.

A relationship engine may use discrete internal state, but the surface experience should be expressed through character behavior.

## 8. Visible diagnostic leakage

Internal labels such as archetype classifications and relationship phases are not inherently user-facing.

A visible diagnostic indicator must be separately justified by the immersive experience; engineering visibility alone is insufficient.

Where an internal state can be communicated naturally through character behavior, behavior is preferred.

Auren's recently added PUBLIC / ENGAGED / GUARDED relationship indicator is therefore subject to successor review under this law. The underlying relationship state remains useful regardless of whether its label remains visible.

## 9. Session conversational continuity

Characters require bounded working conversational context within the active experience.

The runtime should be able to distinguish at minimum:
- topics already discussed;
- information already volunteered;
- questions already answered;
- significant visitor actions/choices;
- boundaries already established;
- unresolved conversational threads;
- offers already made;
- relevant handoffs initiated;
- relevant returns from handoffs.

This is working context, not a permanent transcript-memory requirement.

The purpose is continuity: what has already happened must affect what naturally happens next.

## 10. Repetition law

Human repetition is allowed. Mechanical replay is not.

A character may intentionally repeat an important principle, concern, phrase or story where context warrants it.

But:

**Once an authored response has been delivered, revisiting the same subject within the same session must not mechanically replay the same complete response merely because the same node was reached.**

A revisit should normally:
- acknowledge prior discussion;
- deepen;
- clarify;
- connect to intervening events;
- challenge the premise;
- redirect;
- resume an unresolved thread;
- or intentionally repeat for an authored reason.

Exact same-response replay one turn later is a failure of conversational depth unless explicitly intentional.

## 11. Conversation progression law

Progression is not equivalent to increasing a score.

Progression means the relationship/situation has accumulated enough context that later interaction is materially conditioned by earlier interaction.

A visitor should not be able to reach every meaningful conversational state by randomly clicking independent FAQ branches during the first moments of the experience.

Earlier interaction may affect:
- what the character brings up;
- what they ask;
- what they volunteer;
- what they challenge;
- what they remember;
- what opportunities they offer;
- what boundaries they enforce;
- which unresolved thread they return to;
- which handoff becomes relevant.

## 12. Handoff law

Handoffs are part of conversation continuity, not merely navigation exits.

### Character -> product/external surface

The character establishes a natural conversational reason for the destination.

If the visitor returns through an authorized return path, the character may recognize that they sent the visitor there and continue from that context.

The character must not pretend to know private actions that occurred outside their authority.

### Character -> character

The handoff establishes why another character is relevant.

Authority does not transfer. The originating character does not gain the destination character's private conversation merely because a handoff occurred.

Authorized return context may acknowledge the journey without fabricating knowledge.

### Internal conversational handoff

Topics may yield naturally to other topics/situations without forcing a return to a menu.

Conversation may pivot, resume, deepen, defer, drop, or revisit.

“Back” is a navigation primitive, not the default model for human conversation.

## 13. Character evolution loop

Preferred experience loop:

**encounter -> interaction -> character observation -> changed context/relationship -> changed character behavior -> new interaction**

Not:

**question -> answer -> score -> next diagnostic question**

And not:

**menu -> information node -> Back -> menu**

The underlying diagnostic machinery may operate during the preferred loop, but must remain subordinate to the lived interaction.

## 14. Auren-specific successor direction

Auren's current tempo, typing cadence and short-turn rhythm are useful and should be preserved absent a proven defect.

The primary weakness is the conversation model:
- interview/FAQ framing;
- menu-like options;
- repeated answers;
- shallow revisits;
- insufficient character initiation;
- insufficient visible consequence from accumulated state;
- diagnostic machinery that currently produces too little experiential difference.

The successor should preserve proven state machinery while reconstructing **Learn About Auren** as an in-world character experience rather than an information interview.

Products may remain an explicit informational mode because the visitor deliberately chose Products, but product handoffs should still obey continuity law.

## 15. Architecture separation

Keep these layers distinct:

**Diagnostic/cognition layer**
- relationship state
- archetype evidence
- observations
- bounded session context
- world/context state
- disposition interpretation

**Character layer**
- canonical identity
- motives/disposition
- authority/privacy
- selection of natural next action

**Experience layer**
- dialogue
- behavior
- choices/actions
- situations
- consequences
- handoffs

Diagnostic internals must not leak upward merely because they are convenient to render.

## 16. Implementation test

For any proposed character line or visitor option, ask:

1. Why would this character say/do this **right now**?
2. Does it follow from the current situation and prior interaction?
3. Would it still exist if the diagnostic engine did not need another data point?
4. Does it preserve visitor ownership of the visitor's beliefs/identity?
5. If this topic already occurred, does the new turn recognize that history?
6. Does a handoff preserve conversational context and authority boundaries?
7. Is the visitor experiencing a character/world, or operating a menu?

If the answer exposes diagnostic or navigation machinery as the primary reason for the line, redesign it.

## 17. Cross-room recovery instruction

Future rooms working on Mirrorland character interaction should read this record before changing dialogue architecture, relationship presentation, archetype presentation, conversation memory, handoffs, or character-choice mechanics.

Do not require the owner to re-explain:
- invisible diagnostics;
- continuous relational adaptation;
- no terminal evaluation;
- natural evidence gathering;
- visitor agency;
- conversational continuity;
- anti-replay behavior;
- context-preserving handoffs;
- character-driven delivery.

For Auren:

1. audit the existing Learn About Auren nodes against this law;
2. classify each node/option as KEEP / RECONTEXTUALIZE / REWRITE / REMOVE;
3. identify the minimum bounded session-context model needed to prevent mechanical replay and preserve unresolved threads/handoffs;

Do not begin by adding more diagnostics.
