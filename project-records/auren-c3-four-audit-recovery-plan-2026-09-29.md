# Auren C3 — Four-Audit Durable Recovery and Provisional Successor Plan

Status: **AUDIT_FIRST / NO C3 PRODUCT MUTATION AUTHORIZED**

Date: 2026-09-29

## Governing purpose

Before any C3 implementation, recover the canonical character, archetype, conversation/game, and scene/handoff evidence already present in the repository and related durable project records.

The four audits below are the controlling next operations. They are read-only. They must be completed before a C3 implementation contract is frozen.

C2 remains the accepted published conversational-performance baseline. Its working cadence/runtime is not reopened by this record.

E1 room/environment reconstruction remains separate.

## STEP 1 — AUREN CANON AUDIT

**Priority: FIRST / REQUIRED**

Recover and distinguish hard canon, historical precursor material, superseded material, and later inference concerning Auren.

Required subjects:

- exact canonical age, if established;
- Richie / Richie Rich precursor lineage and transition to Auren Vale;
- evidence for youth and exceptional intelligence;
- arrogant / entitled / privileged public persona;
- whether and how that persona functions as deliberate cover or protective misdirection;
- polished-host behavior and private/deeper behavior;
- Mirror Manor / manor relationship;
- inherited wealth/resources and their intended use;
- protective responsibilities, admission choices, secrecy, cover stories and overprotection;
- relationships with other established characters;
- what Auren canonically knows;
- what Auren may legitimately disclose publicly;
- what is private/protected or belongs to another character/product authority;
- evidence for humor, impatience, confidence, vulnerability, preferences, fears, frustrations and other actual personality behavior.

### Required disposition

Every recovered proposition must be classified as one of:

- HARD_CANON
- SUPPORTED_CHARACTER_EVIDENCE
- HISTORICAL_PRECURSOR
- SUPERSEDED
- CURRENT_CONVERSATION_HYPOTHESIS_NOT_YET_CANON

Do not manufacture the missing age or other biography.

**Output:** durable Auren canon evidence map suitable for C3 dialogue authority.

**Priority: SECOND / REQUIRED**

Recover the exact four archetypes and their existing canonical/game meaning before assigning any conversational choice weights.

Required subjects:

- canonical names of all four archetypes;
- definitions and distinctions;
- existing behavioral signals or examples;
- any scoring, classification, weighting or confidence mechanics;
- whether mixed/near-tied archetypes are already contemplated;
- relationship to Mirror Me / Mirror Me's;

- prior UI/presentation of archetype information;
- any existing rules concerning self-identification versus observed behavior;
- any evidence governing whether archetypes are fixed identity, tendencies, situational modes, or something else.

### Required disposition

Separate:

- CANONICAL_ARCHETYPE_LAW
- EXISTING_IMPLEMENTATION
- EXPERIMENTAL/PREVIOUS IDEA
- NEW C3 POSSIBILITY

No C3 archetype scoring is authorized until this audit returns.

**Output:** durable four-archetype evidence and mechanics map.

# Completion gate for Steps 1–4

The four audits are cumulative and controlling.

No C3 product implementation begins merely because one audit produces useful evidence.

After all four are complete:

1. crosswalk their findings;
2. identify contradictions and unresolved canon;
3. identify reusable mechanisms;
4. identify genuinely new development;
5. re-solidify the successor plan below;
6. only then freeze a C3 implementation contract.

If the audits contradict any provisional idea below, the audit evidence controls.

# PROVISIONAL POST-AUDIT SUCCESSOR PLAN

**Status: PROVISIONAL — MUST BE RE-SOLIDIFIED AFTER STEPS 1–4**

This section preserves the design direction discovered before the audits so it is not lost. It is not implementation authority.

## Product utility layer

Opening target:

- Learn About Products
- Talk About Me

Product path should expose all five legitimate Auren product branches immediately rather than hiding three behind More.

Each product branch should provide useful conversational depth and a real handoff where an existing destination is proven.

Ordinary informational visitors must remain able to use Auren as a site/product navigation hub without playing the relationship game.

## Conversational relationship game

The character conversation is intended to become a relationship-based game/practice environment.

Conversational actions are transactions. A visitor may:

- listen;
- follow up;
- retreat;
- challenge;
- pry;
- disclose;
- respect or violate a boundary;
- manipulate;
- behave selfishly or selflessly;
- maintain or contradict an apparent pattern.

Those actions may change the relationship.

The system should allow the visitor to experience consequences rather than label choices as morally correct or incorrect.

## Trust

Trust is provisional as a persistent relationship state.

Trust should affect Auren before it affects menus:

- low trust: heavier public/arrogant/privileged persona and useful surface information;
- developing trust: greater conversational depth and interpretation;
- higher trust: more personal perspective, reduced reliance on the public mask, deeper voluntary disclosure;
- sufficiently developed trust: Auren may become willing to discuss Mirrorland problems and eventually participate in scene handoffs.

Public facts/navigation must not be artificially paywalled by trust.

A visible trust meter remains a valid design direction pending audit.

## Archetype recognition

Conversational choices may provide evidence toward the four canonical archetypes once Step 2 establishes their exact identities and meanings.

Auren may eventually form and test hypotheses conversationally, for example recognizing that a visitor is coming across like a particular archetype.

Visitor affirmation, qualification or disagreement may itself become additional evidence.

Archetype evidence must alter route, framing, questions and disclosure eligibility — never canonical truth.

## Transactional conversational consequence

The provisional mechanic is intentionally direct:

**current relationship + conversational action -> changed relationship**

Going into a subject, receiving Auren's answer and immediately backing out may itself carry a trust consequence because navigation is part of the conversation rather than invisible UI behavior.

Exact weights/effects remain unbound until the audits.

## Persistence

Same-browser/device persistence is a candidate first implementation so refresh/return does not automatically erase the relationship.

No account-wide, cloud, cross-device or cross-character relationship architecture is authorized yet.

## Differentiated conversations

The goal is that two visitors can use the same touch-choice interface and legitimately experience different conversations because prior conversational behavior changes:

- what Auren thinks he understands about them;
- how much he trusts them;
- how he speaks to them;
- which authored follow-ups are relevant;
- what depth he volunteers;
- which relationship/scene opportunities become available.

This is state-dependent selection from canonical authored truth, not permission to contradict canon.

## Chamber-to-scene game loop

Provisional target:

public chamber
-> conversational relationship
-> increasing/decreasing trust
-> deeper Auren
-> willingness to discuss Mirrorland problems
-> location/problem choice
-> scene with Auren
-> scene behavior continues affecting the relationship

The chamber therefore becomes a potential relationship-based entrance into scenes rather than a conventional game menu.

## Governing design principle

The intended system is a conversational relationship simulator and eventually a conversationally based game about relationships, motives, agendas, general interests, selfish/selfless behavior and interpersonal consequences.

The player should be able to experience bitter or sweet consequences in a fictional environment without those experiments requiring real-world interpersonal stakes.

This definition is preserved for audit comparison and **must be reviewed and re-solidified after Steps 1–4 before becoming binding implementation law.**
