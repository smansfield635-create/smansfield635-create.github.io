Theory

The project proposed that battery wear might appear as several parts of battery condition losing their ability to support performance together. B/P/E/I/V were derived representations and had to be translated into measurements before testing.

Evidence

The positive result is a cell-disjoint held-out association inside the tested battery domain. Direct physical inputs were observed; B/P/E/I/V axes were derived.

Operational interpretation

Possible use remains downstream of validation

A validated future version could potentially support inspection or replacement scheduling, backup-capacity protection, and prioritization. Critical-system deployment is not proven.

Deployment prerequisites

  • Chemistry-specific validation.
  • Load and temperature controls.
  • Calibrated alert thresholds.
  • False-positive and false-negative analysis.
  • Maintenance and safety integration.

Reusable pathway

Future studies may inherit domain translation, frozen hypotheses, unseen-system evaluation, baseline comparison, component tests, negative-result custody, confound analysis, and claim ceilings—not the battery conclusion itself.
