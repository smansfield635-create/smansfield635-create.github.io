Practical

Pressure-to-Capacity Ratio

How much pressure is acting relative to usable capacity?

PCR = Π / max(K, εK)

The pressure-capacity ratio

The ratio compares pressure with protected usable capacity while preserving the reason a safety floor exists.

Practical

The same pressure can be manageable in one state and hazardous in another because usable capacity differs.

Engineering

Kused = max(K, εK) protects division. SAFE_MODE is triggered by K ≤ εK, not by PCR alone.

Evidence

The ratio, safety floor, and SAFE_MODE notation boundary are source-confirmed.

Information

Purpose & limits
Purpose
Compare pressure with capacity without creating an undefined division state.
Limits
PCR is a diagnostic index, not proof of collapse, causation, or universal transfer.
Operation & failure
Architecture
The floor protects computation but does not erase the underlying capacity collapse state.
Operation
Calculate pressure over protected capacity and separately retain whether SAFE_MODE was triggered.
Failure
A small protected denominator can produce a high ratio, but the ratio is not itself the SAFE_MODE trigger.
Symbols
  • PCR — pressure-to-capacity ratio
  • K_used — protected denominator
  • ε_K — capacity safety floor
