This is a prior-art relation, not an evidence class. `APPARENTLY_NOVEL`, `NEW_OPERATIONALIZATION`, or `DISTINCTIVE_SYSTEM_INTEGRATION` never upgrades a scientific result. Numerical novelty scores are prohibited. Priority remains unresolved until a dedicated prior-art review authorizes stronger language.

## C. PROVENANCE / OPERATING CONTEXT

Question: **Under what conditions was this body of work produced?**

Independent development, principal authorship, breadth of operation, repository scale, and exercised runtime use are provenance. They do not establish validity or novelty.

# 5. Two-side card contract

## MEANING side

Audience: an intelligent visitor who may have no engineering or scientific background.

Required function:

1. explain the object in ordinary language;
2. state the practical meaning;
3. explain why a person should care;
4. state any future consequence conditionally;
5. include one clear sentence about what remains unknown.

Forbidden behavior:

- unexplained internal acronyms as the lead;
- dense metrics as the primary explanation;
- turning a future possibility into a current result;
- turning novelty into proof;
- turning independent authorship or scale into scientific support.

## EVIDENCE side

Audience: a technical reviewer, engineer, scientist, researcher, verifier, or skeptical reader.

Required function:

1. name the canonical research object;
2. expose the exact evidence class and domain tags;
3. provide bounded metrics / formal counts / operational identities where authorized;
4. retain negative, null, non-identifiable, and missing-evidence state;
5. state the claim ceiling;
6. state novelty and provenance status separately;
7. identify the next discriminating boundary.

Heavy engineering and research terminology is allowed here.
