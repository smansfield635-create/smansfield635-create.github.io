# EDUCATION CYCLE THREE — PLACEMENT BANK ADEQUACY CONTRACT v1

Status: FROZEN CONSTRUCTION AUTHORITY
Scope: English placement bank + adaptive evidence sufficiency
Product mutation authority: NONE

## Audit finding

The current placement bank contains 21 unique items: three items in each of Stages 2–8 and zero items in Stage 1.

The current runtime clamps learner entry and traversal to Stage 2–8. Therefore the learner-facing choice "Very new to English" cannot currently enter a genuine Stage-1 diagnostic neighborhood.

The current adaptive selector can cycle through a three-item bank. Repeated exposure must not be counted as fresh independent placement evidence.

## Governing architecture

Placement uses eight real diagnostic neighborhoods:

1. Orientation and Baseline
2. Direct Concepts
3. Change the Word
4. Build the Sentence
5. Build the Meaning
6. Read and Respond
7. Unseen Transfer
8. Phase 1 Completion

Self-assessment selects the initial neighborhood only.

## Bank adequacy

Each diagnostic neighborhood MUST contain at least 8 unique placement items before this contract is considered constructed.

Minimum qualified bank:

8 stages × 8 unique items = 64 unique placement items.

Stage 1 must be a genuine beginner bank rather than an alias for Stage 2.

### Stage 1 coverage

Stage 1 must sample multiple elementary written competencies, including at least:

- elementary word/meaning recognition;
- extremely simple sentence meaning;
- basic functional concepts;
- elementary written relationships.

A Stage-1 learner must not be required to demonstrate Stage-2 vocabulary merely because Stage 1 lacks questions.

### Stages 2–8

Each stage must contain enough distinct prompts/nodes to avoid treating repeated exposure as independent confirmation.

Where the stage architecture permits, item pools should sample more than one node or expression of the competency rather than eight cosmetic rewrites of one question.

## Evidence sufficiency

Question count is not the placement objective. Evidence sufficiency is.

A normal in-range placement boundary requires at least 5 UNIQUE relevant observations around the candidate boundary.

Five observations are a minimum boundary-evidence floor, not a requirement to ask five questions at every traversed stage.

If evidence is clean, independent, and consistent, the engine may resolve once the minimum boundary evidence is satisfied.

If evidence is mixed, assisted, contradictory, or otherwise insufficient, the engine should extend using unused items toward approximately 8–10 relevant observations.

If sufficient independent evidence cannot be established, the result must remain uncertain rather than manufacture precision.

## Used-item law

The adaptive runtime MUST maintain a used-item ledger.

For placement authority:

- unseen item = eligible fresh evidence;
- previously answered item = not fresh independent evidence;
- repeated item MUST NOT increase the unique-evidence count;
- item selection preferentially chooses unused items in the required neighborhood;
- exhaustion of a bank must be explicit and must not silently recycle questions as new evidence.

## Traversal efficiency

The learner does NOT answer all 64 items.

The adaptive engine may move rapidly through clearly inappropriate neighborhoods.

Example:

A learner begins at Stage 7.
Initial evidence suggests Stage 7 is too difficult.
A confirming challenge supports descent.
The engine may move toward Stage 6 without collecting five Stage-7 items.

The five-item minimum applies around the eventual candidate boundary where placement is resolved.

## Stage-1 runtime requirement

All lower bounds currently clamped to Stage 2 must be reconciled to permit Stage 1 where appropriate.

This includes:

- provisional entry;
- adaptive traversal;
- downward movement;
- boundary resolution;
- restored-session behavior;
- learner-facing result labels.

Stage 1 must be a legitimate resolved learning entry.

## Independence and assistance

Assisted evidence remains attributable to its item.

Assisted observations may contribute context but must not silently substitute for sufficient independent evidence when resolving an independence boundary.

## Above-range behavior

Stage 8 remains the highest supported diagnostic neighborhood.

Sufficient independent evidence above the Stage-8 boundary may resolve as:

DEMONSTRATED ABILITY EXCEEDS CURRENT PLACEMENT RANGE

The learner must not be marched downward merely to force an in-range result.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- mapping Stage 1 to Stage 2;
- three-item banks presented as sufficient placement instruments;
- modulo recycling counted as new evidence;
- fixed question count regardless of evidence;
- five questions required at every traversed stage;
- eight cosmetic duplicates used to satisfy bank size;
- repeated items counted toward the five-unique-observation floor;
- invented 1,001 coordinates.

## Protected surfaces

This contract does not authorize changes to:

- Cycle One;
- interface-language authority;
- canonical 1,001-concept inventory;
- curriculum ordering;
- speech/listening claims;
- publication architecture.

## Construction acceptance

Construction must prove:

1. eight real diagnostic neighborhoods exist;
2. Stage 1 has its own bank;
3. every stage has at least 8 unique items;
4. total bank contains at least 64 unique items;
5. used-item identity is persisted during placement;
6. repeats cannot increase unique evidence;
7. boundary resolution requires at least 5 unique relevant observations;
8. mixed/assisted/contradictory evidence can extend toward 8–10 observations;
9. clearly inappropriate neighborhoods can be exited before five questions;
10. Stage 1 can be entered, traversed, and resolved;
11. above-range resolution remains valid;
12. learner result remains an honest competency boundary.
