      How we count

      Page loads count page activity. Visits are a separate measure; neither is a unique-person count. Non-bot totals exclude traffic Cloudflare classifies as automated.

      Current measurement boundary

      The summary and rankings cover a rolling 30-day window, ending at the snapshot time. The chart shows 29 complete UTC days and the partial final day. Their start times differ, so the bars need not add up to the summary total. Weekly comparisons use complete UTC days only.

      Home combines / and /index.html. Each ranking entry discloses its recorded sources. “No referrer reported” means the source was not supplied; it does not establish how someone found the site.
