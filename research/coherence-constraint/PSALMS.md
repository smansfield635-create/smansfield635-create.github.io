## Purpose

Define how the coherence-constraint methodology must be perceived before it is applied. PSALMS governs interpretation, not scientific truth, execution, or validation.

## Primary declaration

This system is a candidate constraint-identification and validation method.

It must not be perceived as:
- a proven theory of reality;
- a predictive model;
- a philosophical doctrine;
- an AI truth authority;
- an already-established universal scientific law.

## Stack

PSALMS → perception authority  
6-5-4 application → recognition and compression  
1-2-3 application → adversarial validation  
STOP rule → termination / return-to-investigation boundary

Perceive → compress → test → stop or return.

## Authority map

PSALMS owns system interpretation and layer identification.

6-5-4 owns the proposed movement from observations/variance to patterns to candidate constraints.

1-2-3 owns attempted falsification through candidate definition, polarity/counterexample testing, and cross-domain replication.

STOP owns termination of compression when additional reduction loses explanatory content, or rejection/return when validation requirements fail.

No layer may certify itself.

## Misclassification risks

Primary drift risks:
- treating observations as constraints;
- treating analogy as identity;
- treating recurrence across domains as proof;
- treating a candidate axiom as established law;
- treating AI-assisted articulation as epistemic authority;
- silently replacing the pre-existing foundational 654123 vocabulary with the College Appearances application vocabulary;
- treating philosophical comparison as historical proof.

## Admissibility law

Before applying the method:
1. bound the system;
2. define the terms;
3. identify the evidence class;
4. identify what would count against the claim;
5. distinguish foundational 654123 vocabulary from the epistemic application protocol.

If these are absent: STOP.

## Failure model

Drift appears when compression is forced, counterexamples are explained away by definition, terms change between domains, or validation is used to confirm rather than challenge a favored conclusion.

## Success model

Correct perception produces explicit assumptions, preserved counterexamples, reversible compression, clear falsifiers, and claims whose strength does not exceed the evidence.

## Final law

PSALMS does not make the method true.

PSALMS prevents the room from mis-seeing what the method is while truth-testing remains open.
