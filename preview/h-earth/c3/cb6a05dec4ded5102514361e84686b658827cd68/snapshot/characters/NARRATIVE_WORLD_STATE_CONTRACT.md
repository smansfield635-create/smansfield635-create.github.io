## Governing invariant

`DISCOVERY → WORLD CONSEQUENCE → SPATIAL SIGNAL CONSEQUENCE`

Every world change must derive from explicit narrative state. No star, subordinate light, constellation segment, route cue, architecture reveal, lighting phase, or sensory cue may imply narrative meaning without a state-derived cause.

## Adopted baseline

The following existing Gratitude Coast constructs are frozen unless a demonstrated defect requires bounded repair:

- procedural coastline and terrain-height model
- WebGL2 spatial world and independent water surface
- eleven destination coordinates
- accessible projected DOM discovery controls
- authored destination travel handlers and Bezier camera traversal
- coast map and relationship traversal
- existing `visited` Set as historical discovery bookkeeping

## Successor state seam

`visited → deriveNarrativeWorldState() → WORLD_STATE → renderer + spatial signals + accessible DOM`

`visited` remains historical truth. Derived state owns consequences.

Each existing destination ID receives the same bounded state vocabulary:

- `physicalExpression`
- `arrivalExpression`
- `worldEffects`
- `reveals`
- `sensoryState`

## Initial proof boundary

Only three destinations may receive bespoke world-consequence behavior in the first proof:

- `crossing` — event/geography consequence
- `manor` — architecture/horizon consequence
- `clock` — global environmental consequence

The remaining eight destinations stay structurally represented but do not receive bespoke spectacle until the proof is evaluated.

## Night renderer target

The existing procedural world remains authoritative. The successor may add only spatial/runtime treatment:

- night sky
- moon and restrained lunar illumination
- real-time lunar response over the existing water
- darker terrain response
- atmospheric horizon treatment
- Mirrorland discovery-star expression at existing destination coordinates
- state-derived constellation relationships

No graphic backdrop replacement, free camera, WASD, first-person runtime, or live Compass dependency is permitted.
