## Purpose

Identify manufacturers that can evaluate the Baseline R1 prototype without requiring a premature commercial-scale commitment.

## R1 prototype

Per serving:
- sodium chloride — 750 mg
- potassium chloride — 250 mg
- magnesium citrate — 100 mg, exact raw material pending
- dextrose — 1.5 g
- target dilution — 350 mL water
- target package — single-serve stick/sachet
- current status — engineering prototype, not clinically validated

## Outreach objective

First contact should request:

1. formula feasibility
2. recommended exact magnesium-citrate material
3. elemental magnesium assay
4. whether excipients are necessary
5. unflavored/lightly flavored feasibility
6. dissolution/sediment concerns
7. smallest prototype/pilot quantity
8. stick/sachet MOQ
9. testing options
10. measured osmolality availability
11. stability pathway
12. pilot cost and lead time
13. regulatory-category observations

## Decision rule

Do not select a manufacturer based on sales responsiveness alone.

Compare:
- technical competence
- willingness to preserve formula control
- transparent change control
- prototype accessibility
- testing
- documentation
- cost
- MOQ
- lead time
