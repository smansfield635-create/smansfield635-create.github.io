# H-Earth ADR1 — Architectural Depth Readiness

Contract: `H_EARTH_ADR1_ARCHITECTURAL_DEPTH_READINESS_v1`

## Objective

Prove H-Earth can carry an occupied architectural place before Mirror Manor construction begins.

ADR1 is a diagnostic world-depth gate, not an architecture-design gate. The proxy is intentionally generic and must not create Mirror Manor identity, geography authority, topology authority, or final estate authority.

## Primary visual authority

`PLAYER-SCALE WORLD -> REGIONAL VISTA -> CONTINENTAL CONTEXT`

Player perspective is the primary visual authority. Fidelity follows occupation, not map scale.

## Protected baseline

ADR1 preserves the Gen329 single spherical world manifold and the owner-inspected 23923 direction: continuous ocean, continental outline, broad coastline system, current terrain hierarchy, and accepted responsive traversal. ADR1 does not alter canonical terrain fields, ocean construction, distant-context geography, surface-material authority, the live render package, GPU transport, or the Experience Anchor.

## Diagnostic proxy

The proxy contains only:

- a generic manor-sized mass;
- a sunk foundation plinth;
- a terrace / retaining edge;
- a short terrain-following path;
- a courtyard plane;
- vertical human-scale markers.

It is explicitly **not Mirror Manor**.

## Inspection route

`COAST -> APPROACH -> ESTATE_EDGE -> BUILDING -> FOUNDATION -> COURTYARD -> OUTWARD_VISTA`

The final position looks back toward sea and regional relief from an occupied place rather than an aerial survey.

## Acceptance predicates

1. No terrain representation break at walking distance.
2. No camera penetration or invalid architectural proximity.
3. Architectural contact reads as grounded.
4. Local proxy materials expose multiple environmental-response classes.
5. Player-scale lighting explains form without changing the governing lighting authority.
6. Midground definition is preserved; ADR1 does not increase atmospheric attenuation.
7. Distant geography continues to read as continental.
8. No visible fidelity-domain transition is introduced.
9. Added local geometry stays inside the bounded ADR1 diagnostic budget and the accepted interaction floor remains an owner/runtime inspection requirement.

## Fidelity domains

- **LOCAL** — walking-distance geometry, contact, material response, shadow-readable form.
- **ESTATE** — architecture-supporting morphology, paths, retaining edges, secondary scale structures.
- **REGIONAL** — existing medium terrain/material fidelity.
- **DISTANT** — existing continuous low-cost geography and atmosphere.

Transitions must remain presentation-continuous. ADR1 introduces no map-scale camera authority and no explicit representation switch.

## Machine / owner boundary

The ADR1 harness may prove deterministic geometry, grounding, bounded geometry cost, route availability, architectural stand-off, authority non-expansion, and preservation contracts. It cannot substitute for owner visual inspection of terrain believability, contact appearance, lighting quality, or the interaction-performance floor.

The owner-facing acceptance question remains:

**Can a place exist here without revealing the world's limits?**
