Purpose

Home is the receiver, not the map.

Home steadies the visitor after entry. The Compass handles full-estate orientation.

A receiver page should reduce pressure. It should not demand that the visitor understand every route immediately. It gives enough context to choose a next move.

Content Rule

Value comes before navigation.

The visitor should know why a route matters before being pushed into it.

Each profile explains the value of the next destination. The route button appears after the expandable explanation, not before it.

Boundary

Home does not certify anything.

Home can orient the visitor, but it cannot validate claims, prove systems, or replace evidence rooms.

Laws, Gauges, Frontier, Showroom, Mirrorland, and other rooms carry their own responsibilities. Home only helps the visitor choose where to go next.

Boundary: Home is a receiver and value-profile page. It does not replace the Compass, does not operate as a site-wide directory, does not validate claims, and does not present an orbit map. It clarifies what kind of value the visitor is seeking, then sends the visitor to the appropriate room.
