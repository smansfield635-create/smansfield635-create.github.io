## Why this successor exists

The prior raw TC-RADAR experiment measured a sophisticated but narrow observational scope concentrated on internal hurricane organization. Its corrected authoritative result remains:

`RAW_TC_RADAR_MECHANISTIC_NOT_SUPPORTED`

The successor does not repair, reinterpret, or erase that result.

The scientific distinction motivating this successor is:

`WITHIN_CLASS_RELATIONAL_STRUCTURE != SYSTEM_LEVEL_RELATIONAL_STRUCTURE`

The same corrected historical observations are reorganized into distinct mechanistic classes before a new execution. No outcome labels are changed, no raw radar fields are re-extracted, no new rows are introduced, and the corrected SST missing-value semantics remain fixed.

## Explicitly unmeasured

This source does not directly measure:
- latent heat flux,
- sensible heat flux,
- upper-ocean heat content / warm-layer depth beyond SST/MPI proxies,
- storm-induced cold-wake feedback,
- direct air-sea enthalpy-transfer efficiency.

Therefore this is a cross-class scope test, not a claim that the full hurricane mechanism has been captured.

## Claim ceiling

A favorable result can support a more specific developmental proposition:

`CROSS_CLASS_RELATIONAL_ORGANIZATION_MAY_CONTAIN_INFORMATION_NOT_CAPTURED_BY_INTERNAL_ONLY_OR_BASELINE_VIEWS`

It cannot establish:
- universal coherence laws,
- prospective operational utility,
- earlier warning,
- causality,
- independent confirmation,
- direct transfer/feedback mechanisms,
- or retroactive success of the prior adverse raw-radar test.

The next claim-bearing step after a favorable developmental result would require an independent preregistered test with broader direct class coverage.
