# 2. PSALM construction interpretation

## Perception

The page is a current evidence-state interface, not a generic research portfolio and not a unified success narrative.

A visitor must be able to distinguish at least:

- bounded empirical support;
- configuration-bounded comparison;
- formal proof;
- formal counterexample;
- formal/synthetic evidence;
- negative result;
- not distinguished;
- not identifiable;
- missing evidence / unknown-not-zero;
- preregistered but unexecuted work;
- operating method;
- historical/operating context;
- novelty/distinctiveness status;
- provenance/operating context.

No visual treatment may collapse those states merely because they coexist on one page.

## Structure

The successor page has four major layers:

1. **Orientation / current standing**
2. **Three human chapters containing nine Meaning/Evidence objects**
3. **Challenge / inspection modes**
4. **Secondary research record / exact technical depth**

The 3×3 is semantic. It is not a requirement to display nine equal boxes simultaneously.

## Alignment

Meaning must remain a human translation of Evidence, never an epistemic upgrade.

Evidence status, novelty, and provenance remain independent axes.

Scientific, Agentic, Formal, Engineering, and Operating Context remain domain/evidence tags rather than card sides.

## Law

The following laws are construction invariants:

`NO_CROSSWALK_ROW => NO_PAGE_CLAIM`

`MEANING_SIDE <= EVIDENCE_SIDE`

`NOVELTY != EVIDENCE`

`PROVENANCE != VALIDITY`

`OPERATING_SCALE != SCIENTIFIC_SUPPORT`

`SCIENTIFIC_GENERALITY != ARCHITECTURAL_CROSS_DOMAIN_USE`

`UNKNOWN != ZERO`

`OPERATIONAL_FAILURE != SCIENTIFIC_FAILURE`

`SUCCESSFUL_REASONING != PERMISSION_TO_ACT`

`CONSTRUCTION_CANNOT_GRADE_ITSELF`

`PENDING_WORK != COMPLETED_RESULT`

`LAYOUT_COLLAPSES_BEFORE_TYPOGRAPHY_CRAMPS`

`VISIBLE_BOUNDARY_BEFORE_OPTIONAL_DEPTH`

## Motion

The later interaction grammar is:

`ORIENT -> CHOOSE CHAPTER -> READ MEANING -> OPTIONAL WHY THIS MATTERS -> INSPECT EVIDENCE -> OPTIONAL CHALLENGE MODE -> RETURN TO CHAPTER`

Motion may reinforce hierarchy or contrast. It may not simulate a scientific result, fabricate a measurement, imply causal transfer, or stand in for required information.

1. human object title;

- G11: healthy appearance versus shrinking room to recover;
- G12: intuitive coherence language versus testable formalization;
- G13: AI can assist research versus whether the way it is used measurably helps;
- G21: appearing correct is not the same as authority to change the record;
- G22: build versus independent challenge;
- G23: more safeguards are not automatically better research;
- G31: one independent program versus context-not-proof;
- G32: shared machinery versus separate evidence;

The construction may line-break, size, stack, and spatially contrast these beats. It may not strengthen them.

# 13. Responsive spatial contract

Responsive behavior is semantic before geometric.

## Wide layouts

A chapter may present up to three G-objects in one row when the complete Meaning anatomy remains legible.

Three columns are a maximum, not a target that must be preserved.

## Intermediate layouts

Collapse to two columns as soon as any of the following would occur in three columns:

- dominant contrast beats wrap into body-copy density;
- support copy becomes excessively narrow;
- visible boundary loses distinctness;
- controls crowd each other;
- technical Evidence becomes unusably narrow.

## Narrow layouts

Use one-column chapter reading.

No mandatory horizontal swipe may be the sole way to reach an object or its Evidence.

Split contrasts stack in semantic order.

Question/negative-space treatments retain deliberate space without creating excessive dead scrolling.

Typography must not be reduced primarily to preserve a multi-column layout.

No body copy may be truncated with ellipsis to equalize heights.

Prohibited solely for disclosure persistence:

- `localStorage`
- `sessionStorage`
- cookies
- URL/query/hash mutation
- analytics-backed restoration
- durable application state

# 15. Keyboard, touch, focus, and accessibility

The later live implementation must provide parity across pointer, touch, and keyboard.

Requirements:

- all controls are real interactive elements;
- focus order follows semantic reading order;
- toggling Meaning depth does not strand focus;
- opening Evidence moves focus predictably or retains it on the activating control according to the chosen semantic pattern;
- closing Evidence returns focus predictably;
- no required information is hover-only;
- no distinction is color-only;
- `≠`, arrows, connectors, contraction, and spatial opposition have textual equivalents;
- screen-reader names distinguish `Why this matters`, `View technical evidence`, and `Challenge the result`;
- touch targets remain usable without precision gestures;
- browser Back is not required to exit an object state;
- decorative conceptual diagrams must not be presented as measured data.
