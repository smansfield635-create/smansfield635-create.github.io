# 6. Two-side card contract

## MEANING

Audience: an intelligent visitor who may have no engineering or scientific background.

The Meaning side must:

1. explain the object in ordinary language;
2. state its practical meaning;
3. explain why a person should care;
4. state any future consequence conditionally;
5. state what remains unknown.

It must not lead with unexplained acronyms, protocol jargon, dense metrics, novelty claims, or operating-scale statistics.

## EVIDENCE

Audience: technical reviewers, engineers, scientists, researchers, verifiers, and skeptical readers.

The Evidence side may use heavy technical language. It must:

1. name the canonical object;
2. expose domain tags and exact evidence status;
3. provide authorized metrics, formal counts, operational identities, and configuration details;
4. preserve negative, null, non-identifiable, and missing evidence;
5. state the claim ceiling;
6. state novelty and provenance separately;
7. state the next discriminating boundary.

# 12. Novelty publication rules

2. Evidence is never upgraded because a formulation appears unusual.
3. `APPARENTLY_NOVEL_FORMAL_RESULT` is not a priority finding.
4. `NEW_OPERATIONALIZATION` may describe a specific measurable formulation without claiming invention of its conceptual neighborhood.
5. `NOVEL_BOUNDED_EMPIRICAL_RESULT` describes the exact new observation produced by a frozen study, not invention of agent evaluation or the benchmark category.
6. `CATEGORY_PRIOR_ART_EXISTS` remains explicit for evidence-gated/control-plane and related governance categories.
7. `PRIORITY_UNRESOLVED` remains mandatory where hostile expert prior-art comparison has not closed.
