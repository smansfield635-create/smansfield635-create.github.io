## Governing construct

The placement test measures English proficiency.

Simple question. Clear answer. Increasingly difficult English.

Real-world contexts are permitted only when English comprehension is the dominant source of difficulty. Logic, procedural judgment, coherence reasoning, workplace decision-making, and test-taking inference must not become the primary construct.

## Audit dimensions

- English demand: DOMINANT / MIXED / SECONDARY
- General reasoning burden: LOW / MATERIAL / DOMINANT
- Domain knowledge burden
- Instruction clarity
- Verdict: KEEP / REVIEW / REWRITE

## Rewrite frontier

The strongest construct contamination is concentrated in upper-level rule application, exception handling, temporal calculation, policy inference, conditional device logic, conflicting instructions/agreements, procedural exceptions, and reconciliation of conflicting totals.

These items may use English, but successful response materially depends on solving a problem beyond demonstrating English proficiency.

## Required replacement law

Every REWRITE item must be replaced at the SAME difficulty coordinate with an English-dominant item.

Difficulty may rise through:
- vocabulary depth and precision;
- morphology and grammar;
- syntactic complexity;
- tense/aspect/modality;
- collocation and idiom;
- reference/cohesion;
- paraphrase;
- register;
- longer reading comprehension;
- inference whose answer is recoverable from linguistic context;
- nuanced meaning.

Difficulty must NOT rise primarily through:
- arithmetic;
- procedural decision-making;
- policy optimization;
- workplace judgment;
- coherence/problem-solving puzzles;
- specialized domain knowledge.

REVIEW items require a second-pass determination before construction.

No runtime mutation is authorized by this audit.
