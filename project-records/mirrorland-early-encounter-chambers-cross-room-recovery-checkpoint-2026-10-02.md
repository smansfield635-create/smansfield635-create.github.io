# 2. Cross-character chamber scope correction

All Mirrorland character chambers are **early encounters**.

They are not:
- the complete game;
- the complete character arc;
- unrestricted character chatbots;
- biography interrogation rooms;
- places to exhaust all available canon;
- terminal diagnostic experiences.

They are:
- a taste of the eventual game;
- a taste of the character;
- a taste of the character's story;
- a taste of relationship/connection mechanics;
- internal website navigation/story surfaces;
- reasons to continue through Diamond Gate Bridge and encounter the character again later.

The website itself carries the broader distributed story.

Future character appearances elsewhere on the website/game may reveal additional context that the first chamber intentionally preserves.

## General early-encounter progression

Preferred high-level law:

`MEET -> INTERACT -> NOTICE -> CONNECT -> GLIMPSE -> HANDOFF`

This is not a rigid six-node graph. It describes chamber purpose.

# 3. Character-as-asserter law

The **character owns conversational/story momentum**.

The visitor owns:
- attention;
- natural response;
- local curiosity;
- whether to follow a handoff;
- how they react to what the character exposes.

Free-form or natural visitor expression does **not** mean the visitor may suspend the story indefinitely and interrogate every aspect of the character.

Every substantive character turn should normally do at least one of:
- advance the encounter/story;
- respond briefly to relevant visitor attention and then advance;
- deepen connection in service of the encounter;
- create/perform a necessary handoff.

Characters may:
- initiate;
- redirect;
- decline depth;
- defer a subject;
- plant an unresolved hook;
- change subject;
- move the visitor toward another page/character/place;
- end a line of inquiry because it belongs later.

A hook does not require resolution.

Visitor attention may influence movement. It does not purchase unlimited disclosure.

# 4. Narrative ceiling / future-story preservation

Every early chamber has a hard narrative ceiling.

No amount of visitor persistence or connection during the first encounter automatically unlocks the character's full biography or future game material.

The chamber must preserve future story inventory.

For Auren specifically, the first chamber must not exhaust:
- the parents' international disappearance;
- the international incident;
- what Auren believes is being underrepresented publicly;
- ultimate parent fate;
- deep Vale succession evidence;
- deep sanctuary origin;
- frightened-child origin event;
- betrayed-trust history;
- deepest admission failures;
- protected resident stories;
- later-game discoveries.

These remain available for later Auren encounters, other lawful story surfaces and eventual game development.

# 5. Auren parent hook — first-encounter treatment

The parent-disappearance canon remains exactly as frozen upstream.

However, first-contact presentation is much shallower.

Preferred early hook form:

`I inherited it while they're gone.`

This is a **story seed**, not an invitation to resolve the parent mystery in the same conversation.

The visitor may notice "while they're gone."

Auren may:
- deflect;
- say it is a longer story;
- avoid resolving the ambiguity;
- move the encounter forward.

The first chamber does not need to reveal that "gone" means an international disappearance.

The international incident belongs later unless a separately authorized first-encounter design explicitly changes that ceiling.

# 7. Connection/trust correction

The relationship/connection machinery remains part of the intended Auren experience.

The visible meter remains an interesting and desired concept because it can give the visitor a measurable sense of whether they are connecting with **this character**.

Connection is not:
- a universal communication score;
- a moral grade;
- permission to exhaust the character's story;
- a disclosure override;
- a product-purchase reward.

Attention can affect connection without forcing disclosure.

Connection may affect:
- recognition;
- tone;
- callbacks;
- humor;
- willingness to continue briefly;
- voluntary context within the chamber ceiling;
- which lawful handoff or comment Auren chooses.

The first encounter should sample the mechanic. It should not allow the relationship to be "completed" or maxed out.

The old PUBLIC / ENGAGED / GUARDED implementation is not restored as mandatory underlying law.

# 8. Website/story handoff law

Character chambers are internal website handoff surfaces.

The story should progressively move toward:
- Mirrorland;
- estate/world surfaces;
- products/public work;
- other characters;
- later encounters.

The visitor gets to know a character **while getting to know the story**.

A character chamber is not a stagnant one-on-one conversation whose primary objective is to learn the character's complete biography.

## Auren as perspective hub

Auren can function as a hub from **Auren's angle**.

He may route toward:
- Products and product authorities;
- the Manor/estate;
- Jeeves;
- Elara;
- other lawful internal surfaces as later established.

He is not Jeeves.

His handoffs must sound like Auren's judgment about where the visitor should go next, not neutral concierge language.

Ordinary handoffs should preserve a lawful conceptual possibility to remain briefly with Auren where appropriate, but Auren remains the asserter and keeps momentum moving.

A handoff is not necessarily the end of relationship continuity. Later returns/encounters may remember why the visitor was sent there.

# 10. Elara implementation precedent — completed read-only findings

The actual current Elara implementation was inspected read-only.

Key files:
- `/elara/index.html`
- `/elara/index.js`
- `/elara/elara.voice.js`

Elara contains a stronger chamber precedent than the current Auren runtime.

## Proven Elara structural laws

Her page declares:
- conversation-first;
- contextual route limit = 1;
- automatic opening message limit = 2;
- separate voice authority and runtime authority;
- two visible conversation modes.

Her governing guide law is:

**doorway, not destination**

Her delivery authority states:

**primary purpose: move the visitor into the next meaningful page.**

Default sequence:

`answer -> optional personality flare -> transition -> route`

Delivery bounds:
- ordinary answer maximum: 2 bubbles;
- expressive answer maximum: 3 bubbles;
- warning maximum: 1 bubble;
- maximum personality flares per answer: 1.

Her destination law states that the destination page owns depth while Elara owns sufficient meaning and forward momentum.

Her handoff law states that once the correct owner is clear, stop explaining and open the door.

Prohibited Elara behavior includes:
- five-bubble explanations;
- restating conclusions;
- continuing after the strongest sentence;
- turning guide conversation into destination content;
- generic shared-guide personality language.

## Elara personality mechanism

Elara uses voice-owned **registers**:
- invitation;
- revelation;
- care;
- warning;
- handoff.

She also uses occasional topic-specific **personality flares**.

Flares add character rather than more exposition.

This is a useful precedent for Auren:
- preserve character expression without extending narrative depth;
- use Auren-specific personality behavior, not Elara's tone.

## Elara routing/presentation mechanism

Elara provides:
- contextual route cards;
- one contextual destination at a time;
- mode-specific pathways;
- House Paths;
- fallback destinations;
- status language such as speaking/listening/deciding what to reveal/found the right door.

These are implementation precedents to evaluate, not automatic requirements for Auren.
