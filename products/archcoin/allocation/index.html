Plane 01 · Identity

Distribution gains its own protocol identity.

ARCA / Overview

Domain role

ARCA identifies the Allocation domain inside the four-asset architecture. It is associated with where recognized value goes next, how it is partitioned, and which routing constraints apply.

Asset identity

Name: ARCHCOIN Allocation. Symbol: ARCA. Standard: ERC-20 on the Ethereum EVM family, using 18 decimal places.

Not launched

ARCA remains prospective. Local implementation and transfer qualification do not establish a public launch. No public contract address or deployment transaction is announced for this token.

Plane 02 · Construction

A fixed unit for routing logic, without mutable supply controls.

ARCA / Engineering

Implementation

The shared ArchcoinFixedSupply.sol contract instantiates ARCA with Solidity 0.8.28 and OpenZeppelin Contracts 5.4.0 ERC20.

Fixed-supply behavior

Construction mints the full supply once to a nonzero recipient. The deployed surface has no external mint, public burn, ownership role, pause control, or privileged mutation path.

Qualification

Hardhat 2.28.0 and Toolbox 6.1.0 compile and test ARCA. Its local EVM deployment completes a signed transfer while preserving total supply.

Supply boundary

The local one-million-unit fixture qualifies mechanics only. It does not select the supply or wallet for a future Sepolia or mainnet deployment.

Plane 03 · Traversal

Local routing mechanics come before network representation.

ARCA / Platform

Current surface

The repository binds ARCA to shared source, compilation, tests, local deployment, and signed-transfer qualification. The implementation is substantial but remains local.

Before a public launch

A public launch requires a separately qualified release, confirmed network and supply, a recipient, and explicit deployment authorization. No launch date is announced.

Evidence after deployment

A future platform state may expose an address only with its transaction hash, retrievable bytecode, and independently verified ERC-20 behavior.

Launch status

ARCA is not deployed on Ethereum mainnet. ARCC and ARCR have launched; their deployments do not establish a launch for ARCA.

Plane 04 · Constraint

Distribution meaning cannot outrun evidence.

ARCA / Governance

Immutable token constraints

  • No post-deployment mint.
  • No public burn in version one.
  • No privileged token mutation.
  • Public launch requires separate release qualification and authorization.

Holder-rights boundary

ARCA creates no entitlement to funds, distribution, redemption, governance, ownership, equity, payment, or assignment of value.

Public status

ARCA is a prospective token with a locally qualified implementation. It is not a launched cryptocurrency. Any future launch will need its own verifiable contract address and transaction record.
