# EDUCATION CYCLE THREE — PLACEMENT EVIDENCE CONTRACT v1

Status: FROZEN CONSTRUCTION INPUT
Scope: English Fluency Accelerator placement only
Product mutation authority: NONE

## Purpose

Cycle Three determines an honest English competency starting boundary. It does not claim a precise 1,001-concept coordinate until a separate curriculum-to-concept traversal authority exists.

## Governing law

SELF-ASSESSMENT -> PROVISIONAL PROBE ENTRY -> ITEM-LEVEL EVIDENCE -> ADAPTIVE ADJACENT PROBES -> CONFIRMED COMPETENCY BOUNDARY -> LEARNING ENTRY

Self-assessment may choose where measurement begins. It may never determine verified placement.

## Entry

The single capability self-assessment selects the initial probe neighborhood. A learner claiming substantial ability must begin at a correspondingly higher competency neighborhood rather than elementary material.

## Item evidence ledger

Every answered placement item must preserve at minimum:

- stage
- item id
- competency/node
- evidence class
- correct / incorrect
- assisted / unassisted
- hint use for that item

Evidence must remain attributable to the item that produced it. Global hint count alone is insufficient.

## Evidence classes

Placement must distinguish at least:

- DIRECT_RECOGNITION
- PRODUCTIVE_FORM
- SENTENCE_CONSTRUCTION
- MEANING_CONSTRUCTION
- PRACTICAL_COMPREHENSION
- UNSEEN_TRANSFER
- INTEGRATED_TRANSFER

Different evidence classes may carry different placement significance. A correct recognition item must not silently become equivalent to demonstrated unseen transfer.

## Adaptive traversal

The existing upward/downward/adjacent-probe architecture is retained as the baseline behavior.

Strong evidence may probe upward.
Weak evidence may probe downward.
Mixed evidence requires adjacent confirmation before resolution.

No single self-report response may prevent the diagnostic from moving above or below the provisional entry neighborhood.

## Boundary resolution

Cycle Three resolves a competency boundary, not a pseudo-precise percentage.

The learner-facing result should identify:

1. demonstrated competency below the boundary;
2. the first unresolved or insufficiently demonstrated competency;
3. the recommended starting stage;
4. whether evidence was assisted or unassisted;
5. the traversal used to reach the boundary.

Example form:

Start at: Build the Sentence

Direct concepts and word-change tasks were demonstrated.
Sentence-building is the first unresolved competency boundary.

Percentages derived from only three equally weighted questions must not be presented as precise within-stage competency.

## Assistance

Assistance is measured from observed behavior during placement.

The placement engine must not infer translation dependence from a removed self-report field.

Generic hints are assistance evidence, but they do not establish native-language or bilingual support states unless the interface actually provides and measures that support.

## 1,001 traversal boundary

Cycle Three MUST NOT invent a learner coordinate such as 184 / 1,001 from stage evidence alone.

A precise 1,001 coordinate requires a separately qualified mapping between:

- canonical concepts;
- productive competencies;
- curriculum ordering;
- placement evidence.

Until that authority exists, the verified output is a competency-stage learning entry.

## Protected surfaces

This contract does not authorize changes to:

- Cycle One carousel or exercises;
- top-level EN / ES / ZH interface authority;
- the canonical 1,001-concept inventory;
- curriculum concept ordering;
- publication architecture;
- speech/listening claims;
- Cycle Four or later learning runtime.

## Construction acceptance

Cycle-Three construction is acceptable only if:

- self-assessment affects initial probe only;
- item-level evidence is durable in runtime state;
- assistance is attributable per item;
- evidence classes remain distinguishable;
- adaptive traversal remains bidirectional;
- mixed boundaries receive confirmation;
- learner result reports an honest competency boundary;
- false precision is removed;
- no unsupported 1,001 coordinate is claimed.
