## 1. Objective

Construct the Auren successor chamber so the visible experience implements the accepted early-encounter standard without destroying useful existing Auren semantic, continuity, privacy, product, or relationship machinery.

This is a bounded refit, not permission to rewrite Auren canon or unrelated product/world surfaces.

## 2. Required visible structure

Top-level modes:

`Meet Auren | Products`

Requirements:
- visible as top-level chamber controls;
- same Auren/session across both modes;
- mode switch is not a connection event;
- no automatic penalty for Back;
- no `more products` grouping.

Products mode must directly expose:
- ARCHCOIN;
- Education;
- Five Flags;
- Nutrition;
- Book / The Nine Summits of Love.

## 3. Required conversation model

Replace fixed-choice-tree primacy with an expression-anchor presentation model.

Each authored Auren turn presented for visitor response must expose:
- Auren message(s);
- a bounded set of meaningful expression anchors;
- one natural visitor expression per intended anchor;
- semantic identity for each anchor sufficient for attention/continuity interpretation.

Visitor expressions must not be generic adjacency-dependent paraphrases when a more specific natural expression is available.

The number of visitor expressions is determined by meaningful anchors, not `slice(0,3)` or another arbitrary fixed count.

## 4. Character momentum

Runtime sequencing must permit Auren to:
- initiate;
- answer briefly and continue;
- redirect;
- decline depth;
- defend a boundary;
- plant an unresolved hook;
- offer one contextual handoff;
- resume after a lawful return.

Visitor input influences continuation but does not suspend Auren's encounter momentum indefinitely.

## 5. Delivery bounds

Implement:
- automatic opening <= 2 Auren messages;
- ordinary turn normally 1-2 Auren messages;
- expressive exception <= 3 only when character expression warrants it;
- concise boundary response;
- one contextual destination at a time;
- no repeated conclusion after strongest line;
- typing/listening/thinking/speaking pacing without text inflation.

## 6. Meet Auren first-encounter ceiling

The implementation may expose:
- Auren Vale;
- age 13;
- Manor ownership/taking over;
- high-level inherited responsibility;
- practical protection/control glimpse;
- shallow character/world handoffs.

Parent hook:
`I inherited it while they're gone.`

If pursued naturally, first-encounter maximum clarification may include:
- `they` = parents;
- they disappeared while traveling;
- Auren does not know exactly what happened.

Do not expose in first encounter:
- international-incident exposition;
- public-account dissatisfaction in depth;
- theories/evidence;
- ultimate fate;
- deep succession machinery;
- frightened-child origin;
- full owner-to-custodian story;
- betrayed trust;
- deepest admission failures;
- protected resident identities/stories.

Connection cannot override this ceiling.

## 7. Plain-language delivery

Character-visible dialogue must be audited for specification/design leakage.

Prefer concrete human language.

Do not mechanically ban technical terms when the subject genuinely requires them, but remove internal-system phrasing from ordinary dialogue.

The implementation must specifically revisit current product dialogue containing language such as:
- constraint;
- prospective;
- demonstrated performance;
- public boundary;
- architecture/benchmark evidence;
- optimization;
- engineering-document phrasing where unnecessary.

Underlying product truth must remain precise.

## 8. Product spread contract

Each of the five product spreads must contain:
- concrete short explanation;
- useful/interesting point in ordinary language;
- accurate current status;
- Auren personal angle where lawfully authored;
- distinct expression anchors;
- natural visitor expressions tied to those anchors;
- one depth handoff to rightful authority;
- no browsing/purchase connection reward.

### Five Flags
Must preserve:
- five-signal concept;
- helper points without giving away answer;
- `Can I try it?` anchor;
- current truth: not yet playable through this chamber; demo in the works;
- Auren may be personally excited about it;
- `Why that one?` anchor.

Do not claim demo availability before product authority supports it.

### ARCHCOIN
Preserve actual live/prospective status and no unsupported investment/adoption claims.
Explain relevance before technical detail.

### Education
Explain actual ability/placement concept in ordinary language.
Do not convert system/benchmark evidence into proven human learning outcomes.

### Nutrition
Keep prototype status explicit.
Do not imply clinical validation, final formula, or commercial completion.

### Book
Allow Auren's perspective as a real anchor.
Deep interpretation routes to Elara.
Avoid vague `Different how?`; prefer an anchor such as `What makes the Book different to you?`.

## 12. Handoff contract

At most one contextual destination is foregrounded at a time.

Auren explains enough to make it meaningful, from his perspective, then stops.

Destination owns depth.

Where appropriate, the visitor may briefly choose Auren's perspective instead of immediately leaving, but the chamber must not use that possibility to become indefinite.

Return continuity may remember the originating subject/thread.
