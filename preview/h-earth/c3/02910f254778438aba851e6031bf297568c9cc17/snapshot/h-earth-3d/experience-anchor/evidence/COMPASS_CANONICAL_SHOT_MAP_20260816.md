## Narrative-to-picture choreography

Text timing is not a subtitle track. Exact final frame timings are deferred until the silent picture cut establishes actual motion and dwell, but ownership is locked:

- Opening: `Navigation requires orientation.` may precede the first major Compass reveal. READ first, then release completely into C01 LOOK.
- `Without orientation, there is no navigation.` enters only on C01/C02 deceleration or transition preparation; it must clear before C02's strongest movement.
- Beat 3A `Here at Diamond Gate Bridge,` occupies a calm exit/threshold preparation field before demanding connection imagery.
- Beat 3B `we define our questions through their connections.` completes the thought on a separate calm authority interval; it must not cover the strongest Summits/stars/lattice inspection.
- `And the stars provide the answers.` is positioned at a deceleration/transition edge surrounding the star/question reveal, never over its strongest visual discovery.
- `Reality itself relies on their existence.` is positioned at a calm handoff into structural proof; lattice/Diamond/Laws hero motion must retain uncontested visual authority.
- `Therefore.` is punctuation after proof, during fade-down/deceleration, not a conventional caption over new exploration.
- Beat 7A `You're never lost,` begins the emotional homecoming field after the proof sequence has released.
- Beat 7B `no matter how far from home.` completes during homecoming preparation, before the final Compass becomes visually demanding.
- `Nothing navigates better than a compass.` is the terminal signature statement after/with a clean resolved Compass hold; no fresh exploration may compete beneath it.

1. Compass establishment -> navigation: clean spatial continuation.
2. Navigation -> stars/Heart: dissolve or light-direction continuity.
3. Heart -> Nine Summits: intentional conceptual connection transition.
4. Summits -> questions/stars: light/point match.
5. Questions/stars -> gem/lattice: geometric match.
6. Lattice -> Laws/evidence: decisive cut or controlled fade.
7. Laws/evidence -> `Therefore.`: fade-down punctuation.
8. Never-lost pair -> Compass: homecoming dissolve / spatial return.
9. Compass return -> final statement/signature: clean hold; no slideshow title-card reset.

Transitions are subordinate to source motion. No decorative transition may be used to disguise unrelated footage.
