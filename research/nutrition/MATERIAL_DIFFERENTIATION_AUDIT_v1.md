## 1. PURPOSE

Determine whether the recovered AM/PM and Baseline formulations are materially different enough from current products to justify manufacturer progression.

This is not a patentability opinion and does not establish legal novelty.

The gate asks a narrower product-development question:

> Does the actual product architecture provide a meaningful formulation-, delivery-, timing-, or system-level difference from products already being sold?

Allowed dispositions:

- **PASS** — materially differentiated enough to justify prototype/manufacturer progression.
- **REVISE** — concept remains viable, but current formulation is not sufficiently differentiated yet.
- **KILL** — current product is too close to existing products to justify continued investment.

## 4. AM/PM DIFFERENTIATION FINDINGS

### NOT DIFFERENTIATING BY ITSELF

The following cannot support a material-difference claim alone:

- AM/PM timing
- morning activation / evening recovery narrative
- magnesium glycinate in PM
- broad micronutrient coverage
- personalized daily packs
- medication-interaction screening
- low-friction daily packetization

These are already represented in current products.

### POTENTIALLY DIFFERENTIATING

The recovered architecture still contains a possible product-level distinction if it is actually engineered and demonstrated:

**A. Minimum practical unit burden**

The recovered target of approximately five physical units/day is materially leaner in concept than many large supplement-stack or multi-pack systems, but this must be confirmed against direct competitor serving burdens and real fill-volume feasibility.

**B. Deliberately sparse formula**

The recovered system is much narrower than HealthyCell-style 90+ ingredient systems and avoids large botanical/adaptogen stacks.

This can become a real design identity:

> broad essential-micronutrient coverage without unnecessary ingredient proliferation.

**C. Interaction-driven temporal partitioning**

This could be meaningful only if AM/PM placement is demonstrably driven by:
- absorption conflicts,
- food dependence,
- GI tolerance,
- dose burden,
- specific timing constraints,

rather than a generic “energy morning / recovery night” split.

**D. Integrated EPA/DHA**

The recovered architecture explicitly includes a separate 1 g EPA+DHA component within the daily system. This is useful integration but is not established as unique by itself.

**E. Bounded personalization**

Potential differentiation exists if personalization alters a compact common formula under strict limits rather than simply assembling a larger custom pill pack.

That architecture is not yet implemented.

---

## 5. OBJECT_A GATE DECISION

**DISPOSITION: REVISE**

Reason:

The current recovered formula is credible as a compact AM/PM multinutrient prototype, but **AM/PM timing and personalization are already occupied market territory**.

The product earns progression only if the next version proves at least one substantive difference in the product itself:

1. unusually low total physical unit burden for comparable essential coverage,
2. interaction-driven partitioning that differs materially from conventional AM/PM storytelling,
3. materially cleaner/sparser formula with no unnecessary botanicals,
4. bounded personalization without expansion into a large supplement stack.

### Hard consequence

Do **not** send OBJECT_A_AMPM to a manufacturer as a differentiated commercial product yet.

Manufacturer feasibility may still be used to test the unit-burden hypothesis, but commercialization is gated on substantive differentiation.

## 8. BASELINE DIFFERENTIATION FINDINGS

### NOT DIFFERENTIATING BY ITSELF

The recovered four-ingredient formula does not establish material uniqueness through ingredients alone.

Sodium + potassium + magnesium + carbohydrate already exists across hydration products.

Likewise, the following are not enough by themselves:

- low sugar
- simple ingredient count
- natural/minimal formulation
- hydration support
- electrolyte powder
- pre-meal timing alone

Pre-meal consumer nutrition products now exist.

### IMPORTANT FORMULATION OBSERVATION

The recovered Baseline amounts are recorded as **compound masses**, not guaranteed elemental-mineral amounts.

Therefore direct comparison against competitor nutrition labels must not treat:
- 750 mg sodium chloride as 750 mg sodium,
- 250 mg potassium chloride as 250 mg potassium,
- 100 mg magnesium citrate as 100 mg elemental magnesium.

This reconciliation must occur before any label-level superiority or ratio claim is made.

### POTENTIALLY DIFFERENTIATING

Baseline still has a potentially defensible white space if the formulation is intentionally optimized around the **meal transition**, rather than generic hydration or appetite suppression.

That would require the product itself to embody this architecture:

**A. Meal-transition rather than sports/recovery hydration**

The intended state transition is:
pre-meal → meal → post-meal

not:
exercise → sweat replacement → recovery.

**B. Not a protein preload**

BOOST demonstrates that pre-meal positioning itself is occupied, but its mechanism/product architecture is fundamentally different:
- 10 g whey protein
- satiety/hunger focus
- GLP-1 framing
- small ready-to-drink shot

Baseline can remain distinct if it stays a light electrolyte/carbohydrate preparation rather than converging toward protein/fiber appetite suppression.

**C. Very low powder burden**

Recovered Baseline is only ~2.6 g total powder per 500 mL, substantially lighter in material burden than many flavored hydration mixes and protein preloads.

This is an engineering distinction worth testing, but not yet a validated consumer advantage.

**D. Possible electrolyte/carbohydrate ratio optimized specifically for pre-meal use**

This could become the central formulation difference.

It is not yet established.

The current historical formula was not demonstrated to be optimized against:
- water,
- Hydrant-like hydration,
- higher-sodium electrolyte products,
- pre-meal protein products.

## 9. OBJECT_B GATE DECISION

**DISPOSITION: REVISE — CLOSEST TO PASS**

Reason:

Baseline has a more distinctive **use architecture** than OBJECT_A, but the recovered four-ingredient formula is still compositionally close to ordinary electrolyte/glucose hydration products.

The concept becomes materially different only if the formulation is deliberately engineered for the meal-transition use case and produces a meaningful reason for its exact:
- sodium level,
- potassium level,
- magnesium level,
- glucose level,
- fluid volume,
- pre-meal timing.

### Hard consequence

Do **not** manufacture the historical four-ingredient recipe merely because it is simple.

First determine whether its ratios and serving design are actually optimized for the intended pre-meal function.
