# Agentic Frontier Comparative Study v1

Status: FROZEN_PROTOCOL
Public baseline: 4abee910e521229ec1a90004372d77dee233e297
Primary adversary: OpenHands v1.14.0
OpenHands target: c0ba9e6d2b73dca07fe1127b91c1eff719853846
Population: 24 paired tasks

## Research question
Does the Diamond Gate execution architecture produce superior verified material outcomes, lower human intervention burden, stronger recovery, or stronger collaborative continuity than OpenHands under equivalent frozen task contracts?

## Design
Four strata of six paired tasks each: implementation/repair, long-horizon repository work, collaborative handoff, and failure/recovery. Every pair must freeze starting state, task contract, available tools, acceptance criteria, model/configuration attribution, and human-intervention rules before outcomes are opened.

## Primary measures
1. Acceptance success and specification coverage.
2. Human interventions and intervention severity.
3. Verified material outcome per elapsed execution window.
4. Regression incidence and recovery success.
5. Collaborative handoff continuity and authority/scope preservation.
6. Cost/token telemetry where genuinely available; unavailable telemetry is reported as unavailable rather than estimated.

## Fairness rules
No post-outcome protocol tuning. No raw PR-count winner claim. SUPPORT/infrastructure work is not independent material output. Configuration differences must be attributed. Diamond Gate failures remain in the corpus. OpenHands failures remain in the corpus. A system is not credited until the frozen acceptance criteria are satisfied.

## Claim boundary
The study may establish performance under this protocol. It may not establish universal superiority, global rank, or company endorsement. Any public conclusion must name the exact OpenHands version/target and the bounded experimental conditions.
