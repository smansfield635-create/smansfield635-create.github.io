What First Contact Changes

Four assumptions break immediately.

The practical danger is not only what waits on the other side. It is what people bring with them when they believe discovery makes them entitled to control.

Assumption One Reachable does not mean understood.

Access reveals a surface. It does not provide the complete history, culture, system logic, or consequence map behind it.

Assumption Two Discovery does not create ownership.

Mirrorland existed before Dextrion found it. Contact changes the relationship, not the world’s right to exist independently.

Assumption Three Good intentions do not prevent damage.

Help offered without context can destabilize the system it is meant to repair.

Assumption Four Returning home does not erase responsibility.

Once you carry knowledge across the threshold, you must decide what can be repeated, tested, adapted, or withheld.

Your First Mission Decision

Enter as a witness.

Before you try to fix a system, learn what it is protecting. Before you transfer an idea, learn what conditions made it work. Before you call something progress, identify who bears the cost when it fails.
