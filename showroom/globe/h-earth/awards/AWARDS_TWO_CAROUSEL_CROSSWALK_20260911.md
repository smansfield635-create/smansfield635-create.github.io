## 1. Governing separation

The Awards page contains two carousels with different jobs. They must not collapse into one content system.

### Top carousel — five memorable estate claims

Purpose: answer **Why should a judge care?**

Front-card law:

```text
TITLE
HEADLINE

[LIVING CURRENT ANIMATION / PROOF]

ENTER
```

The front contains no explanatory paragraph, technical qualifier, evidence summary, provenance note, secondary thesis, or film.

Opening a top card reveals:

1. one compelling or surprising fact that makes the headline true in Diamond Gate Bridge;
2. the connection of that fact to the wider estate;
3. concise supporting evidence and claim boundaries where useful;
4. a route into the current work when appropriate.

The opened state is not a substitute for the lower award chapter film. Top-card content must remain fact-led and estate-led.

### Bottom carousel — six Trophy Standard award chapters

Purpose: answer **Where can this work legitimately compete, and what is the chapter case?**

The bottom carousel is reserved for the six award-lane chapters and the films made for those chapters. The chapter film is the distinguishing media object of the opened bottom card.

Opening a bottom card may contain:

1. the chapter film;
2. the chapter argument;
3. potential award/category correspondence;
4. supporting proof and routes into the live work.

The films belong to this lower chapter system, not to the top five estate-claim cards.

## 2. Authority relationship

The five top claims are **not** the award categories.

The six lower chapters are **not** required to map one-to-one to the five top claims.

The governing relationship is a matrix:

```text
ACHIEVEMENT <-> PUBLIC CLAIM <-> AWARD LANE <-> EVIDENCE <-> SUBMISSION
```

One estate achievement may support multiple award lanes. One award lane may draw evidence from multiple estate achievements.

No existing award lane is retired merely because the five public claims change. Removing an award lane requires a separate finding that the current estate no longer supports that competitive field.

## 3. Top-carousel successor crosswalk

### Card 01 — EXPERIENCE

**Title**

`EXPERIENCE`

**Headline**

`A website can behave like a place.`

**Front animation / proof**

Use the current Compass or a bounded current representation of the actual spatial orientation system. The animation should show a real estate interaction such as a current object resolving forward, a room/orientation relationship, or another unmistakably current Compass behavior.

Do not substitute a retired schematic, generic diagram, legacy illustration, or unrelated concept asset.

**Opened fact / argument**

The memorable fact is that Diamond Gate Bridge does not organize the visitor primarily as page-to-page navigation. Orientation, movement, selection, travel and explicit return are part of the experience grammar.

The opened state should connect this to the orientation cinematic, Compass, rooms, Mirrorland transitions and return semantics without turning the front card into documentation.

- Compass & Navigation

- Integrated Experience & Original IP
- Independent Construction / technical craft where relevant

## 6. Visual provenance law

Previous construction overvalued technical donor authenticity. A repository-owned donor or direct renderer reuse is not automatically the correct Awards representation.

The successor rule is:

```text
CURRENT PUBLIC PROOF
-> BEST CURRENT REPRESENTATION
-> BOUNDED AWARDS ADAPTATION
```

not:

```text
AVAILABLE INTERNAL DONOR
-> AWARDS CARD
```

Therefore:

- legacy or retired imagery is presumptively disqualified from the top carousel;
- abstract diagrams must justify themselves against current live product proof;
- direct renderer reuse is allowed only when it remains the strongest current representation;
- front animation must reinforce the headline rather than merely prove that an internal component exists;
- no visual is retained solely because it appeared in a predecessor Awards implementation.

## 7. Language law

Top-card headlines must operate as memorable arguments rather than internal taxonomy.

They should be understandable without prior Diamond Gate Bridge vocabulary and should contain a familiar assumption plus an unexpected turn where possible.

Disallowed front-card tendencies include:

- internal architecture jargon;
- PR numbers as the central achievement;
- category labels masquerading as claims;
- explanatory secondary paragraphs;
- technical qualification text;
- evidence dumps;
- abstract statements that require repository context to understand.

Detailed engineering, provenance, receipts, claim boundaries and qualification belong behind ENTER or on the dedicated Evidence / Governance / Developer surfaces.

## 8. Non-duplication law

The top and bottom carousels may discuss the same estate object from different roles, but they must not duplicate each other.

Top carousel:

```text
MEMORABLE CLAIM
-> CURRENT ANIMATION
-> ENTER
-> INTERESTING FACT / WHY THE CLAIM IS TRUE HERE
```

Bottom carousel:

```text
AWARD CHAPTER
-> OPEN
-> CHAPTER FILM
-> CATEGORY CORRESPONDENCE
-> SUPPORTING PROOF
```

If a top-card opened state begins behaving like a chapter film package, it has crossed into the bottom carousel's role.

If a bottom chapter loses its award correspondence and film identity and becomes only a short estate fact, it has crossed into the top carousel's role.

## 9. Program-level calibration

The six Trophy Standard lanes are strategic fields, not the final list of award-program category names.

A later program-specific crosswalk must map each lane to the then-current award programs, category names, eligibility rules, submission deadlines, asset requirements and candidate estate objects.

Program-level targeting must not rewrite the five memorable estate claims merely to mirror a program's taxonomy.

## 10. Acceptance test for future Awards work

Before approving a future top-carousel successor, verify:

1. exactly five top claims remain legible and memorable;
2. each front card contains only title, headline, current living animation/proof and ENTER;
3. each opened top card contains a compelling fact and estate connection rather than a chapter film;
4. no retired visual survives by inheritance alone;
5. COHERENCE is represented as a first-class current achievement;
6. TRUST is about earned claims/evidence/authority, not merely PR #1000;
7. WORLD uses current world proof rather than retired Brain imagery;
8. ESTATE uses current estate identity rather than generic platform abstraction;
9. the six lower Trophy Standard chapters remain intact as award lanes unless separately retired;
10. each lower chapter preserves its film role and category correspondence;
11. no top-claim rewrite silently removes an award lane;
12. claim boundaries remain consistent with current public Evidence, Governance and live product state.
