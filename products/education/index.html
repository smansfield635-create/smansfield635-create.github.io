Performance-Gated Language Learning

Learn from where you actually are.

This program does not assume every learner starts at the beginning. It combines a canonical 1,001-concept foundation with productive language systems, adaptive placement, guided practice, and target-language-only demonstration.

Phase 1 currently measures reading comprehension and written or constructed expression. It does not claim to measure speaking, pronunciation, listening, or oral fluency.

What does the benchmark evidence establish?

The canonical 1,001-concept architecture achieved 100% practical completion on the third prospective frozen 768-task benchmark when the productive morphology law already in force was scored correctly. That is internal architecture evidence. It is not a claim that a human learner becomes fluent after completing this demo.

What happens after placement?

The verified placement becomes the learner's starting coordinate. A learner may begin inside a stage rather than replaying work they already demonstrated. If the learner tests above Phase 1, that result remains intact even though the current playable demo is limited to Phase 1.

How is support treated?

Native-language help is legitimate during learning, but assistance is recorded. Final proficiency and final transfer evidence require target-language-only performance.

Language-support cycles

NATIVE_SUPPORTED → BILINGUAL_BRIDGED → TARGET_DOMINANT → TARGET_ONLY.

Stages define what competency is being taught. Cycles define how much native-language scaffolding is available.

Adaptive placement law

SELF_REPORT → PROVISIONAL_TEST_ENTRY → ADAPTIVE_DIAGNOSTIC → VERIFIED_PLACEMENT → DEMO_ENTRY_INITIALIZATION.

Strong performance probes upward. Weak performance probes downward. Within-stage position is derived from competency evidence, not screen count.

Measurement separation
  • Direct vs productive
  • Trained vs transfer
  • Assisted vs unassisted
  • Native-supported vs target-only
