# Classical Philosophy Stress Tests v1

Status: COMPARATIVE RESEARCH NOTES — NOT HISTORICAL OR PHILOSOPHICAL PROOF

## Purpose

Preserve the Plato / Aristotle / Socrates comparisons developed during the College Appearances run while correcting claim strength. These comparisons are hypotheses about structural resemblance. Primary-text scholarship is required before stronger historical claims are made.

## Plato

Working resemblance:
- multiplicity / appearances can be compared with the protocol’s observation/variance layer;
- movement toward intelligible structure can be compared with pattern compression;
- Forms invite comparison with invariance.

Important limit:
Plato’s Forms must not simply be equated with modern “constraints.” The metaphysical, epistemological, and ethical commitments of the Forms exceed this method’s current definitions.

Research question:
Can 6-5-4 provide a reproducible procedure for identifying candidate invariants without presupposing Platonic metaphysics?

## Aristotle

Working resemblance:
- inquiry from particulars resembles observation-level intake;
- classification and causal analysis resemble pattern organization;
- Aristotle’s accounts of first principles, demonstration, induction, substance, and causes must be examined before claiming he lacked a stopping rule.

Important correction:
The earlier shorthand “Aristotle mastered 6-5 but never reached 4” is too strong without primary-text analysis.

Research question:
How does the proposed STOP rule compare with Aristotle’s treatment of first principles and demonstration?

## Socrates

Working resemblance:
- elenchus is relevant to contradiction detection and adversarial testing;
- aporia is relevant to the treatment of unresolved inquiry.

Important limit:
Socratic aporia should not automatically be characterized as a failed closure mechanism. It may serve a different philosophical function.

Research question:
Can the 1-2-3 adversarial protocol be compared meaningfully with elenchus without reducing Socratic inquiry to modern falsification?

## Current finding

The three comparisons are useful stress-test lenses, but none currently validates 6-5-4, 1-2-3, the compression metric, or the candidate coherence boundary.

Next scholarly gate:
primary-text citations + specialist review + explicit counterexamples.
