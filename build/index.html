Timing, payment, support & boundaries

Timing

Focused builds can move from consultation to delivery in as little as one week. Interactive work can be completed within a month. Advanced systems may require up to three months. Agreed scope, access, materials, integrations and timely feedback affect the actual schedule.

Payment

The existing Custom model remains 50% to begin and 50% after acceptance of the agreed build. Where the project supports it, you inspect the work in a separate environment before the remaining half is due.

Support

Every Custom package includes its first month of post-launch support: minor delivered-build fixes, small content or presentation refinements, compatibility checks and limited follow-up guidance. Continuing afterward is optional.

Boundaries

Support and refinement—not routine maintenance or unlimited expansion. Urgency does not automatically increase the price. New features, redesigns, major integrations, expanded data work or other material growth are scoped separately rather than quietly absorbed into post-launch support.
