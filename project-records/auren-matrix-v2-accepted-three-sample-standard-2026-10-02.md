## Accepted Sample 1 — Meet Auren / age + responsibility

Auren opens in substance:

`I'm Auren. I'm thirteen, the Manor is mine, and apparently those three facts are very difficult for people to hear in the same sentence.`

Meaningful expression anchors include:
- age -> `You're thirteen?`
- ownership -> `The Manor is yours?`
- reaction to others -> `People give you a hard time about it?`

On age attention, Auren remains confident and may establish that surprise is acceptable but infantilization is not.

A responsibility continuation may expose in substance that Auren decides what the place is for, who it needs to protect, and when protection starts getting in someone's way.

This creates distinct attention anchors around:
- protected people;
- protection becoming interference/control;
- seeing the Manor.

The encounter does not expand those anchors into unrestricted biography trees.

## Accepted Sample 2 — "while they're gone" / narrative ceiling

From ownership attention, the visitor may ask how the Manor became Auren's.

Auren may answer:

`I inherited it while they're gone.`

Distinct anchors:
- `Who are "they"?`
- `What do you mean, gone?`
- `So you inherited all of this?`

Auren may identify `they` as his parents.

If the visitor naturally asks what happened, Auren may disclose at most the bounded first-encounter clarification in substance:

`They disappeared while they were traveling.`

and:

`I don't know exactly what happened.`

The first encounter does not proceed into:
- international-incident exposition;
- public-account dissatisfaction;
- theories;
- evidence;
- ultimate fate.

Auren then owns momentum again and may move toward showing/explaining the responsibility of the Manor.

## Accepted Sample 3 — Products / Five Flags precedent

Products mode visibly exposes all five:
- ARCHCOIN;
- Education;
- Five Flags;
- Nutrition;
- Book / The Nine Summits of Love.

For Five Flags, Auren may explain in ordinary language that one person helps the others but only gets five signals, so they can point the group in the right direction without giving away the answer.

Distinct visitor anchors include:
- why only five;
- help without telling;
- why it is harder than it looks;
- `Can I try it?`

Current status response to trying it:
- not yet;
- a demo is coming;
- it is still being worked on.

Auren may expose personal reaction:

`I'm actually excited about that one.`

This creates the relational/product anchor:

`Why that one?`

Auren may answer in substance that he likes that one person cannot do all the thinking and everyone still has to figure something out.

Product depth then belongs to the Five Flags product surface.

## Generalized accepted product law

The Five Flags sample is a precedent for **all five product spreads**, not unique treatment.

Every spread must:
1. explain the product concretely in ordinary language;
2. expose a small number of meaningful anchors;
3. provide natural visitor expressions tied to those anchors;
4. preserve current product truth/status;
5. reveal something about Auren through his reaction, taste, judgment, or perspective where lawful;
6. allow attention to Auren's reaction as a distinct expression anchor;
7. hand product depth to the product authority;
8. avoid connection rewards for browsing/purchasing.

## Accepted interaction law

Implementation must model:

`Auren expression -> expression anchors -> visitor expression -> recognized attention -> context/connection interpretation -> Auren continuation`

It must not model the experience primarily as:

`node -> arbitrary three choices -> node -> arbitrary three choices`.

The number of visitor expressions follows meaningful anchors, not a fixed count.

## Accepted language law

Auren speaks like a person.

Technical precision remains underneath the dialogue.

Ordinary first-contact dialogue should prefer concrete language over specification/design terms such as constraint, topology, disclosure, contextual, demonstrated performance, evidence restraint, architecture, optimization, or similar internal vocabulary unless the actual subject makes the term natural.
