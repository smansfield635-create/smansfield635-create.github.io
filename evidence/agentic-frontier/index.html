Scene 03 · Governed agency

Agency is separated from authority.

The system distinguishes who can act from what may lawfully change. State identity, mutation scope, verification, receipts, merge, publication, and recovery remain separate transitions.

Documented multi-agent topology is evidence of coordination structure. It is not yet proof that the topology improves efficiency.
