Qualified Collapse Predicate

When is collapse formally qualified rather than merely suspected?

CollapseQualifiedd = (B256d ≥ 256) ∧ (P192d ≥ 192) ∧ (min(Ed, Id, Vd) ≤ εd)

The collapse predicate

Collapse requires simultaneous saturation of burden and pressure plus failure of the weakest required spine axis.

Practical meaning

Things can begin breaking down before anyone calls the situation a crisis, but qualification requires all declared conditions.

Engineering interpretation

The predicate is conjunctive and noncompensatory. Every clause must be true at the same evaluation state.

Purpose

Prevent elevated burden, elevated pressure, or isolated weakness from being mislabeled as fully qualified collapse.

Limits

Diagnostic qualification does not by itself establish universal empirical proof, causation, or operational readiness.

Pressure-to-Capacity Ratio

How much pressure is acting relative to usable capacity?

PCR = Π / max(K, εK)

The pressure-capacity ratio

The ratio compares pressure with protected usable capacity while preserving the reason a safety floor exists.

Π = G · X K = P · R · A · C Kused = max(K, εK)

Practical meaning

The same pressure can be manageable in one state and hazardous in another because usable capacity differs.

Engineering interpretation

Kused = max(K, εK) protects division. SAFE_MODE is triggered by K ≤ εK, not by PCR alone.

Purpose

Compare pressure with capacity without creating an undefined division state.

Limits

PCR is a diagnostic index, not proof of collapse, causation, or universal transfer.
