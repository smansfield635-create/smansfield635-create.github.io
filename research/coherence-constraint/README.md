## Compression measurement

Proposed operational measure:

Q = Coverage × Parsimony × Consistency

A compression step is provisionally accepted only when:
- explanatory coverage remains within an explicitly chosen tolerance;
- parsimony improves without hiding counterexamples;
- internal contradiction does not increase;
- the resulting statement remains testable.

This metric is a proposed operationalization and requires independent methodological review before being treated as validated measurement.

## Start / stop law

START when the bounded inquiry contains meaningful variance, competing explanations, or unresolved observations.

STOP compression when further reduction destroys explanatory coverage or removes distinctions needed to test the claim.

A candidate invariant is admissible for further testing only when:
1. its terms are operationally defined;
2. a concrete falsification condition can be stated;
3. polarity / counterexample testing has been attempted;
4. cross-domain translation does not silently change the meaning of the terms.

Failure at any gate returns the claim to investigation rather than promoting it.

## Validation discipline

The 1-2-3 protocol is adversarial:
- 1: state the candidate boundary precisely;
- 2: search coherent and incoherent / positive and counterexample cases;
- 3: test translations in multiple domains.

Cross-domain recurrence is evidence of structural similarity, not proof of universality.
