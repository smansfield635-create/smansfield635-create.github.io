How the models are used

A model becomes usable only when its assumptions and failure conditions remain visible.

The model field carries the equations. This layer explains the shared discipline: admit the inputs, declare the method, freeze the comparison, expose the proposition to failure, and preserve the result.

01Admit

Define what may enter and preserve explicit no-match outcomes.

02Construct

Declare variables, equations, procedures, zero behavior, and notation.

03Compare

Freeze baselines, comparators, thresholds, partitions, and expected outputs.

04Falsify

State the conditions that would defeat the interpretation.

05Record

Preserve positive, negative, mixed, held, and non-consumable outcomes.
