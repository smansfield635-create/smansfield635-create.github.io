## 2. Problem statement
The current chamber is materially improved architecturally but still treats conversation too much like a node-by-node dialogue tree.

Observed defects include:
- grammatical clarification masquerading as story progression, e.g. legacy "Who are they?";
- visitor expressions that merely restate facts;
- interrogation ladders that extract details without developing meaning;
- privacy applied too broadly to questions that should open the named story cast;
- too few outward story/world bridges;
- completed paths not cleanly restoring meaningful unvisited paths within their family;
- risk of repetition before the first-encounter traversal is genuinely exhausted.

The chamber must feel like a traversable early encounter, not a questionnaire and not a navigation menu.

## 4. Traversal law
Every beat is part of a traversal path.

Runtime does not need hardcoded conversational bridges between every node.

Within the active family:
1. Follow the visitor-selected path while it has meaningful first-encounter material.
2. Mark consumed beats/path territory as visited.
3. Preserve unvisited paths within that same family.
4. When the current path reaches its natural ceiling or handoff, restore meaningful unvisited paths from that family.
5. Structural movement may go backward to an earlier fork if that fork was not discussed.
6. Narrative movement must still move forward because the restored material is new to the visitor.
7. Consumed material should not replay while meaningful unvisited material remains.
8. Repetition becomes permissible only after meaningful first-encounter material in that family is exhausted.
9. Repetition after exhaustion must acknowledge prior conversation rather than pretending the encounter restarted.

No global relevance engine is required for this behavior.

## 5. Visitor-expression law
Every visitor expression must earn the next beat.

A valid visitor expression does at least one of:
- pursues a meaningful consequence;
- tests or challenges Auren's perspective;
- opens meaningful story/relationship territory;
- reveals something useful about the visitor;
- advances toward a naturally earned website/world handoff.

Invalid by default:
- pronoun clarification when the referent is already obvious;
- noun extraction;
- paraphrasing Auren's previous sentence;
- confirming an already-established fact;
- generic "tell me more" filler;
- another question solely to prolong the exchange.

Example:
- REMOVE: "Who are they?" after Auren has already said "my parents."
- KEEP: "What happened?" after learning the parents disappeared.
- RETURN: Auren answers "What happened?" once and then returns naturally to consequence, e.g. what became his responsibility.

A meaningful short follow-up is allowed. An interrogation ladder is not.

## 6. Story disclosure and privacy law
Privacy must not suppress the public story.

Questions such as "Who else is here?" or "Who is in the Manor?" are potential story/cast bridges, not automatically protected-identity requests.

The successor matrix must distinguish:
A. named/public story characters whom Auren may identify and briefly discuss;
B. unnamed children/residents/people whose identities remain private.

Auren may name canon-authorized story characters such as Dextreon and Alaric only after repository/canon authority for their first-encounter disclosure and characterization is recovered.

For protected unnamed residents:
- existence may be acknowledged where canon permits;
- names/private stories remain protected;
- privacy activates when the visitor presses for protected identity/details, not merely because the visitor asks who exists in Auren's world.

## 7. Destination law
Website destinations are consequences of meaningful traversal, not artificial navigation prompts.

A surviving conversational family must ultimately resolve into at least one:
- meaning -> relationship/story continuation -> destination;
- meaning -> product/world continuation -> destination;
- meaning -> deliberate first-encounter ceiling because deeper material is intentionally reserved.

Potential existing destination classes to audit:
- Mirror Manor
- Characters / named cast
- Audralia / wider world
- Coherence Diagnostic
- Elara
- individual product surfaces

These are candidates, not mandatory destinations for every path.

Do not invent dialogue solely to expose a destination.
Do not hardcode a bridge merely because a page exists.
A destination becomes available when the traversal naturally earns it.
