Plain-language reading

Accountability is ownership without excuse transfer. It links actor, authority, action, outcome, correction, and review rather than stopping at intention.

Ownership

Claim the outcome, not merely the intention.

Attribution

Identify who or what actually caused the action.

Repair

Correct the condition and reduce recurrence.

Pressure response

Keep ownership intact when incentives change.

Engineering controls

Expose authority boundaries, write ownership, approval gates, custody transitions, immutable receipts, repair ownership, and unresolved responsibility.

Failure behavior

Accountability fails when blame or ownership is transferred without evidence, authority is assumed after the fact, or explanation substitutes for repair.

Reading layers

Practical, Engineering, and Empirical claims remain separate.

Practical reading

Identify the people, organizations, or operating roles responsible for decisions and consequences.

Engineering reading

Expose authority boundaries, write ownership, approval gates, custody transitions, and immutable receipts.

Empirical reading

Show which actions and decisions are supported by logs, records, attestations, or missing custody evidence.

Method

For each material action, preserve actor identity, authority basis, action, resulting state, receipt, review path, and repair disposition.

Audit boundary

A log proves an event occurred; it does not by itself prove rightful authority. An assignment proves authority was granted; it does not prove correct execution.
