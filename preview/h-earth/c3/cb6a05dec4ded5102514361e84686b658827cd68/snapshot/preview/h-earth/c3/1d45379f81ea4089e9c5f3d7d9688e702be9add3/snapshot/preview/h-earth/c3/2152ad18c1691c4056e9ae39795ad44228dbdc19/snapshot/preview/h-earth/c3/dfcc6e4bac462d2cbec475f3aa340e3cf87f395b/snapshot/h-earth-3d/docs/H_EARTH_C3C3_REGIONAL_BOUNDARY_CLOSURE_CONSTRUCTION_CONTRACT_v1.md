## 3. Architectural conclusion

C3C3 adopts SELECTIVE ENCLOSURE rather than universal natural-terrain continuation.

### 3.1 Open directions

Ocean-facing sectors remain open. Requirements:

- no enclosing wall;
- no fake opposite landmass;
- no terrain cap inserted across open water;
- sky and ocean remain visually continuous to atmospheric distance;
- distant ocean/horizon treatment may use haze, curvature cues, spectral falloff, and far-field water expression, but must not expose renderer background or geometry termination.

### 3.2 Connected-region directions

Problematic landward/lateral sectors become intentional boundaries to other regions. Requirements:

- boundary reads as part of the world, not as a technical limit;
- it may use ridge, pass, canyon, forest line, architecture, wall, escarpment, mist bank, silhouette layering, or another region-appropriate form;
- distant content may be visually unresolved through atmospheric perspective, haze, depth falloff, reduced contrast, and silhouette compression;
- the boundary may be labeled as a connected-region threshold;
- the label SHALL NOT claim the adjacent region is unfinished, under development, unavailable for engineering reasons, or otherwise expose implementation status;
- no new region must become traversable in C3C3.

The user-facing interpretation is: `THE WORLD CONTINUES THERE; THIS REGION ENDS HERE.`

Restore strong foreground/midground/background separation without discarding the atmospheric gain.

Required mechanisms may include:

- directional lighting tied to world-space solar authority;
- terrain normal response and slope shading;
- distance-dependent contrast reduction;
- atmospheric perspective and aerial scattering;
- horizon haze distinct from local fog;
- silhouette separation between near ridges, intermediate terrain, and far continuation;
- material-value tuning that prevents large terrain masses from collapsing into one flat tonal plane.

Acceptance predicate:

`FOREGROUND_DISTINCT && MIDGROUND_DISTINCT && BACKGROUND_RECEDES && ATMOSPHERIC_GAIN_PRESERVED`
