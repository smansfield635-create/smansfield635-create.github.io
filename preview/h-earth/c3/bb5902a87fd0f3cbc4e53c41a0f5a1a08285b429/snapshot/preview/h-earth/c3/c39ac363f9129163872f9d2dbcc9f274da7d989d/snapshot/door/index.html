Practical Diagnostic Lens

Pressure is not the same as failure.

One of the bridge’s clearest practical uses is triage under constraint: helping distinguish visible stress from structural collapse risk. The goal is not to predict everything. The goal is to classify the present condition more responsibly.

  • Stable The system still has enough usable capacity to keep functioning.
  • Strained Pressure is visible, but the evidence does not justify calling it collapse.
  • Constrained Capacity is being consumed faster than the system can comfortably absorb, adapt, recover, or control.
  • Evidence-Incomplete The required inputs are missing, corrupt, or non-admissible, so the claim must stop short.
  • Collapse-Qualified Pressure, burden, capacity loss, weakest-axis failure, and admissible evidence meet the declared threshold.
