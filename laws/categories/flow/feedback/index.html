Feedback

What returned from the result, and how did it alter what happened next?

Practical reading

Show how an outcome changes a later action, setting, allocation, or behavior.

Engineering reading

Identify the feedback path, controller, delay, gain, saturation, and potential for oscillation or runaway response.

Empirical reading

Show whether a return signal and subsequent state change were actually observed, rather than inferred from sequence alone.

Claim boundary

Where the interpretation stops

Temporal return does not prove a functioning feedback mechanism unless the return path and resulting change are observed.
