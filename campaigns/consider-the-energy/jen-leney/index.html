6 Stones: service made operational.

The 6 Stones board selected Jennifer Leney as Executive Director in February 2019. Her leadership carries forward an organization built to mobilize community around practical needs rather than asking one person or institution to carry them alone.

Community Powered Revitalization.

In April 2025, 6 Stones completed work on the 900th home in the history of Community Powered Revitalization. That Spring Blitz mobilized 765 volunteers across 24 homes.

A wider field of care.

Home for Hope, Operation Back 2 School, food assistance, home repair, and volunteer mobilization express a broader commitment to helping people move from surviving toward thriving.

Coalition as community infrastructure.

Jen’s work at 6 Stones is built around coalition: churches, businesses, cities, nonprofits, schools, volunteers, donors, and neighbors each carry different forms of capacity, and community mobilization makes those capacities available to one another.
