RMA2 executes the only combination permitted by the RMA0 fallback law. It combines the top two RMA1 single-family impact results without adding a third family or changing any neutralization parameter.

Pass G, pass H, and the combined diagnostic pass are rendered across the fixed eight-scene suite. The combined pass is evaluated using the same frozen causal gate applied in RMA1.

The checkpoint closes whether the combination passes or fails the causal gate, provided execution integrity passes. Its mechanical classification is either:

- `BOUNDED_COMBINATION_REPETITION_CAUSAL`, or
- `NO_SINGLE_OR_BOUNDED_COMBINATION_PASSES_CAUSAL_GATE`.

RMA2 does not authorize a product candidate. RMA3 consumes the classification and issues the final useful-cue-retention or perceptual-comparison research disposition.
