How we count

The reporting order is total activity → Cloudflare-classified automation → owner/development activity → remaining external non-bot activity.

Historical owner activity before September 21, 2026 cannot be deterministically identified and is treated conservatively. From the qualified measurement boundary forward, three registered development devices are measured independently. Cloudflare visits are not represented as unique people.

Current measurement boundary

Cloudflare independently qualified heartbeat paths for the Galaxy S26 and two Galaxy Tab A9 development devices. This allows future reports to separate development activity from the remaining non-bot population instead of estimating it from browser characteristics.
