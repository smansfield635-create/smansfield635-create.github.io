## Controlled-comparison law

Executable comparison must preserve distinct identities for method, model, comparator, instrument, evidence, result, standing and provenance.

`METHOD != MODEL`

`MODEL != RESULT`

`INSTRUMENT != MODEL`

`COMPARATOR != INSTRUMENT`

`EXECUTION != EVIDENCE_STANDING`

A controlled transformation may expose how models behave under the same declared method or how methods process the same declared object, but comparison mechanics may not silently alter object identity, source state, evidence standing or claim ceiling.
