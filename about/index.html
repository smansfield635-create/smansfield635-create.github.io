The human layer

The work is ultimately about people.

Diamond Gate Bridge is a community effort because coherence is not only a systems problem. People live inside the systems they build. Coherence-oriented tools are explored here as aids for reflection—not diagnoses, truth machines or substitutes for human judgment.

Builders, reviewers, researchers and users strengthen the project when they can challenge the same evidence together. Better self-observation can support healthier relationships with ourselves and create better conditions for relationships with others. That is a direction for inquiry and practice, not a claim of proven causality.
