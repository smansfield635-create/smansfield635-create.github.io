Evidence

Shared premise

Evidence moves a claim beyond private insistence by giving another reader an observable, attributable, and checkable record.

  1. Observation

    Story focus. Observation identifies the event, state, or measurement that was actually encountered before interpretation is added.

    Practical

    Lens frame. Use evidence to replace trust in the claimant with something another person can inspect, question, and compare.

    What this selection adds. Separate what was seen or measured from what it is believed to mean. A clear observation lets disagreement begin from the same event.

    Engineering

    Lens frame. Represent evidence with observation identity, collection method, sampling context, provenance, transformation history, and an independent inspection path.

    What this selection adds. Capture the observation with timestamp, unit, sampling point, collection method, and raw-versus-derived status so later processing remains visible.

    Empirical

    Lens frame. Evidence standing depends on what was observed, how records were collected, which cases were included, and whether the record can support the claimed inference.

    What this selection adds. Admit the raw or minimally transformed record together with sampling context and missing observations. Interpretation alone is not an observation record.

  2. Record

    Story focus. Record turns an observation into a recoverable object that can survive time, transfer, and review without depending on the original witness.

    Practical

    Lens frame. Use evidence to replace trust in the claimant with something another person can inspect, question, and compare.

    What this selection adds. Keep enough detail that another reader can understand what happened after the moment has passed. A conclusion without its record cannot be re-examined.

    Engineering

    Lens frame. Represent evidence with observation identity, collection method, sampling context, provenance, transformation history, and an independent inspection path.

    What this selection adds. Assign stable identity, timestamp, schema, source link, version, and custody fields. Record transformations rather than overwriting the originating observation.

    Empirical

    Lens frame. Evidence standing depends on what was observed, how records were collected, which cases were included, and whether the record can support the claimed inference.

    What this selection adds. Require inspectable logs, measurements, notes, or source artifacts with exclusions and missingness retained. A copied claim is not a new evidence record.

  3. Provenance

    Story focus. Provenance explains where the record came from, who or what produced it, and which transformations separate it from the originating event.

    Practical

    Lens frame. Use evidence to replace trust in the claimant with something another person can inspect, question, and compare.

    What this selection adds. Ask how the record reached the page and what could have changed along the way. Reputation may guide attention, but it cannot replace recoverable lineage.

    Engineering

    Lens frame. Represent evidence with observation identity, collection method, sampling context, provenance, transformation history, and an independent inspection path.

    What this selection adds. Preserve source identity, owner, collection system, transformation chain, version, and custody transitions so substitutions and stale records remain detectable.

    Empirical

    Lens frame. Evidence standing depends on what was observed, how records were collected, which cases were included, and whether the record can support the claimed inference.

    What this selection adds. Evidence without provenance provides weak support for durable inference because selection, transformation, and source dependence cannot be evaluated.

  4. Checkability

    Story focus. Checkability gives someone other than the original claimant a practical route to inspect the record and challenge the interpretation.

    Practical

    Lens frame. Use evidence to replace trust in the claimant with something another person can inspect, question, and compare.

    What this selection adds. Provide the record, method, and boundary needed for another reader to ask whether the conclusion follows. Explanation can organize evidence but cannot replace access to it.

    Engineering

    Lens frame. Represent evidence with observation identity, collection method, sampling context, provenance, transformation history, and an independent inspection path.

    What this selection adds. Expose stable source links, reproducible transformations, validation rules, and failure messages instead of requiring private context or undocumented tools.

    Empirical

    Lens frame. Evidence standing depends on what was observed, how records were collected, which cases were included, and whether the record can support the claimed inference.

    What this selection adds. A checkable result retains the observations, metadata, method, and comparison path needed to reproduce or falsify the reported inference.

    Claim boundary. Evidence without provenance is weak support for durable inference.
