Consistency

  1. Alignment across time

    Practical
    Consistency means the system does not rewrite its standard to justify the latest behavior. It should reduce exceptions, explanations, and contradictions across contexts. Same claim, same standard.
    Engineering
    In engineering terms, Consistency maps to deterministic behavior, stable classification, repeatable outputs, bounded variance, and preserved constraints under comparable inputs. Identical conditions should produce identical outcomes.
    Empirical
    For Consistency requires repeated observations across time and context. A single good result is not enough. Strong evidence: repeated same-class outcomes under same-class conditions.
