Plane 01 · Identity

Outgoing obligation becomes a bounded protocol unit.

ARCP / Overview

Domain role

ARCP identifies the Payable domain inside the four-asset architecture. It is associated with what must leave, how an obligation moves toward execution, and how settlement is constrained.

Asset identity

Name: ARCHCOIN Payable. Symbol: ARCP. Standard: ERC-20 on the Ethereum EVM family, using 18 decimal places.

Not launched

ARCP remains prospective. Local implementation and transfer qualification do not establish a public launch. No public contract address or deployment transaction is announced for this token.

Plane 02 · Construction

Transferable behavior without an administrative back door.

ARCP / Engineering

Implementation

ARCP is instantiated by the shared Solidity 0.8.28 ArchcoinFixedSupply.sol contract, built on OpenZeppelin Contracts 5.4.0 ERC20.

Fixed-supply behavior

The constructor mints once to a nonzero recipient. No callable mint, public burn, ownership role, pause switch, or privileged mutation function survives deployment.

Qualification

Hardhat 2.28.0 and Toolbox 6.1.0 compile and test ARCP. Local EVM qualification deploys the contract and signs a transfer while proving total supply remains fixed.

Supply boundary

The 1,000,000-unit test fixture is engineering evidence, not an authorized issuance decision. Network supply and recipient wallet remain unfrozen.

Plane 03 · Traversal

Local settlement behavior precedes public-network evidence.

ARCP / Platform

Current surface

The repository contains ARCP’s shared source, compiler setup, tests, local deployment, and signed-transfer qualification. That establishes a working local implementation.

Before a public launch

A public launch requires a separately qualified release, confirmed network and supply, a recipient, and explicit deployment authorization. No launch date is announced.

Evidence after deployment

The platform will expose a contract address and transaction evidence only after on-chain bytecode and ERC-20 behavior can be independently retrieved and checked.

Launch status

ARCP is not deployed on Ethereum mainnet. ARCC and ARCR have launched; their deployments do not establish a launch for ARCP.

Plane 04 · Constraint

A token name cannot authorize a payment.

ARCP / Governance

Immutable token constraints

  • No post-deployment mint.
  • No public burn in version one.
  • No privileged token mutation.
  • Public launch requires separate release qualification and authorization.

Holder-rights boundary

ARCP creates no legal payment obligation, settlement guarantee, redemption right, creditor status, equity position, governance power, or ownership claim.

Public status

ARCP is a prospective token with a locally qualified implementation. It is not a launched cryptocurrency. Any future launch will need its own verifiable contract address and transaction record.
