THE STORY BEHIND THE WORK

Pressure.
Reflection.
A change in direction.

The turning point was a decision to examine where my time, energy, choices and relationships were leading—and to change direction.

China became a catalyst. Chinese philosophy gave me a simpler language for ideas I had struggled to express, while Christian teaching and other philosophical traditions remained part of the frame.

That reflection became Consider the Energy. The Inner Underdog developed as I explored why underdog stories feel so universally human. The work continued through 256 and The Nine Summits of Love.

Today, that same curiosity also finds expression in interactive worlds, browser experiences and AI-assisted building. The questions keep turning into things people can explore.

A little more about the philosophy

Responsibility begins with what can still be changed. It does not mean every pressure was self-created. Dignity means recognizing your own value while still recognizing the value of others.

I want the conversation to illuminate: to give people room to examine an idea and reach their own conclusions.
