Scientific Law / Reality Test

The Reality branch asks whether theory, evidence, measure, and limits remain separate. This page uses Scientific Law as an expanded test branch, not as one of the sixteen law stars.

Source Matching

Water-quality analysis begins with a source anchor: city and state, utility name, Consumer Confidence Report, lab result, or public database page. A sourced contaminant record is not the same as an unsourced general water claim.

No-Match Discipline

The SOH resolver includes explicit no-match reasons rather than forcing evidence into an applicable slot. Missing cycle data, exceeded cycle limits, model mismatch, or capacity-basis mismatch prevent consumption of the warranty floor.

Threshold Distinction

EPA legal compliance, EWG health-based guideline concern, household exposure-reduction posture, and validated harm are different claim categories and must not be collapsed.

Multi-Gate Verification

Industrial water treatment requires bulk organic reduction, target analyte reduction, transformation product limits, residual oxidant control, and contaminant-specific byproduct monitoring.

Diagnostic Boundary

Diagnostic framing is not clinical validation. Health-risk education, contaminant classification, and exposure mechanism language must not diagnose the reader or imply clinical proof.

Evidence Boundary

The project distinguishes evidence, validation, candidate output, active bounds, and production status. A source may support a candidate, a fixture may test contract behavior, and a resolver may support comparison, but none of these automatically creates a final empirical validation claim.
