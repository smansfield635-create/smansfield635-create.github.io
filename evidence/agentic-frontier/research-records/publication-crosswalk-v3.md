**MORE CHECKPOINTS != BETTER WORK.**  
**THEY HAVE TO EARN THEIR BURDEN.**

### In real life

A hospital, factory, or financial process can add layers of review. Those layers are useful only if they prevent enough meaningful mistakes to justify the delay, complexity, and cost they introduce.
