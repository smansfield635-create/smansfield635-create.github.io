# H-EARTH AUTONOMOUS VISUAL QUALIFICATION TRACK v1

MODE: BOUNDED AUTONOMOUS VISUAL DEVELOPMENT
STATUS: FROZEN EXECUTION CONTRACT
MAX QUALIFIED VISUAL CANDIDATES: 6

## Purpose

Advance visible H-Earth terrain depth without requiring interval authorization after every candidate.

## Frozen authorities

The following are held constant throughout this track unless the track terminates and a new contract is explicitly authorized:

- public route: `/showroom/globe/h-earth/`
- camera authority
- compositor authority
- controls / navigation authority
- coastline and admitted morphology
- protected public renderer architecture
- source geographic identity
- deployment authority

No candidate produced by this track may deploy automatically.

## Qualified baseline

Use the latest qualified public-corridor baseline established by the exact-SHA A/B harness. The procedural painted-surface treatment is diagnosed as visually harmful; removal of that treatment alone is insufficient because underlying large-form depth remains weak.

## Search surface

Candidates may mutate only the minimum upstream surfaces needed to test:

1. terrain mesh sampling density and distribution;
2. large-form terrain geometry representation;
3. geometric vertex/face normal construction and continuity;
4. mountain/ravine/cliff normal variation;
5. bounded presentation adjustments required to expose accepted geometry.

Do not reopen camera/compositor hypotheses. Do not restore heavy contour, strata, rake, or painted face-band presentation as a substitute for geometry.

## Candidate loop

For each candidate:

1. state one falsifiable visual hypothesis;
2. make one bounded implementation;
3. require syntax/shader/build validity;
4. execute exact candidate SHA through the qualified public corridor;
5. require actual route readiness;
6. capture physical screenshot evidence;
7. compare against qualified baseline and best-so-far;
8. record disposition: ADVANCE, REJECT, or TERMINATE.

An unambiguous construction defect may be repaired and rerun without consuming a visual candidate slot. A candidate consumes a slot only after it reaches a qualified rendered state.

## Advancement criteria

A candidate may become best-so-far only when evidence shows improvement in large-form terrain readability while preserving:

- coastline / morphology;
- route readiness;
- interaction corridor;
- absence of initialization/browser/shader failures;
- absence of dominant painted/cartoon banding.

Primary visual dimensions:

- mountain face separation;
- ridge / valley readability;
- near-to-mid depth;
- geological form continuity;
- foreground dimensionality;
- atmospheric distance hierarchy.

## Stop boundary

Stop immediately when either:

A. a candidate produces a substantial, physically visible improvement in large-form depth without a material regression in the preservation criteria; or
B. six qualified visual candidates have been evaluated.

At boundary B, return the best-so-far candidate, all candidate dispositions, and the remaining diagnosed bottleneck. Do not continue searching.

## Promotion law

No candidate merges into production merely because it wins this track. The best candidate is returned for owner visual approval.

## Execution discipline

- no interval approval requests inside the six-candidate boundary;
- no unrelated refactors;
- no deployment;
- no renderer-architecture replacement;
- no expansion of scope after a failed theory;
- evidence before conclusion.
