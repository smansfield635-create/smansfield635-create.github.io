The Secret of Life Edition

Featured collection

Three shirts. One edition.

The Secret of Life Edition turns a repeated language—live, love, listen, laugh—into three physical reminders. They belong together because each one asks the same question from a different direction: how do you carry an idea into the way you live?

Beyond the garment

The shirt is the reminder. The idea is the environment.

Consider the Energy has always been larger than apparel. The graphics, phrases, and decision frameworks around the shirts show the thought-world the products came from.

That is why this page is not only a rack of designs. The catalog moves between what can be worn and what can be considered.

Broader catalog

The edition is the beginning, not the whole apparel language.

Outside the three-shirt edition, other designs carry distinct Consider the Energy ideas. They remain separate from the trilogy while belonging to the same broader catalog.

Choice and possibility

A ceiling matters only when a decision meets it.

Risk is not a promise of reward. It is the acknowledgement that greater possibility can demand a meaningful choice.

Direction

What looks like care can still move in the wrong direction.

Fear can be disguised as love, but love can never be disguised as fear.

The shirt carries one of the recurring Consider the Energy distinctions into public space: look past the label and consider what the action actually creates, protects, diminishes, or destroys.

Recognition

Name the pattern before it becomes the environment.

The manipulation shirt turns a conceptual warning into something immediately visible: using another person's mind rather than engaging their agency changes the quality of the interaction.

Purchasing is active for all three Secret of Life shirts through Fourthwall.
