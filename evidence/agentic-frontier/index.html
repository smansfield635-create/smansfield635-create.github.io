03 · Governed Agency

Agency is useful only inside a legible boundary.

The governing topology separates intent, permission, execution, fresh verification, adoption, and publication. A supervising hook can change future decision input; it cannot silently become mutation authority.

Parallel agents, orchestrators, checkpoints, scoped permissions, provenance, and fail-closed behavior are established mechanisms. The research contribution under test is the integrated lifecycle and its behavior across heterogeneous work.

04 · Maturity

What is mature—and what is still research?

Maturity and evidence strength are kept distinct. The operating system can be demonstrated in real production while the stronger scientific claim remains below protocol or frontier standing.

A successful local production history can strengthen synthesis evidence. It cannot substitute for hostile conformance, independent implementation, or external reproduction.
