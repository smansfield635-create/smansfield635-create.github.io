# 1. Corrected controlling proposition

The Research Frontier successor must distinguish **information architecture** from **interaction architecture**.

The v2 3×3 answers:

> What intellectual material belongs in the Research Frontier, and how is it related?

The restored six-card carousel answers:

> How does a visitor move through that material as a product experience?

Those are different layers.

The controlling construction rule is:

> **The carousel is the protected product architecture. The 3×3 is a semantic source map. Meaning and Evidence are the two actual inspectable card faces.**

No later implementation may infer a nine-card grid, a three-chapter page, a long-form scrolling replacement, or accordion-first disclosure merely from the existence of the 3×3 content model.

# 4. Protected baseline interaction floor

The exact restored baseline is blob:

`ecdecab46a263c2200a4deedfa83c9aa347c7e46`

That baseline establishes the minimum successor interaction floor. A later successor may refine implementation details, styling, rendering quality, or semantic labels, but it may not remove the following behaviors without a separately governed explicit owner supersession:

## 4.1 Orbit

- exactly six selectable research objects exist in one orbital carousel;
- a front/current object is distinguishable from the other five;
- horizontal pointer drag rotates the carousel;
- horizontal touch swipe rotates the carousel;
- keyboard Left / Right rotates the carousel;
- the selected object remains identifiable while the orbit moves;
- the carousel does not degrade into a six-tile grid as the primary desktop or mobile experience.

## 4.2 Open-card inspection

- opening the selected card suspends orbit interaction;
- the selected card identity is retained;
- the opened experience is visually and behaviorally distinct from the orbit state;
- vertical gesture / scrolling reads the currently visible face;
- the opened card is not represented merely as an accordion expanded inside a scrolling page.

## 4.3 Two-sided interaction

The current restored baseline uses Scientific ↔ Agentic faces. The successor changes the **semantic labels** of those two faces to:

- `MEANING`
- `EVIDENCE`

The two-sided interaction itself remains protected.

Required successor behavior:

- every one of the six cards has both faces;
- the Meaning face is the default human-facing face unless an exact retained user state lawfully selects otherwise;
- horizontal gesture while a card is open changes Meaning ↔ Evidence;
- an explicit accessible control provides the same face change for keyboard and assistive-technology users;
- the face change is a genuine state transition between two full card surfaces, not a show/hide details expansion within one long document;
- vertical reading on either face does not accidentally rotate the orbit or flip the card;
- face identity is always programmatically determinable.

## 4.4 Return

Closing the opened card must return the visitor to the same selected orbit object. A successor may retain the exact rotational angle or an equivalent deterministic selected-object state, but it may not reset the visitor to card 1 merely because inspection ended.

## 4.5 Shared bridge

The shared bridge remains separately inspectable from an opened card and remains distinct from either card face.

It may not become:

- a seventh carousel card;
- a replacement for Evidence;
- an ambient claim that scientific and agentic results validate one another;
- a generic footer used to merge evidence classes.

Its causal boundary remains explicit.

# 7. Face contract

## 7.1 Meaning face

The Meaning face is the human-facing front of the selected card.

Its job is to answer:

- What is this?
- Why should I care?
- What is unusual here?
- What remains unresolved?

The Meaning face must use the approved v2 Meaning source text and Meaning-side composition contract as its semantic authority. It may compress, stage, or typographically decompose that source material only if the resulting copy remains within the exact source meaning and claim ceiling.

The first visible composition should prefer contrast over block prose.

Examples of already approved contrast grammar include:

- G11: **IT STILL LOOKS HEALTHY. / BUT ITS ROOM TO RECOVER IS SHRINKING.**
- G12: **“COHERENT” IS EASY TO SAY. / SCIENCE NEEDS SOMETHING IT CAN TEST.**
- G21: **THIS APPEARS CORRECT ≠ AUTHORIZED TO CHANGE THE RECORD.**
- G22: **BUILD IT. / THEN LET SOMETHING ELSE TRY TO BREAK IT.**
- G23: **MORE SAFEGUARDS ≠ BETTER RESEARCH. / THEY HAVE TO EARN THEIR COST.**
- G31: **ONE OPERATING PROGRAM. MANY KINDS OF WORK. / CONTEXT, NOT PROOF.**
- G32: **THE MACHINERY IS SHARED. / THE EVIDENCE IS NOT.**
- G33: **WHAT HAPPENS WHEN THE ORIGINAL OPERATOR ISN’T THERE? / CAN THE WORK STILL REMEMBER ITSELF?**

For combined cards, those component contrasts may be editorially sequenced on one Meaning face. They must not be flattened into a dense paragraph merely to make the pairing fit.

The Meaning face must not lead with dense metrics, internal protocol identifiers, long source inventories, or implementation metadata.

## 7.2 Evidence face

The Evidence face is the technical back of the selected card.

It must expose enough exact technical material for a skeptical reader to inspect the claim without turning the Meaning face into an engineering document.

The Evidence face may contain:

- exact evidence status;
- domain tags;
- metrics and formal counts;
- configuration details;
- negative / null / not-identifiable records;
- novelty status;
- provenance status;
- claim ceiling;
- prior-art boundary;
- prospective discriminating boundary;
- canonical source references.

Evidence, novelty, and provenance remain visually and semantically distinguishable.

The Evidence face must not be an expandable footnote beneath the Meaning face. It is the second primary surface of the card.

# 8. Evidence-face inspection lenses

The six existing Research Frontier modes remain valid, but they are no longer required to occupy the six carousel positions in the successor.

They are frozen as subordinate **Evidence-face inspection lenses**:

1. `Strongest Attack`
2. `Evidence Held`
3. `Negative Result`
4. `Missing Evidence`
5. `Reproduction Boundary`
6. `Next Boundary`

A later implementation may expose only lenses that materially apply to the selected card, or expose all six with unavailable/non-applicable states where that improves consistency. It must not fabricate content merely to populate every lens.

Lens interaction is subordinate to Evidence:

`EVIDENCE_FACE -> OPTIONAL_LENS -> EVIDENCE_FACE`

A lens may use tabs, segmented controls, nested panels, or another accessible technical-inspection device because it is **inside the Evidence face**. The prohibition on accordion substitution applies to replacing the two primary Meaning/Evidence faces, not to legitimate technical organization inside Evidence.

Negative evidence and missing evidence must remain first-class when present.

# 9. Interaction state machine

The successor state model is conceptually:

`ORBIT`

`CARD_OPEN_MEANING`

`CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE`

`EVIDENCE_LENS`

`SHARED_BRIDGE`

The exact implementation may use different internal state names, but observable behavior must remain equivalent.

## 9.1 Required transitions

`ORBIT + horizontal swipe/drag -> ORBIT / different selected card`

`ORBIT + Left/Right -> ORBIT / different selected card`

`ORBIT + open selected card -> CARD_OPEN_MEANING`

`CARD_OPEN_MEANING + horizontal face gesture/control -> CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE`

`CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE + horizontal face gesture/control -> CARD_OPEN_MEANING`

`CARD_OPEN_MEANING + vertical gesture -> CARD_OPEN_MEANING / scroll`

`CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE + vertical gesture -> CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE / scroll`

`CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE + open lens -> EVIDENCE_LENS`

`EVIDENCE_LENS + close lens -> CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE`

`CARD_OPEN_MEANING|CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE + open bridge -> SHARED_BRIDGE`

`SHARED_BRIDGE + close -> prior selected card / prior face`

`CARD_OPEN_MEANING|CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE + return -> ORBIT / same selected card`

## 9.2 Gesture arbitration

Horizontal versus vertical intent must remain discriminated so that:

- ordinary vertical reading does not flip the card;
- ordinary vertical reading does not rotate the carousel;
- horizontal orbit manipulation does not trigger card opening accidentally;
- horizontal opened-card face changes do not rotate the background orbit.

The baseline implementation's direction-lock behavior is valid precedent.

# 10. Keyboard, touch, focus, and accessibility

The protected experience is multimodal.

## Orbit

- Left / Right rotates;
- the selected/front card is the keyboard focus target or has an equivalent clear focus model;
- Enter / Space or an explicit control opens the selected card;
- focus indication remains visible.

## Open card

- focus moves into the opened card without losing object identity;
- an explicit control changes Meaning ↔ Evidence;
- swipe may perform the same transition on touch/pointer devices;
- Escape or explicit Return returns to orbit;
- focus returns to the selected orbit object;
- focus is trapped within modal/card inspection when the implementation remains modal.

## Evidence lenses

- lens controls must be keyboard operable;
- selected lens state must be programmatically exposed;
- lens content must not hide required claim ceilings behind pointer-only interaction.

## Shared bridge

- bridge open/close is keyboard operable;
- closing restores the prior card and face state.

Accessible semantics are required in addition to the visual two-face effect. Accessibility does not authorize replacing the two-face product interaction with a one-page accordion.

# 11. Responsive and mobile law

The carousel remains a carousel on narrow screens.

Responsive design may change:

- card dimensions;
- orbit radius/depth;
- typography scale;
- surrounding negative space;
- number of partially visible neighboring cards;
- control placement;
- gesture thresholds.

Responsive design may not change the primary product into:

- a vertical list of six cards;
- a 2×3 or 3×2 grid;
- a nine-object grid;
- three stacked chapters;
- an accordion document;
- a linear long-form successor where swipe/rotation is no longer the principal object-selection behavior.

If spatial pressure requires simplification, **visual density collapses before interaction identity**.

---

# 12. Reduced-motion and fallback law

Reduced motion changes animation intensity, not the information architecture or state machine.

With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`:

- six carousel objects still exist;
- selection still changes through the carousel control model;
- Meaning and Evidence remain distinct faces/states;
- the face-change control remains available;
- return-to-orbit state remains preserved;
- bridge and Evidence lenses remain available.

A no-JavaScript or catastrophic-runtime fallback may expose a linear readable representation for basic access. That fallback is not the primary product architecture and must not be used as evidence that a live successor may replace the carousel with a linear document.

# 14. Six-card content obligations

A later live construction must preserve the following card-level source boundaries.

## Card 01 — Seeing fragility before failure

Source: `G11` only.

Meaning centers on hidden loss of future options before visible failure.

Evidence centers on the bounded RTS24 predictive result plus the later NOT_IDENTIFIABLE matched-state boundary, without treating the latter as a refutation.

## Card 02 — Making coherence precise

Source: `G12` only.

Meaning centers on turning intuitive coherence language into formal objects.

Evidence centers on the bounded formal theorem, Diamond Operator checks, formal counterexample, and unresolved orientation/priority boundaries.

## Card 03 — Testing the machinery doing the research

Sources: `G13 + G23`.

Meaning must distinguish:

1. a bounded existing comparison showing a configuration difference; and
2. a prospective question about whether additional research safeguards measurably earn their cost.

Evidence must not present the prospective process-ablation question as already answered.

## Card 04 — Reasoning is not authority

Sources: `G21 + G22`.

Meaning centers on two linked but distinct principles:

- correct-seeming reasoning is not mutation authority;
- construction should not be its sole grader.

Evidence may expose exact-state admission, verification, role separation, and operational custody as implemented method. It may not claim that those mechanisms have already been shown to improve scientific quality unless separately measured.

## Card 05 — One independent architecture across different kinds of work

Sources: `G31 + G32`.

Meaning must keep both boundaries visible:

- independent/single-program operating context is context, not proof;
- shared research machinery across domains is architectural breadth, not cross-domain scientific validation.

Evidence may expose provenance and operating-method architecture. It must not merge them into a scientific evidence claim.

## Card 06 — Can it survive another operator?

Source: `G33` only.

Meaning centers on whether the work can preserve exact state, uncertainty, negative results, and continuation boundaries beyond the original operator.

Evidence may show internal succession mechanisms and bounded recovery examples. External operator/organizational transfer remains unresolved until actually demonstrated.
