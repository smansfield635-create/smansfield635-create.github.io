Practical diagnostic lens

Pressure is not the same as failure.

The Door preserves its bounded triage language while the carousel is reconstructed.

  • Stable — usable capacity remains sufficient.
  • Strained — pressure is visible without a collapse claim.
  • Constrained — capacity is being consumed faster than comfortable absorption.
  • Evidence-Incomplete — required inputs are missing or non-admissible.
  • Collapse-Qualified — declared threshold conditions are satisfied.
