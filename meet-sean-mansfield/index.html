THE STORY BEHIND THE WORK

Pressure.
Reflection.
A change in direction.

Living in China gave me a reason to question the direction I was taking. I began looking more closely at my choices, my relationships and where I was putting my energy.

Chinese philosophy gave me a simpler language for ideas I had struggled to express. My Christian faith remained part of that reflection, alongside ideas I encountered in other philosophical traditions.

That reflection became Consider the Energy. The Inner Underdog grew from another question: why do we recognize ourselves so readily in someone who has been counted out? Those questions continued into my writing, including The Nine Summits of Love.

Today, I also build interactive worlds and browser experiences with AI. Starting without a coding background has given me another way to explore the same question: what can you learn to do when you are willing to begin?

A little more about the philosophy

Responsibility begins with what can still be changed. It does not mean every pressure was self-created. Dignity means recognizing your own value while still recognizing the value of others.

I want people to leave with something they can examine in their own lives, and room to reach their own conclusions.
