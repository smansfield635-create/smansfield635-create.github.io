## Pillar 1 — Learning System
Existing source: /products/education/

Use the existing 1,001-concept English Fluency Accelerator as the human-understandable learning case.

Verified implementation characteristics:
- bounded 1,001-concept foundation;
- productive patterns rather than vocabulary memorization alone;
- adaptive placement;
- performance-gated progression;
- learner self-claim selects the starting probe but does not determine final placement;
- difficulty moves upward/downward based on demonstrated performance;
- placement evidence records stage, difficulty, item, node, competency, evidence class, correctness, and freshness;
- sustainable/unsustainable boundaries are tracked;
- mixed evidence can terminate as UNCERTAIN_BOUNDARY rather than fabricated certainty;

Evidence boundary:

### B. Coherence Diagnostic
Existing source: /coherence-diagnostic/

Verified current structure:
- Stage 1: self-rated coherence;
- Stage 2: claimed primary and support decision patterns;
- Stage 3: ten randomized pressure scenarios;
- first move weighted ×2 and support move ×1;
- six displayed subscales: Sequence Integrity, Evidence Discipline, Action Realism, Field Stability, Correction Capacity, Contradiction Resistance;
- calibration compares self-rating with revealed scenario score;
- claim alignment compares claimed archetype blend with revealed blend;
- practical and technical result faces;
- local-browser operation with no account requirement, no API call, no emailed answers, and no result storage in the described version.

Hard claim boundary:
This is a self-reflection and decision-pattern instrument. It is NOT a medical, mental-health, legal, employment, intelligence, or official personality-type diagnostic. Do not present it as validated psychometrics.

### C. Estate Laboratory / Gauges
