## Primary question

Can independent observers recover constitutive relations, system boundaries, and identity-preserving route structure in systems they did not design, then predict withheld disruption, support, substitution, fracture, and restoration outcomes better than preregistered conventional alternatives?

## Evidence class

A primary result requires all of the following:

- at least four admissible externally authored systems;
- at least two materially different domains;
- at least two independent system-author teams;
- at least two independent observer teams;
- hidden truth and held-out outcomes controlled by a separate custodian;
- sparse or noisy observations in at least half of the systems;
- matched-output conditions with different internal provenance;
- frozen predictions before unblinding;
- identical data access for the theory model and all baselines.

Systems authored by the theory team may be used only for platform conformance. They cannot contribute to the primary scientific result.
