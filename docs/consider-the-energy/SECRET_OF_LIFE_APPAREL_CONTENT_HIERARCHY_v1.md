## 1. Governing content principle

The apparel environment is not a generic store grid.

The governing sequence is:

`IDEA -> COLLECTION -> OBJECT -> EDITORIAL CONTEXT -> BROADER APPAREL -> CONNECTED PHILOSOPHY -> FUTURE COMMERCE`

Commerce is deliberately last and is not part of this construction cut.

The Secret of Life Edition must read first as a coherent authored environment and second as a catalog.

## 2. Surface A hierarchy — Community Object

Surface A:

`campaigns/consider-the-energy/index.html`

The existing four-face Community Object hierarchy is preserved.

No content hierarchy is being rewritten on Surface A.

Within the existing Brand face, the apparel subsection remains a three-shirt featured expression.

The frozen apparel order on Surface A is:

1. Learn to Love to Listen
2. Learn to Live to Laugh
3. Learn to Live to Love

The third position is the only shirt-content substitution authorized on Surface A.

Higher the Risk / Higher the Ceiling leaves this three-shirt position but remains part of the wider Consider the Energy apparel universe on Surface B.

No additional apparel, editorial graphics, store controls, or catalog groups are added to Surface A.

## 3. Surface B hierarchy — Secret of Life Edition

Surface B:

`campaigns/consider-the-energy/secret-of-life-edition/index.html`

Surface B owns the substantive apparel/editorial experience.

Its content hierarchy is frozen in the following order.

### Level 1 — Hero identity

Primary title:

`The Secret of Life Edition`

Hero function:
- establish this page as the apparel/editorial expression environment for Consider the Energy;
- establish the Secret of Life Edition as the featured collection;
- avoid presenting the page as a checkout/storefront first;
- preserve connection to Consider the Energy and The Nine Summits of Love without making either the hero's dominant title.

The exact supporting hero prose remains a construction-phase writing task, but it must support this hierarchy and may not replace the frozen title.

### Level 2 — Featured collection

The Secret of Life trilogy is the first substantive content block after the hero.

Collection membership is frozen:

1. Learn to Live to Love
2. Learn to Love to Listen
3. Learn to Live to Laugh

These three shirts must be perceived as one deliberate collection, not as three unrelated catalog entries.

Collection function:
- introduce the repeated Learn / Live / Love / Listen / Laugh language family;
- make the trilogy visually dominant over the broader catalog;
- establish wearable expression as a consequence of the philosophy.

No general-catalog shirt may visually outrank the trilogy.

### Level 3 — Editorial transition

After the featured collection, the page shifts from one collection into the broader Consider the Energy idea system.

Editorial graphics may begin here.

Editorial-transition function:
- prevent the page from collapsing into a merchandise grid;
- reveal that the shirts emerge from a larger body of original ideas;
- create visual and conceptual pacing between apparel groups.

Editorial graphics are not a new catalog category and are not merchandise.

### Level 4 — Broader Consider the Energy apparel

The broader catalog follows the featured trilogy.

Frozen initial membership:

- Higher the Risk / Higher the Ceiling
- Fear / Love
- Manipulation

These items remain distinct from the Secret of Life trilogy.

They may be presented individually or in a broader apparel grouping, but must not be mislabeled as part of the Secret of Life trilogy.

Their function is to demonstrate that the apparel language can extend beyond one edition.

### Level 5 — Editorial interludes within the broader catalog

Approved editorial graphics may interrupt the broader apparel sequence when they strengthen the nearby idea.

Frozen editorial pool:

- Five D's of Deceit
- Gratitude vs. Entitlement
- Expectations Without Limitations
- 7 D's of Decision Making

The editorial pool is curated, not exhaustive.

The adjacency law is:

`EDITORIAL GRAPHIC MUST EXPLAIN, DEEPEN, CONTRAST, OR PREPARE THE IDEA NEAR IT`

It may not be inserted solely because visual space is available.

Strong semantic affinities already recognized:
- Five D's of Deceit may support the Manipulation / recognition-of-manipulation idea family;
- Expectations Without Limitations may support possibility, ceiling, growth, or risk-related material;
- 7 D's of Decision Making may support agency / discernment / choice transitions;
- Gratitude vs. Entitlement may support community, posture, value, or interpersonal-energy transitions.

These affinities guide construction but do not authorize rewriting the source graphics.

### Level 6 — Philosophy and lineage

After the apparel/editorial body, the page may explain where the ideas connect.

This layer may connect:
- Consider the Energy;
- The Nine Summits of Love;
- the Book route;
- the broader community.

Function:
- show lineage and relationship;
- give the visitor a path deeper into the ideas;
- avoid duplicating the Community Object's full philosophy;
- avoid turning the apparel page into a second Nine Summits page.

The page may link outward; it should not absorb sibling projects.

### Level 7 — Connected paths / exit layer

The final content layer may preserve clear onward routes:
- Consider the Energy Community;
- Nine Summits of Love;
- Book;
- other already-approved connected paths that remain relevant.

The exit layer comes after the apparel/editorial experience rather than interrupting the featured collection.

## 4. Narrative priority law

When visual space, mobile compression, or interaction constraints require prioritization, content priority is:

1. Hero identity
2. Secret of Life trilogy
3. Key editorial context
4. Broader apparel
5. Additional editorial interludes
6. Philosophy/lineage
7. Connected paths

The trilogy may not be demoted below the broader catalog.

Editorial graphics may be reduced in number before core apparel is removed.

Connected-path material may be compressed before apparel identity is weakened.

## 5. Merchandise versus editorial law

Every visual asset on Surface B must resolve to one of two semantic classes:

### Merchandise
A real approved shirt source intended to represent apparel.

Merchandise can:
- carry a title;
- carry a short interpretive caption;
- later gain commerce metadata in a separate operation.

### Editorial
An original graphic used to communicate an idea.

Editorial graphics can:
- establish context;
- create pacing;
- deepen nearby apparel meaning;
- act as a conceptual bridge.

Editorial graphics cannot:
- receive shirt/product labeling;
- receive price/size/cart affordances;
- be implied to be physically purchasable;
- occupy the same semantic card treatment as merchandise if that treatment would confuse the distinction.

## 6. Content-density law

The environment should feel curated, not exhaustive.

Therefore:
- not every available CTE graphic must appear;
- not every shirt must receive a long essay;
- repeated slogans must not be explained multiple times;
- visual pauses are allowed;
- major apparel objects should have enough breathing room to remain individually legible;
- editorial material should break monotony rather than create another wall of cards.

The page is an authored sequence, not an archive dump.

## 7. Content ownership law

Surface A owns:
- concise Community-facing brand expression;
- the three-shirt Secret of Life preview inside the Brand face.

Surface B owns:
- complete featured collection presentation;
- broader apparel catalog;
- editorial/environmental graphics;
- catalog-level explanation of apparel idea families;
- future commerce attachment points.

Nine Summits owns:
- its full philosophical/book journey.

Book route owns:
- its dedicated book entry identity.

R.O.B. and W.A.V.E. own:
- their campaign-specific material.

Surface B may connect to these sibling areas but may not duplicate their full content.

## 8. Existing tab-shell law

The current Secret of Life page uses the existing campaign tab system.

This hierarchy does not require a new interaction system.

Construction may:
- reuse and relabel existing tab sections;
- reorganize content inside the existing page shell;
- use ordinary in-page sections inside tabs where appropriate.

Construction may not:
- create a new carousel/tab controller;
- create a new app runtime;
- introduce commerce state;
- modify shared `campaigns/index.tabs.js` merely to realize this hierarchy.

If the frozen hierarchy cannot be expressed through existing page primitives, construction must stop and report the exact blocker before expanding runtime scope.

## 9. Copy law

This hierarchy freezes function and order, not every final sentence.

Final construction copy must:
- preserve the original CTE voice;
- distinguish collection identity from product captions;
- avoid generic e-commerce language;
- avoid inventing product availability, pricing, sizing, inventory, shipping, or ordering claims;
- avoid representing future commerce as currently operational;
- keep editorial graphics in their original authored wording.

Exact final copy remains subject to candidate review.

## 10. Mobile hierarchy law

On narrow/mobile presentation:
- hierarchy must remain the same even if composition changes;
- the Secret of Life trilogy remains the first major apparel experience;
- broader catalog follows;
- editorial interludes must not push core apparel so far apart that the catalog becomes difficult to understand;
- horizontal overflow must not be required to understand the hierarchy;
- no desktop-only visual ordering may reverse the semantic sequence on mobile.

## 11. Explicit exclusions

The content hierarchy does not include:
- checkout flow;
- Add to Cart;
- price;
- size selector;
- color selector;
- inventory;
- shipping;
- customer account;
- product reviews;
- recommendation engine;
- unrelated CTE graphics;
- unrelated personal imagery;
- arbitrary decorative stock imagery.

Those require separate authority or explicit later scope.

## 12. Candidate acceptance conditions created by this hierarchy

A future construction candidate passes the hierarchy check only if:

1. `The Secret of Life Edition` is the dominant hero identity.
2. The three-shirt trilogy is immediately recognizable as the featured collection.
3. Learn to Live to Love / Learn to Love to Listen / Learn to Live to Laugh remain grouped as one collection.
4. Higher the Risk / Higher the Ceiling is preserved, but outside the trilogy.
5. Fear / Love and Manipulation have clear broader-catalog homes.
6. Editorial graphics read as authored context, not fake merchandise.
7. The page has visual/conceptual rhythm rather than one uninterrupted product grid.
8. Sibling philosophy links deepen the experience without taking over the apparel page.
9. Surface A still reads as the existing Community Object with only the approved shirt substitution.
10. No commerce functionality is implied as live.
