## 4. PHASE-1 TARGET — MESO LANDSCAPE SYSTEM

Phase 1 treats the landscape as five representative terrain classes:

1. MOUNTAIN
2. FOOTHILL
3. VALLEY / DRAINAGE
4. PLAIN / LOWLAND
5. COAST / SHORE TRANSITION

The objective is not maximum geometric complexity. It is readable, connected landscape structure across all five classes under the accepted Adaptive-C observer scale.

## 5. REQUIRED MESO FEATURES

### Mountain
- ridge continuity
- secondary ridge hierarchy
- readable shoulders
- saddles/passes
- drainage separation
- no return to monolithic walls

### Foothill
- progressive descent from primary relief
- overlapping low ridges/benches
- slope breaks
- transition into lowland without abrupt mathematical termination

### Valley / Drainage
- coherent downhill corridor
- widening downstream tendency
- banks/side slopes readable at observer scale
- tributary or inter-spur relationship where appropriate
- no isolated negative Gaussian pits

### Plain / Lowland
- non-flat broad undulation
- shallow depressions and rises
- long-wavelength grade changes
- enough structure to provide depth without becoming mountainous

### Coast / Shore Transition
- inland-to-shore grade continuity
- berm/bank/shelf transition where geographically appropriate
- no giant planar shoreline facets
- terrain definition must connect naturally into existing water/coast authority

## 6. MULTISCALE ASSIGNMENT LAW

Phase 1 must distinguish representation responsibility:

MACRO FIELD:
continent/coast/range/basin placement.

MESO GEOMETRY:
ridges, foothills, terraces/benches, valleys, drainage corridors, banks, gullies and slope breaks.

LOCAL GEOMETRY — HELD FOR LATER PHASE:
small rocks, small erosion cuts, debris-scale relief and fine ground breakup.

SURFACE DETAIL — HELD FOR LATER PHASE:
normal/roughness/albedo/sediment microdetail and small displacement.

SECONDARY GEOMETRY / ECOLOGY — HELD FOR LATER PHASE:
rocks/boulders/vegetation density systems.

Phase 1 must not force local/surface/ecology detail into the meso terrain field.

## 8. REPRESENTATIVE WITNESS REGIONS

The audit must bind deterministic witness coordinates/regions for:
- one mountain approach/traverse
- one foothill transition
- one valley/drainage corridor
- one lowland/plain traversal
- one coast/shore transition

Witnesses must be derived from current repository geography/terrain evidence, not invented solely to make tests pass.

## 9. MEASUREMENT

At each witness region record, where applicable:
- elevation range
- slope distribution
- curvature proxy
- local relief over 8/16/32-unit windows
- drainage/valley signal
- ridge/foothill signal
- Adaptive-C sample spacing
- triangle/facet dimensions
- visible-terrain clearance correspondence

No universal AAA numeric threshold is assumed. Measurements establish H-Earth's own baseline and successor deltas.

## 10. CONSTRUCTION LAW

No Phase-1 product mutation until the read-only crosswalk identifies the smallest owning layer(s).

The first mutation must:
- preserve Adaptive C exactly;
- preserve terrain-support correctness;
- preserve performance architecture;
- address more than one landscape class only when the same proven field law owns them;
- remain bounded to the minimum required terrain/relief authority;
- avoid renderer/material/camera compensation.

## 11. VISUAL ACCEPTANCE

Owner inspection must include traversal through all five terrain classes.

Advancement questions:
- Does each class read distinctly?
- Are transitions between classes continuous?
- Does the landscape retain depth away from mountains?
- Are valleys/drainage readable as landforms?
- Do plains retain subtle structure rather than becoming flat sheets?
- Does the coast connect naturally to inland terrain?
- Has performance remained acceptable relative to accepted Adaptive C?
- Does terrain support remain correct?

A mountain-only improvement cannot pass Phase 1.

## 12. STOPPING LAW

Stop before construction if:
- ownership is ambiguous;
- required meso information already exists but is being lost downstream;
- a proposed repair requires reopening Adaptive C infrastructure;
- a surface/material/ecology problem is being misclassified as meso geometry.

## 13. NEXT DETERMINISTIC OPERATION

Perform the read-only five-class landscape-definition crosswalk against the accepted Adaptive-C baseline. Do not mutate product state until that crosswalk is complete.
