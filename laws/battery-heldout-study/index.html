Applied evidence · Battery domain

First strong cell-disjoint held-out signal.

Across 1,653 evaluation cycles from unseen batteries CS2_34, CS2_36, and CS2_38, the B/P/E/I/V axis model reached AUROC 0.9394 for predicting an 80% retained-capacity crossing within the next 20 cycles.

What was found?

The framework carried substantial information about approaching battery degradation into batteries excluded from development. That makes the result nontrivial and worth continued testing.
