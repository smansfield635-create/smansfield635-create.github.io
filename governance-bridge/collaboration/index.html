ABC Roofing & Remodeling

From first call to live operation.

ABC contacted LBS looking for help improving operations. They did not arrive with a diagnosed problem. This walkthrough shows how we would prepare, discover what is actually worth fixing, make a proposal, onboard the company after agreement, and then put the system into use.

ABC Roofing is fictional. The timeline below is modeled for this demonstration, not a promised implementation schedule.

Before signing

Find out whether there is something worth solving.

DAY 0ABC reaches out
Management says it wants better visibility and smoother operations, but does not prescribe the answer.
DAY 1Public research
What we look at: locations, services, customer reviews, complaints and publicly described processes.
What we bring to discovery: questions, not accusations. Public feedback from comparable Florida roofing operations suggests communication, field supervision, follow-through, quality control and handoffs are useful places to ask about.
DAY 3Discovery meeting
We ask management to walk us through the business in ordinary language: where jobs get hung up, what managers chase people for, what customers call back about, what happens when crews find a problem, who owns follow-up, and when the lead manager gets involved.
DAY 4What ABC confirms
Some field problems can wait too long for a manager response.
Customer follow-up can remain with one person without becoming visible to the wider management team.
A job can look normal in the operating system while something important still needs attention.
Lead management wants to know both what is wrong and whether somebody is already handling it.
DAY 5Proposal
Keep ABC's existing CRM and operating process. Add a management layer around the confirmed trouble spots. Local managers see what they need to handle; lead management sees the whole company, what is being handled, what is waiting, and what keeps happening. The proposal defines the first scope, required access, implementation assumptions and commercial terms.
Decision point
ABC accepts the proposal.

Agreement signed · implementation authorized

After signing

Connect what management agreed to monitor.

DAY 9Access & onboarding
ABC provides the agreed access to job status/history, responsible managers, relevant communications, tasks/follow-ups, approvals/documents and branch ownership. We do not ask for unrelated company data.
DAY 11Map the real operation
Confirm how Tampa, Orlando and Jacksonville actually work, who owns what, what managers already see, what should trigger attention, and when an issue should move up to lead management.
DAY 14Set up Local & Global views
Local: what each manager needs to handle. Global: what lead management needs to know across the company, including whether local issues are actually being handled.
DAY 15Test before launch
Run known situations through the system. Make sure ordinary jobs stay ordinary, real problems become visible, the right manager sees them, and actions/follow-ups behave as expected.
DAY 16Go live
The existing CRM remains the operating system. The Bridge adds management visibility around the areas ABC chose to monitor.
Implementation complete
