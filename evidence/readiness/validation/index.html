TRL 7 · Evidence-Semantics Validation

The proof has to match the claim.

The machine-readable record was the first half of the job. This page tells the human story behind the second half: every piece of evidence must be bound to the criterion it is actually being used to support.

The story: polished language is easy. Credibility should be harder. If a capability, evidence class or required identity stops matching the claim, the validation should stop with it.
