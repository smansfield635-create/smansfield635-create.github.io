## C3.1 purpose

Preserve accepted C2 conversational performance while making the approved product topology deterministic and implementation-ready.

C3.1 is not the trust engine, archetype engine, persistence engine, relationship game, or new scene system.

## Character/mode law

There is one Auren.

Opening interaction exposes two distinct conversational purposes:
- **Products**
- **Learn About Auren**

They are modes of the same canonical 13-year-old character, not separate personalities.

Auren's public layer remains sharp, impatient, self-assured and personality-bearing in Products mode. Products mode must not flatten him into a brochure or neutral help agent.

Learn About Auren is the route in which deeper character/narrative material may progressively emerge subject to Step 1 disclosure authority.

## Three-choice law and opening topology

The accepted C2 law remains: **maximum three contextual choices at any one time**.

The five direct products must therefore be reachable progressively rather than displayed as five simultaneous choices.

Frozen Products topology:

**Opening**
1. Products
2. Learn About Auren

**Products — first bank**
1. ARCHCOIN
2. Five Flags
3. More products

**Products — second bank**
1. Education
2. Nutrition
3. Book / Nine Summits of Love

Every product remains reachable in no more than two product-menu selections from the opening Products choice.

After a product conversation, contextual Back/Products navigation may return to the appropriate product bank without imposing a trust penalty or forcing repeated introductory dialogue.

Community / Consider the Energy is not substituted into the five-product set. If surfaced in C3.1, it is a separate campaign/community handoff reached contextually from relevant dialogue, never a sixth direct product choice.

## Product dialogue-bank law

Four products receive substantive progressive Auren conversation to the developed ARCHCOIN/C2 standard:
- ARCHCOIN
- Five Flags
- Education
- Nutrition

Each bank must:
- preserve Auren's personality;
- be progressive rather than expose every question at once;
- remain within three contextual choices;
- distinguish Auren's perspective from destination authority;
- use latest durable product truth;
- support an explicit destination handoff when the visitor wants the authoritative surface;
- avoid unsupported claims.

C3.1 may deepen authored content; it must not manufacture parallel product truth.

## Book / Nine Summits exception

Book is intentionally different.

Auren:
- knows what it is;
- may show natural personal interest;
- gives concise, surface-level orientation;
- does not receive a fifth deep product dialogue bank.

Deeper Book / Nine Summits conversation hands to Elara.

Frozen progression:
**Auren awareness / interest / concise orientation → Elara deep handoff.**

This exception must not be generalized to ARCHCOIN, Five Flags, Education, or Nutrition.

## Community treatment

Community / Consider the Energy remains a separate existing campaign gateway, not one of the five direct products.

C3.1 may include a contextual campaign handoff where relevant, but must not:
- replace Book with Community;
- expand the Products menu beyond the three-choice law;
- misclassify Community as a sixth product;
- create a deep product bank merely to force symmetry.

## Handoff law

Auren may hand off to existing destination authorities.

C3.1 handoffs must:
- use existing legitimate routes;
- identify the destination accurately;
- preserve destination ownership of official truth;
- never expose another character's held/private canon;
- never imply that routing grants Auren new knowledge.

ARCHCOIN's existing direct handoff is preserved.
Five Flags, Education and Nutrition receive explicit conversational handoff bindings.
Book hands deeper conversation to Elara.
Community may receive a contextual campaign handoff if included.

## Nutrition law

Nutrition dialogue is authored against latest durable Nutrition truth, including newer durable development where applicable, not merely stale public chamber copy.

Prototype/open engineering status must remain explicit. C3.1 may not turn unresolved prototype, manufacturing, clinical, medical, efficacy or commercial questions into settled claims.

Updating the Nutrition destination page is independent work and is not a C3.1 prerequisite, metric or blocker.

## Allowed mutation scope for C3.1 implementation

Primary authorized implementation surfaces:
- `products/auren/auren.voice.js`
- `products/auren/auren.chamber.js` only where required to support the frozen topology/handoffs without changing accepted cadence
- `products/auren/index.html` only if a minimal binding/cache identity change is required by the implementation

Prefer voice-bank/topology extension over runtime replacement.

No other product, character, scene, campaign, world-state or destination file is authorized for mutation by C3.1 without a separately explicit scope expansion.

## Preserved C2 mechanics

C3.1 must preserve:
- progressive conversation;
- three-dot typing indication;
- accepted timed typing/reading cadence;
- choices delayed until Auren completes his turn;
- maximum three contextual choices;
- reduced-motion handling;
- deterministic voice-node lookup;
- current route-handoff discipline.

Historical manual tap-to-advance is precedent only and is not restored by C3.1.
Historical silent-return behavior may inform clean Back navigation but does not supersede current C2 cadence.

C3.1 implementation cannot close until evidence proves:
1. opening presents Products and Learn About Auren as separate modes of one Auren;
2. no state exposes more than three contextual choices;
3. all five direct products are reachable;
4. ARCHCOIN, Five Flags, Education and Nutrition each have substantive progressive Auren dialogue;
5. Book remains concise and hands deeper conversation to Elara;
6. Community remains separate from the five-product set;
7. required product/campaign/character handoffs resolve to legitimate existing destinations;
8. Back/navigation does not inherently penalize trust;
9. C2 timing/typing/delayed-choice behavior remains intact;
10. Auren remains visibly in character in Products mode;
11. no trust, archetype, persistence or new scene engine is accidentally introduced;
12. no destination/product/character authority is overwritten.

Verification must include source/diff qualification plus browser interaction evidence for the reachable topology and handoffs.

## Explicit non-goals

- trust/affinity/reputation scoring;
- cross-session relationship persistence;
- remembered visitor-choice ledger;
- conversational archetype accumulation or scoring;
- archetype-sensitive routing;
- trust-sensitive disclosure;
- trust-sensitive scene admission;
- relationship-driven scene unlocking;
- a new Auren cardinal/problem/discovery engine;
- changes to cardinal world authority;
- Nutrition chamber modernization;
- replacement of C2 cadence;
- classification of Auren by visitor archetypes.
