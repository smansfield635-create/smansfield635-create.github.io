# Preregistration

## Universal candidate

For a bounded system with independently recoverable constitutive relations, selective disruption of a constitutive relation removes a predictable family of identity-preserving routes. External support or functional substitution may preserve immediate visible output without restoring endogenous reachability. Strengthening unrelated capacities does not reopen the lost route. Relation-specific restoration or a predeclared lawful substitute selectively reopens it, potentially subject to hysteresis.

## Primary falsification target

The candidate fails materially if independent observers cannot recover substantially concordant boundaries and constitutive relations, if selective disruption does not produce the preregistered route loss, if unrelated capacity reliably compensates, if support/substitution cannot be distinguished from endogenous continuity under withheld challenges, or if relation-specific restoration does not selectively reopen the predicted route.

## Design

External system authors register hidden systems and intervention plans with a separate custodian. Observer teams receive only the public observation package and the frozen relation/intervention vocabulary. They submit sealed structure estimates and held-out predictions before intervention outcomes are released. Baseline models receive the same admissible inputs.

## Primary endpoint

The primary endpoint is the preregistered composite `SEVERE_TEST_SCORE`, combining:

1. constitutive-relation recovery;
2. boundary recovery;
3. route-family recovery;
4. matched-output provenance discrimination;
5. withheld route-loss and route-reopening prediction;
6. incremental performance over the strongest baseline.

The exact component weights and thresholds are frozen in `prediction-and-scoring.v1.json`.

## Result integrity

No theory, route rule, threshold, feature admission rule, baseline, exclusion rule, or adjudication category may change after the first external observation package is released. Any necessary change creates a separately versioned successor and leaves the original challenge outcome intact.
