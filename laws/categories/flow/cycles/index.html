Reading layers

Practical, Engineering, and Empirical claims remain separate.

No reading is open on entry. Each reading remains available without being treated as interchangeable.

Practical reading

Show the repeated operation or exposure and the real consequence of accumulation or recovery.

Engineering reading

Expose cycle definition, state transition, counters, accumulated throughput, reset conditions, and exit thresholds.

Empirical reading

Show cycle count, longitudinal observations, trend, variability, and whether recurrence is stable or drifting.

Procedure

Recurrence must be defined with an interval, a state transition, and a rule for what accumulates, resets, recovers, or exits. Otherwise repetition is merely descriptive.

Audit boundary

Age, exposure, conventional burden, and unmeasured trend remain alternative explanations. A repeated pattern cannot be promoted into mechanism without additional evidence.
