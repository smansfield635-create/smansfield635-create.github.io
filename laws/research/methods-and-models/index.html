CP6-CONTENT-062

Zero-Aware Product Discipline

Zero-Aware Product Discipline 
If any normalized pressure term = 0:
  Π = 0

If any capacity or capacity-damage term = 0:
  K = 0

Otherwise:
  product = EXP(SUM(LN(normalized_value)))

Zero terms are preserved rather than silently dropped through logarithmic aggregation. This protects the diagnostic from falsely preserving pressure or capacity when a required component has collapsed to zero.
