# Chapter 02 Opening — 11s Cinematic Acceptance Standard

Status: FROZEN / AUTHORITATIVE FOR THE CHAPTER 02 OPENING 0–11s CHECKPOINT

This document is the durable acceptance authority for construction and review of the first 11 seconds of Chapter 02. “Technically valid” is necessary but insufficient.

## Governing checkpoint law

`TECHNICAL PASS × CINEMATIC PASS × PHYSICAL PLAYBACK PASS = ACCEPTABLE CHECKPOINT`

A failure in any one dimension stops the cycle. A file that merely has the expected duration/frame count is not a finished checkpoint. A playable file with weak cinematography is not a finished checkpoint. Only the combined pass may move forward.

## First-11s award-cinematography gate

The 0–11s picture must satisfy all of the following:

1. **One intentional camera move.** The shot must read as authored cinematography, not captured navigation. No finger-drag feeling, cursor-like corrections, hesitation, accidental acceleration, or operator artifacts.

2. **Immediate geographic story.** The picture must clearly progress through: distant Gratitude → increasing land scale → readable coast/relief → shoreline arrival → lateral shoreline reading.

3. **Motivated camera movement.** Forward travel must use controlled acceleration/deceleration while preserving continuous spatial orientation.

4. **Deliberate composition evolution.** Horizon, coastline, terrain mass, negative space, and depth must change in a way that creates visual progression rather than merely increasing proximity.

5. **Cinematic depth.** Foreground/midground/background separation, atmospheric perspective, terrain relief, and coastline dimensionality must remain legible.

6. **Intentional lighting.** The sun must not collide with terrain, clip distractingly, or become an accidental focal point. Exposure and atmosphere must remain coherent throughout the move.

7. **New visual state every few seconds.** No dead stretch, redundant travel, meaningless drift, or prolonged hold.

8. **Resolved final two seconds.** The approach must begin reading the shoreline laterally. Merely arriving near land is insufficient.

9. **Delivery integrity.** No visible banding, shimmer, macroblocking, scaling artifacts, frame-cadence problems, or codec-induced degradation.

10. **Picture must tell the story without typography or music.** The shot must have a clear beginning, development, and visual resolution even with text and audio removed.

## Typography gate — later stage

Typography is evaluated separately after the image passes. It must be compositionally integrated with the image, timed to visual events, restrained, legible, and never function as a caption pasted over weak footage.

The existing `0–4 / 4–9 / 9–11` structure remains valid because it gives the image room to establish itself before and after the expression.

## Audio gate — later stage

Music must reinforce camera progression rather than disguise pacing problems.

The `11s` musical transition is a structural synchronization point. It must not be used to force the picture into an unnatural cadence.

## Deterministic execution sequence

`FROZEN SOURCE → COMPATIBILITY PROBE → DEVICE PLAYBACK PASS → EXACT 11S CONSTRUCTION → TECHNICAL VERIFICATION → AWARD-CINEMATOGRAPHY REVIEW → OWNER PHYSICAL REVIEW → KEEP | ONE_REPAIR | REJECT`

Stages must not be collapsed. A downstream pass cannot retroactively waive an upstream failure.

## Disposition semantics

- `KEEP`: all three governing dimensions pass and the owner accepts the physical result.
- `ONE_REPAIR`: one bounded repair is authorized against the exact checkpoint while preserving the frozen source/story and the governing acceptance law.
- `REJECT`: the checkpoint is discarded; it does not progress to later typography/audio assembly as an accepted image foundation.

## Supersession rule

This standard remains binding for the Chapter 02 opening 0–11s checkpoint until the owner explicitly supersedes it. Later typography, audio, encoding, or publication work may add stricter gates but may not weaken or bypass this standard.
