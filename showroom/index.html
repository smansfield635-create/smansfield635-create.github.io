These tabs explain the layers behind the experience. They do not select a route, move the constellation, change the Compass, or control the Diamond and Window renderers.

A threshold, not a directory

The Showroom introduces Mirrorland through a layered experience: atmosphere establishes scale, the constellation establishes direction, and the Diamond establishes the central engineering object.

Four independent layers

  • Cosmos establishes environmental depth.
  • Constellation establishes route selection.
  • Diamond establishes object inspection.
  • Window establishes the foreground threshold.

Evidence Explanation and verification

Four cardinal gateways

  • North begins the Mirrorland story.
  • East opens character routes.
  • South opens wonders and world routes.
  • West opens mysteries and unfinished paths.

Sixteen explicit destinations

Each cardinal opens a four-route cluster. Selecting a room prepares its route in the controller. Entering the destination remains an explicit action.

Evidence tabs do not call controller route methods, open clusters, select child routes, alter the URL, or enter a destination.

Visual rendering

The compositor coordinates rear and front crystal drawing around the fixed Compass plane. The crystal system owns meshes, orbital targets, animation, and visible crystal drawing.

Semantic continuity

Four cardinal controls and sixteen child controls remain in the document whether or not crystal rendering is available. Their fallback artwork disappears only after the crystal renderer declares readiness.

A hidden fallback drawing is not a removed control. Semantic route targets remain available to keyboard, pointer, and assistive technology users.

Fixed center

The Showroom Compass remains centered while crystals move around it. Its visual mount is not navigation authority and does not inherit crystal geometry.

Explicit return

Selecting the Compass prepares the Main Compass return option through the Showroom controller. It does not navigate immediately.

The page-level Cosmos does not read Compass geometry, change Compass selection, or register itself as a Compass renderer.

G3 computational gem

  • 16 radial sectors.
  • 16 structural bands.
  • 256 lattice seats.
  • 512 surface triangles.
  • 800 lattice connections.

Two public views

Object View presents the computational gem as a whole. Lattice View reveals its internal structural system. The evidence tabs do not replace those renderer controls.

Diamond controls remain inside the Diamond ownership surface. Explanatory tabs remain outside it.

Foreground threshold

The Window is rendered in front of the Diamond and opens the threshold into the central object stage.

Independent ownership

The Window is not owned by the Compass, does not participate in crystal depth classification, and does not depend on the Cosmos renderer.

Functional chain

  • Controller owns selection and routes.
  • Compositor owns projection and layer ordering.
  • Crystals owns crystal geometry and drawing.
  • Interactions owns pointer and gesture behavior.
  • Diamond and Window own their own stages.

Decorative atmosphere

Cosmos owns one inert fixed 2D canvas, stars, dust, sparkles, rare meteors, and rare spacecraft. Its failure is nonfatal and leaves static CSS atmosphere behind.

Ownership boundary

Cosmos does not register compositor nodes, read crystal projection, claim pointer input, modify semantic controls, alter routes, or supervise Diamond and Window lifecycle.
