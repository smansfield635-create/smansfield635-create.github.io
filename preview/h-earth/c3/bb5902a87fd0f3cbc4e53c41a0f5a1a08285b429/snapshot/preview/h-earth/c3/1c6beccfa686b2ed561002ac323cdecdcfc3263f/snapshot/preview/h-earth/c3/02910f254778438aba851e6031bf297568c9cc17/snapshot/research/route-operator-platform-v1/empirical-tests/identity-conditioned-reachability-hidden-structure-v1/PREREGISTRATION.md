# Preregistration

## Universal candidate under test

A bounded system may preserve visible output after losing identity-defining internal relations. Selective disruption of a constitutive relation closes the original identity-preserving route; external support or functional substitution may preserve generic output without restoring that route; unrelated capacity does not reopen it; relation-specific restoration reopens it, subject to possible hysteretic reentry requirements.

## Primary test

A frozen observer, denied access to hidden truth and hidden implementation, must infer from black-box intervention observations:

1. the constitutive relation set;
2. the bounded internal node set;
3. the ordered original route;
4. the distinction among endogenous preservation, external support, functional substitution, fracture, and hysteretic restoration;
5. predictions for six withheld interventions.

## Primary success criteria

- exact constitutive-relation recovery;
- exact boundary recovery;
- exact ordered-route recovery;
- six of six held-out predictions correct;
- matched-output discrimination among endogenous, externally supported, and substituted states;
- all package-integrity and isolation checks pass.

## Failure ownership

Failure to recover hidden structure is assigned to this observer protocol or theory operationalization unless a predeclared implementation or custody defect independently invalidates execution. No post-result change to the hidden system, relation catalog, observer algorithm, success thresholds, held-out cases, or evaluator is permitted for this frozen run.
