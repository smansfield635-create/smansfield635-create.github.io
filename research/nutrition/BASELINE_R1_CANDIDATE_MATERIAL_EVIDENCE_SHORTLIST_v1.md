## 1. PURPOSE

Advance the material gate as far as public evidence permits without pretending that a web product page is a lot-specific COA or that a laboratory/reference material is automatically suitable for administration.

This artifact identifies candidate specifications only.

It does **not** purchase materials, select a final supplier, accept a lot, or authorize human dosing.

---

## 2. HARD EVIDENCE RULE

A candidate product page may establish:

- named chemical identity,
- catalog identifier,
- published assay/specification,
- published standards alignment,
- availability of COA documentation.

It cannot establish:

- the lot that will actually be received,
- lot-specific assay,
- lot/container match,
- expiration/retest status for the received lot,
- suitability for the intended human-study setting.

Therefore every candidate below remains **PENDING**.

## 5. R1-M03 — MAGNESIUM CITRATE

Public search confirmed that USP magnesium-citrate reference material exists, but the reference-standard page explicitly describes it as a quality-test/assay material rather than a material for administration.

That does not resolve the central R1 ambiguity:

**What exact magnesium-citrate material is intended for the 100.0 mg R1 raw-material mass, and what elemental-magnesium fraction does that material actually contain?**

No public candidate should be silently inserted merely to fill the blank.

Required evidence remains:

- exact administration-appropriate candidate,
- product/specification identity,
- chemical/form/hydration definition where applicable,
- declared or assayed elemental Mg content,
- lot-specific COA.

**PENDING — critical identity gate remains unresolved.**

## 6. R1-M04 — DEXTROSE

A concrete public specification was identified:

**Sigma-Aldrich D9559 — Dextrose monohydrate**

Published specification includes:

- identity: dextrose monohydrate / D-(+)-glucose monohydrate,
- formula: C6H12O6·H2O,
- CAS: 14431-43-7,
- molecular weight: 198.17,
- assay: 97.5–102.0% on anhydrous basis,
- meets USP testing specifications,
- supplier provides COA access.

This discovery is scientifically important because the frozen R1 ledger currently says only “dextrose.” Choosing monohydrate rather than anhydrous dextrose resolves a real material-form variable and therefore cannot be done silently.

### Status

**PENDING — candidate identified, but exact dextrose form must be explicitly selected/amended before acceptance.**
