Primary relationship

Feasible region

A state space separates admissible and inadmissible regions, with each controlling constraint clipping the space.

FEASIBLE REGION
ADMISSIBLE = {x | every required constraint C_i(x) holds}

Engineering controls

Declare hard and soft constraints, fixed thresholds, dependencies, tolerances, resource limits, environmental conditions, and failure conditions.

Failure behavior

A result obtained under one feasible region cannot be assumed valid after a controlling constraint changes.

Reading layers

Practical, Engineering, and Empirical claims remain separate.

Practical reading

Show real limits imposed by capacity, environment, resources, policy, safety, or operating conditions.

Engineering reading

Declare hard and soft constraints, fixed thresholds, dependencies, tolerances, and failure conditions.

Empirical reading

Show which constraints were observed, assumed, held constant, violated, or not measured.

Method

Map every materially controlling condition before interpreting an observed result or attempting transfer.

Audit boundary

Hidden constraints and unmeasured regime changes remain unresolved rather than treated as harmless.
