Why Diamond Gate existsA larger purpose, available when you want it.Open the story and Chapter One video. The Compass remains immediately usable without expanding this section.

Diamond Gate exists because useful ideas are often separated by the boundaries between disciplines, products, institutions, and stories. The estate gives those different kinds of work one place to meet without pretending they are all the same thing.

Perhaps the next step is not abandoning what came before, but making those pieces easier to see together. The Compass is how you orient yourself inside that larger purpose.

North gives context. East opens worlds. South measures and governs. West builds and tests. Bring a question and move until the right room becomes clear.
