## 2. PRODUCT CONCEPT

**Working name:** Baseline

**Intended use architecture:** low-burden pre-meal preparation beverage.

**Design intent:**
- simple formulation
- low powder burden
- low sugar
- non-stimulant
- no protein preload
- no meal-replacement behavior
- no unnecessary botanicals
- no ingredient-count inflation

**Target delivery:** single-serving powder stick or sachet mixed with water before a meal.

- minimal excipients

- no protein
- no fiber
- no botanicals

- no unnecessary sweetener
- unflavored or very lightly flavored first prototype
- no addition of actives without approval
