# 10. Observable successor state model

The required conceptual states are:

`ORBIT(selectedCard)`

`CARD_OPEN_MEANING(selectedCard)`

`CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE(selectedCard)`

`EVIDENCE_LENS(selectedCard,lensId)`

`SHARED_BRIDGE(selectedCard,returnSide)`

Internal implementation names may differ.

Required transitions:

- `ORBIT(i) + horizontal drag/swipe -> ORBIT(j)`
- `ORBIT(i) + Left/Right -> ORBIT(j)`
- `ORBIT(i) + open -> CARD_OPEN_MEANING(i)` unless a separately authorized retained-state rule applies
- `CARD_OPEN_MEANING(i) <-> CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE(i)` through horizontal intent and explicit accessible control
- vertical reading on either side remains on that side
- `CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE(i) -> applicable EVIDENCE_LENS(i,k) -> CARD_OPEN_EVIDENCE(i)`
- `CARD_OPEN_MEANING|EVIDENCE(i) -> SHARED_BRIDGE(i,returnSide) -> exact prior card + side`
- `CARD_OPEN_MEANING|EVIDENCE(i) -> Return to Orbit -> ORBIT(i)`

The future implementation may create a completely new visual mechanism for these transitions.

The required invariant is state continuity, not preservation of the predecessor animation or markup.

# 11. Gesture, focus, mobile, and reduced motion

The successor must preserve intent isolation:

- horizontal orbit intent selects another object;
- vertical page/side reading does not rotate the orbit;
- opening is not accidentally triggered by a completed drag;
- horizontal open-card intent changes Meaning/Evidence without rotating the background orbit;
- Shared Bridge reading does not move the background orbit or change the selected side accidentally.

Keyboard/focus requirements:

- Left/Right selects orbit objects;
- Enter/Space or an explicit accessible control opens the selected object;
- focus enters the opened object while selected identity remains stable;
- `View the Other Side`, `Inspect Shared Bridge`, `Return to Selected Side`, and `Return to Orbit` are keyboard operable;
- return restores focus to the exact selected orbit object;
- side/lens identity is programmatically determinable.

Responsive law:

`MOBILE_IS_A_DISTINCT_COMPOSITION`

Seven objects remain one interaction family on desktop, tablet, and mobile, but dimensions, visibility, control placement, typography, pacing, geometry, and motion may be redesigned per viewport.

Reduced-motion law:

`REDUCED_MOTION_PRESERVES_COMPLETE_SEMANTICS`

Motion may simplify or disappear; object selection, Meaning/Evidence access, Shared Bridge access, lenses, and exact return remain available.
