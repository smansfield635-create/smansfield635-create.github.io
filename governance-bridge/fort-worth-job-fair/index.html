Governance Substrate

Valid records do not automatically establish a valid result.

Different systems, teams and workflow stages can each produce locally valid evidence while the correspondence between those records remains unproven. The governance question is whether the required subject and transitions remain established strongly enough to permit the downstream claim.

Don't replace the systems that run the work. Use the evidence they already produce and determine what that combined evidence actually justifies.

A bounded way to test relevance

Start with one completed operation.

No system replacement is required to ask whether the governance layer adds useful information. Reconstruct one bounded operation from the evidence that already exists, then qualify what the chain actually supports.

  1. Choose one completed operation.
  2. Identify the objective.
  3. Establish authorization.
  4. Trace execution and evidence.
  5. Determine the supported result.
  6. Ask what that justifies doing next.
