## 1. What is being built

One film only:

**“Welcome to Diamond Gate Bridge” — the cinematic introduction for the main Diamond Gate Bridge homepage.**

This is not:
- a redesign of About;
- a redesign of the existing Chapter One awards film;
- a redesign of any Chapter 2–5 awards film;
- a redesign of the homepage itself;
- a generic product tour;
- a collection of isolated icons or feature demos.

The correct conceptual model is:

**This homepage cinematic is the “Chapter One” of Diamond Gate Bridge as a whole.**

It should introduce the estate the way a strong opening chapter introduces a larger work.

Inside this homepage cinematic, the term **“Chapter One”** refers specifically to the actual Chapter One section/destination on the homepage. That section is one subject within the film. It must not be confused with the role of the homepage cinematic as the estate’s opening film.

## 2. Governing creative interpretation

The film is a **cinematic compression of the current homepage experience**.

It should make the visitor feel that they have entered Diamond Gate Bridge, been oriented by the Compass, seen where to begin, understood that the estate contains multiple ways to engage, crossed into deeper story/world material, and returned to the Compass with context and agency.

The film should not invent symbolic stand-ins when stronger real Diamond Gate source material already exists.

The visitor should finish the film understanding approximately:

> Diamond Gate Bridge is a place.  
> The Compass orients me.  
> Chapter One is a place to begin.  
> There are multiple ways to engage.  
> The estate contains story, research, software, people, and worlds.  
> I can choose where to go next.

The Compass is not a generic compass rose.

The actual homepage Compass is a spatial navigation system with:
- dimensional stars;
- rotation;
- clustering;
- cardinal relationships;
- room exposure;
- Mirrorland revelation;
- live interaction.

The failed cinematic reduced this to thin concentric line art. That is unacceptable.

Use the strongest actual Compass geometry and motion authority available in the estate.

### Chapter One
Chapter One in this homepage film means the real Chapter One section/destination.

Do not substitute:
- a heart;
- a broken-heart metaphor;
- generic story symbolism.

The prior heart was both visually poor and conceptually forced. In the recorded failed film it was also upside down.

### Three Ways to Engage
The House, Coheriscope, and Awards belong to a shared presentation system.

Do not dismantle that structure into:
**brain → house → trophy** as three unrelated isolated scenes.

The House is not a Chapter One object.

The House belongs to the carousel / Three Ways to Engage family.

Treat these as a system.

Do not reduce them to tiny floating cards whose text cannot be meaningfully read.

The existing section already has strong editorial composition and large typography. That is a better source language than the failed miniature-card treatment.

### Mirrorland
Mirrorland is valuable because it already creates real narrative causality.

The Compass can reveal a threshold and change the nature of the experience.

Use that actual transition instead of inventing an unrelated transition effect.

### Audralia / world material
Use the strongest existing planetary/world representation.

Do not replace an actual world with a flat island outline and orbital doodles.

### Awards
Use the existing awards identity/presentation.

Do not substitute a simplistic trophy-outline icon if a stronger estate representation exists.

## 8. Why the current film failed

The failed homepage film is not simply “under-polished.” Its directing and source treatment are structurally wrong.

### Composition failure
Repeated formula:
- small object in center;
- large empty black field;
- small caption below.

This turns the viewport into a display case instead of a cinematic frame.

### Weak camera language
The film mostly swaps objects.

Successful Diamond Gate work uses:
- push-in;
- reveal;
- crop;
- scale;
- foreground/background;
- defocus/refocus;
- spatial movement;
- meaningful camera transitions.

### Asset inconsistency
The heart, line-art Compass, generic brain, outline House, flat world, trophy icon, and small UI cards do not appear art-directed into one coherent film.

They look like unrelated graphics placed on the same dark background.

### Source reconstruction failure
Strong existing estate objects were replaced by weaker handmade approximations.

This is one of the most important failures to avoid.

### Hierarchy failure
Carousel objects were promoted into isolated narrative scenes.

That made the film feel like:
“heart → cards → brain → house → globe → trophy”
instead of a coherent homepage journey.

### Typography failure
Successful About typography is compositional and architectural.

The failed homepage film treated language as small labels under exhibits.

### Narrative-causality failure
The sequence enumerated destinations rather than transforming one idea into the next.

The next film must make the transition itself communicate why the next beat follows.

### Escalation failure
The film did not build toward a midpoint, threshold, climax, or transformed return.

The final Compass therefore felt like “Compass again” rather than a meaningful return.

## 9. Important mistake history

The successor room should know what went wrong so it does not repeat it.

### Mistake 1 — treating the film like a product walkthrough
The homepage film is an estate-opening awards-style cinematic, not an instructional tour.

It can still orient the visitor, but orientation must happen through cinema.

### Mistake 2 — inventing a new visual language
A “look-development reel” was proposed even though Diamond Gate already has multiple successful films.

That was unnecessary.

The visual language is not unknown.

The task is **integration and direction**, not proving that Diamond Gate can make a good film.

### Mistake 3 — confusing Chapter One with the film’s role
The homepage cinematic is conceptually the estate’s opening chapter.

But the actual Chapter One section inside it remains a specific homepage destination.

Keep those two meanings separate.

### Mistake 4 — misclassifying the House
The House was incorrectly described as a Chapter One object.

Correction:
**The House belongs to the Three Ways to Engage / carousel family.**

### Mistake 5 — using invented heart symbolism
The current film used a heart as shorthand for Chapter One.

That connection was not required by the storyboard and was not an authoritative homepage representation.

Remove it.

### Mistake 6 — simplifying strong estate assets
Several objects were effectively redrawn as simplistic outlines or wireframes.

This produced an obvious regression from already-existing Diamond Gate work.

### Mistake 7 — validating state instead of pixels
Previous technical reviews could prove DOM/runtime state while still missing visible rendering failures.

One known example:
a gate could be logically hidden while remaining visibly rendered because of CSS precedence.

Future qualification must include actual rendered playback, not only state/DOM assertions.
