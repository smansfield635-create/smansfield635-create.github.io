Bring the context. Keep moving.
Copy the context request into your existing assistant, then paste its answer here. Review the initial brief once. After setup, the project continues through one prompt and one returned response.
For an existing project, copy its continuation prompt into your tool and paste the returned response here. It saves and prepares the next prompt automatically. Identity conflicts, changed objectives or decisions, and unsupported completion claims pause for review. Unanswered outcome or dependency questions can produce a bounded reconciliation request. Once answered, remaining limitations stay recorded and the next prompt returns to unfinished work; acknowledging a limitation does not resolve it. You can inspect or restore older checkpoints. Existing briefs and v0.1 handoffs are supported too.
Saving & privacy
Project records, attempt logs, pasted drafts and requested changes save in this browser’s local storage on this device. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to view them. They are not synced to another device or sent to an AI service. Each project has a stable local ownership reference for future account migration; this is not an authenticated ownership or access-control system.
Browser data can be cleared or lost. Download a backup for portable recovery. Backups include your project text; keep them somewhere you trust. Reference notes do not include the referenced files. Moving projects to Trash keeps their history; Delete permanently or Empty Trash removes those records and drafts from this browser. Downloaded backups remain separate.
Imported text is treated as data. Claims, approval identity, semantic meaning and provider compatibility are not independently verified.
