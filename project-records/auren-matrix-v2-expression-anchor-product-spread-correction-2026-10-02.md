# Auren Matrix v2 Correction — Expression Anchors and Product Spreads

This correction must be applied before the three Matrix-v2 samples are rewritten or accepted.

## 1. Problem corrected

Matrix v2 correctly made visitor attention meaningful, but the first sample pass still allowed under-anchored visitor expressions such as:

`Different how?`

That wording can be grammatically understandable while remaining too dependent on adjacency. It does not identify which part of Auren's expression the visitor actually noticed.

The product sample also allowed design/specification vocabulary to leak into Auren's spoken language, including terms such as `constraint`.

Both defects weaken the intended person-to-person interaction.

## 2. Expression-anchor law

**EVERY OFFERED VISITOR EXPRESSION MUST BE TIED TO AN IDENTIFIABLE PART OF WHAT AUREN JUST EXPRESSED OR TO AN ALREADY-ESTABLISHED ACTIVE CONTEXT.**

The governing chain is:

`Auren expression -> bounded expression anchors -> visitor notices one anchor -> visitor expression names/reflects that anchor -> Auren recognizes what was noticed -> Auren continues`

An expression anchor may be:
- a person;
- a concrete fact;
- a phrase Auren used;
- a consequence;
- an opinion;
- an emotion/reaction Auren exposed;
- a distinction;
- a practical action;
- a product capability/status;
- a visible contradiction or curiosity gap;
- a prior detail still active in the exchange.

Visitor expressions should not be vague merely because the immediately preceding sentence makes them decipherable.

Examples:

Auren: `I inherited it while they're gone.`

Lawful distinct visitor expressions include:
- `You inherited the Manor?`
- `Who are "they"?`
- `What do you mean, gone?`

Each response demonstrates attention to a different anchor.

## 3. One-expression-per-anchor design law

When Auren exposes multiple meaningful anchors in one turn, the authored visitor-expression field should preserve a distinct natural expression for each anchor that the visitor is meant to be able to pursue.

This does **not** mean every noun or sentence requires a button.

It means:
- do not collapse materially different attention targets into one generic response;
- do not offer three paraphrases of the same attention target;
- do not manufacture beliefs for the visitor;
- do not expose more anchors than the early encounter can responsibly support.

The number of offered expressions should follow the meaningful anchors, not an arbitrary fixed choice count.

## 4. Plain-human-language law

Auren's spoken language must not sound like a product specification, governance record, diagnostic engine, or design document.

Behind-the-scenes machinery may remain technically precise. Auren's delivery should be concrete and human.

Words/phrases requiring strong justification before appearing in ordinary Auren dialogue include:
- constraint;
- authority;
- disclosure;
- topology;
- orientation;
- contextual;
- infrastructure;
- demonstrated performance;
- evidence restraint;
- prospective;
- optimization;
- architecture;
- implementation;
- diagnostic terminology.

This is not a banned-word dictionary. A technically precise word may be used when it is genuinely natural for Auren and the subject. The default is simpler speech.

**The conversation should feel intelligent because Auren understands the subject, not because he uses technical vocabulary.**

## 5. Product-spread law — applies to all five products

The expression-anchor law and plain-human-language law apply to **every Auren product spread**, not only Five Flags.

Each product spread must provide:

1. **What it is** — concrete, short, understandable.
2. **What is interesting/useful about it** — in ordinary language.
3. **Current truth/status** — live, prototype, in development, not yet available, etc., without inflation.
4. **Auren's personal angle** — where supported or lawfully authored as character preference/reaction.
5. **Distinct visitor expressions** — tied to the meaningful anchors Auren exposed.
6. **One lawful depth handoff** — product authority owns details.
7. **No automatic connection reward** for browsing, opening, or purchasing.

The product spread is still part of meeting Auren.

**Products must reveal Auren through how he talks about them, not merely use Auren as a catalog interface.**

## 6. Five Flags corrected precedent

Preferred conversational shape:

Auren may explain, in substance:

`Five Flags is harder than it looks. The person helping you only gets five signals, so they can point you in the right direction without basically giving you the answer.`

This exposes distinct anchors such as:
- harder than it looks;
- only five signals;
- help without giving away the answer;
- trying the game.

Lawful visitor expressions may include:
- `Why is it harder than it looks?`
- `Why only five?`
- `So they can help, but they can't tell you?`
- `Can I try it?`

If the visitor asks to try it, current owner-directed status language is:

`Not yet. There'll be a demo soon. It's still being worked on.`

Auren may then add a personal reaction in substance:

`I'm actually excited about that one.`

That personal reaction creates another lawful expression anchor:

`Why that one?`

The exact dialogue remains sample material, not immutable runtime copy.

Do not claim the Five Flags demo is currently playable.

Do not imply price, liquidity, adoption, investment return, or capabilities not supported by product authority.

## 8. Education spread requirements

Avoid phrases such as `demonstrated performance` in ordinary first-contact dialogue when simpler language works.

The spread should allow distinct attention to:
- the 1,001-word/concept learning foundation;
- the fact that the system looks at what the learner can actually do rather than only what level they claim;
- adaptive placement in ordinary language;
- what is currently available versus not yet proven;
- Auren's own reaction to the idea/system where lawfully authored;
- opening the Education page.

Do not turn benchmark/system evidence into claims of proven human learning outcomes.

## 12. Character-revelation law

Every product spread should answer two questions simultaneously:

1. What did the visitor learn about the product?
2. What did the visitor learn about Auren from the way he responded to it?

If the answer to #2 is `nothing`, the spread is incomplete as a character-chamber interaction.

Auren's personal angle should not be artificially bolted onto every response. It should appear where natural, and when it appears it becomes a real attention anchor.

## 13. Connection interaction

Attention to Auren's personal reaction may matter relationally because the visitor noticed **Auren**, not merely product information.

However:
- selecting a personal-angle expression is not automatically a connection point;
- asking a product question is not automatically neutral or positive;
- connection interpretation remains contextual and Auren-relative;
- product browsing itself still earns nothing.
