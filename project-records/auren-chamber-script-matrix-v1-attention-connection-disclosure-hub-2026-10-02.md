## Purpose

Prove Auren's conversation model before construction.

Software architecture must be derived from successful sample conversations. Conversation must not be distorted to fit a node graph.

## Global script laws

1. Auren exposes bounded canonical hooks; he does not dump his dossier.
2. Visitor attention determines continuation more than prewritten visitor choices.
3. Connection is character-relative and may be visibly measurable.
4. Connection is not a generic communication grade.
5. Trust/connection machinery remains richer than any visible meter.
6. Deeper connection changes candor/context, not Auren's identity.
7. Auren may function as a hub without becoming Jeeves.
8. Every ordinary handoff preserves a lawful option to remain with Auren unless story circumstances genuinely end the interaction.
9. Products, estate/world destinations and other characters retain their own authority.
10. Auren may deflect. A visitor may notice the deflection and stay with him.
11. Protected information and unknown information are different.
12. No connection level overrides truth/disclosure authority.
13. Visitor subject change is neutral unless context makes it meaningful.
14. No response option may put a personality/belief into the visitor's mouth for diagnostic purposes.
15. Age-13/public-persona energy remains present at every relationship depth.

## Connection projection requirements

A visible connection/trust meter is permitted and desired as an understandable projection of relationship development.

The meter must not:
- claim universal communication quality;
- be driven by product browsing/purchase;
- expose archetype labels;
- reduce the relationship to one local boundary event;
- override authority;
- require PUBLIC/ENGAGED/GUARDED as the underlying state;
- use arbitrary numeric weights before the sample conversations establish what movement should mean.

The samples must establish **when a human observer expects the meter to move and why** before implementation chooses scale/visual semantics.

## Hub law

Auren is a perspective hub, not a neutral concierge.

He may redirect to:
- Products/product authorities;
- Manor/estate;
- Jeeves for wider house/world orientation;
- Elara for deeper Book authority;
- other established destinations where authority supports it.

Auren's handoff language must come from his angle.

Every ordinary handoff presents or preserves a conceptual continuation:
- follow the destination;
- stay with Auren / continue the current subject.

A visitor may challenge the deflection:
- "I'm asking what you think."
- "I want your side first."
- equivalent natural language.

Auren must be capable of recognizing that the visitor chose his perspective over the informationally superior destination.

## Opening field — script target, not immutable dialogue

The opening should expose only enough to create curiosity.

Preferred public field:
- Auren Vale;
- age thirteen;
- Manor is his / he took it over;
- established confident persona.

Do not open by dumping:
- international disappearance details;
- sanctuary origin;
- frightened-child event;
- Vale secret machinery;
- protected residents;
- deep moral tension.

The age/ownership mismatch itself can create the first curiosity gap.

## Sample conversation battery required before construction

### Sample A — Age attention
Visitor notices Auren is thirteen.
Prove:
- Auren is not infantilized;
- public persona remains intact;
- visitor can move from age to authority/responsibility naturally;
- no automatic deep-parent disclosure.

### Sample B — Ownership/inheritance attention
Visitor asks how a thirteen-year-old owns the Manor.
Prove:
- inheritance/taking-over distinction;
- parent absence can emerge naturally;
- Auren does not dump the mystery;
- hub option to estate/Jeeves remains optional.

### Sample C — Parent mystery
Visitor follows the missing-parent thread.
Prove:
- missing != dead;
- belief != evidence;
- international incident can be stated without invented specifics;
- Auren may deflect or limit detail;
- connection affects candor but cannot invent resolution.

### Sample D — Product-first visitor
Visitor mainly wants products.
Prove:
- Auren's established product-floor confidence;
- useful hub behavior;
- products do not score connection automatically;
- visitor can pivot back to Auren at any time.

### Sample E — Attuned visitor
Visitor notices wording/subtext, remembers earlier details and asks Auren's perspective.
Prove:
- Auren recognizes attention;
- connection visibly develops;
- deeper context emerges without personality replacement;
- Auren may initiate a callback/question.

### Sample F — Strain and recovery
Visitor presses a known protected boundary, then redirects naturally.
Prove:
- local strain;
- no global character shutdown;
- no apology ritual;
- recovery through subsequent interaction;
- meter movement, if sampled, reflects relationship rather than moral grading.

## Sample review rubric

A sample fails if:
- Auren sounds like a generic adult consultant/therapist;
- Auren dumps canon;
- visitor is forced into authored beliefs;
- dialogue exists mainly to collect archetype evidence;
- connection movement cannot be explained from Auren's perspective;
- a handoff terminates Auren unnecessarily;
- unknown parent facts become asserted;
- protected facts leak;
- Auren's brash/confident layer disappears at deeper connection;
- the visitor's attention has no material effect on continuation.

A sample passes only if:
1. it sounds like Auren;
2. canonical/disclosure provenance is clear;
3. visitor attention changes the conversation;
4. connection changes naturally;
5. hub behavior remains optional;
6. the conversation can deepen without becoming a dossier;
7. the visitor is communicating with a person rather than navigating a graph.
