## 6. Trust / relationship law

Trust is canonically relevant to Auren, but no generalized trust engine exists. Therefore C3 trust is **NEW DEVELOPMENT GROUNDED IN CANON**.

Relationship state may affect Auren's tone, public-mask reliance, voluntary personal perspective, relevant personal follow-ups, disclosure depth within his own authority, willingness to discuss Mirrorland, and willingness to offer a lawful scene opportunity.

Trust must not withhold ordinary public product facts/navigation.

Not yet bound: numeric range, weights, visible meter, bands, decay, reset semantics, persistence, eligibility thresholds, or exact action effects.

A visible trust meter remains optional, not recovered law.

**Back is not automatically a trust penalty.** Navigation may have relational consequence only when authored context gives the action meaning. Ordinary navigation, respectful retreat, avoidance, boundary respect, abrupt abandonment, contradiction, and prying must not be collapsed into one UI event.

## 7. Consequence model

Refined successor principle:

**current relationship state + authored visitor action + context → deterministic relationship-state transition**

Potential authored actions include listen, follow up, challenge, disclose, retreat, respect/violate a boundary, pry, contradict, manipulate, demonstrate consistency, or act selfishly/selflessly where fictional context actually establishes that meaning.

These are C3 design vocabulary, not recovered canon. Ambiguous button presses must not be assigned motives.
