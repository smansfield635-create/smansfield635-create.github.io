Reverse Audit

What happens when the result is traced backward and attacked by simpler explanations?

Reverse audit starts with the claimed result and moves backward through method, inputs, comparators, receipts, and source declarations.

Plain-language reading

A result is not protected by having already occurred. Reverse audit asks whether a simpler explanation, confound, threshold defect, source drift, observer dependence, or custody break can weaken it.

Procedural readings

Purpose, inputs, procedure, challenge, and record remain distinct.

Purpose

Make misleading success difficult to preserve.

Inputs

Use the same source identity, admission, outcome, baseline, comparators, observation window, and custody.

Procedure

Replicate, ablate, compare, challenge thresholds and observer dependence, and preserve failure without rewriting it.

Challenge

Burden AUROC 0.9704 exceeded combined 0.9394; raw+H* 0.7770 underperformed raw 0.7850; weakest-axis 0.6743 remained preliminary; hard MQ had zero sensitivity and balanced accuracy 0.4994.

Record

Preserve exactly what passed, failed, and remained unresolved.

Reverse method

  1. Verify evidence admissibility and frozen baseline.
  2. Apply the same rules independently without post-hoc weighting.
  3. Compare against stronger ordinary explanations and ablations.
  4. Challenge thresholds, false positives/negatives, observer dependence, and intervention contradictions.
  5. Preserve failures as failures.

Method standing

Observer-replication and decisive-falsification procedures are admitted methods. Their registrations and protocols are custody/method records, not completed validation studies.

Audit consequence

The result remains promising inside the named battery domain while mechanism, confound independence, replication, and operational use remain open.

Custody consequence

ACK packets, receipts, screenshots, graphics, and mockups may document status or alignment; they do not authorize implementation or prove scientific/runtime execution.

Claim boundary

A result surviving one held-out evaluation may still fail under confound control, transfer, independent replication, threshold calibration, source review, or operational testing.

No repair-by-presentation

This page does not repair defects, invent provenance, alter the baseline, or upgrade a method/registration into validation.
