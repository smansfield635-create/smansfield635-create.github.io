The same constraint can be read in two languages. The human side asks how the law governs behavior, accountability, trust, correction, and consequence. The engineering side asks how the same law appears as state, route, interface, metric, validation, or runtime boundary.

Human / Practical Law

A practical law is not a slogan. It is a repeatable constraint on how people, institutions, groups, and decisions behave when pressure, time, consequence, and accountability are introduced.
