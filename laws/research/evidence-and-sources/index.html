Investigation readings

Problem, evidence, method, result, and limits remain distinct.

Problem

What can actually be known from the admitted sources and observations?

Evidence

1,653 final-test cycle records from three complete held-out cells; combined AUROC 0.9394; burden comparator 0.9704.

Method

Expose acquisition, observation unit, sampling window, exclusions, missingness, custody, and source identity.

Result

Strong bounded ranking support; ordinary burden remains the stronger comparator.

Limits

Source existence and provenance do not establish mechanism, transfer, deployment fitness, or independent replication.

Missing evidence

  • Conventional-aging controls and burden ablation.
  • Chemistry transfer.
  • Independent external replication.
  • Threshold calibration and operational false-alarm analysis.

Derivative-method custody

Frozen registrations and protocol files can establish source identity, file hashes, and claim boundaries. They do not themselves establish empirical correctness or validation.
