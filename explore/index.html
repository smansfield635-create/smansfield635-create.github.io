How This Page Works

Explore prepares the question. Frontier handles the lane.

The distinction is intentional. Explore helps visitors name the surface of the issue. The Frontier hub is where deeper lane selection and applied review belong.

Explore

Introduces the frontier labels at a practical level. It helps the visitor recognize whether the issue is about energy, water, waste, infrastructure, trajectory, or another frontier surface.

Frontier

Receives the visitor after the surface has been identified. The Frontier hub is the correct place to choose or inspect the actual applied lane structure.

Evidence

A surface label is not proof. It does not validate a technology, certify feasibility, approve a claim, or establish production readiness.

Boundary: Explore is a surface-scratch entry page for frontier labels. It does not replace the Compass, does not operate as Governance, does not validate frontier claims, and does not independently route into specialized lane pages. Every frontier surface option intentionally opens the same Frontier hub at /explore/frontier/.
