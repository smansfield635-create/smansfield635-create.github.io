Flow · Detection before interpretation

Signals

What changed, and how was that change detected?

A signal is the detectable trace of a change moving from a source through a channel to a receiver. Detection can support a bounded relationship without establishing cause, mechanism, or readiness to act.

Primary relationship

Change becomes knowable only after it survives the path.

The source, channel, receiver, noise, delay, and threshold remain separate. A reading that arrives is not automatically a trustworthy signal, and a trustworthy signal is not automatically a causal explanation.

SOURCE CHANGE → SIGNAL → CHANNEL → RECEIVER → DETECTION
Source changeA state differs from its prior or reference condition.
SignalThe change produces an observable trace.
ChannelThe trace moves through a medium or measurement process.
ReceiverA sensor, observer, or system accepts the trace.
DetectionThe trace crosses a declared decision rule.

Reading layers

One relationship, three distinct interpretations.

Practical, Engineering, and Empirical readings are selectable but cannot silently substitute for one another.

What warning or reading appeared?

In practical use, a signal is the first observable indication that a system may be changing. The useful question is not whether the display moved, but whether the movement was detected consistently enough to support attention.

  • Name the real change being watched.
  • State who or what receives the warning.
  • Keep the decision consequence separate from the detection itself.

How did the trace survive the path?

Engineering requires an explicit source, channel, observation process, latency, noise model, threshold, and receiver state. Missingness, saturation, drift, and timing can change the meaning of the same apparent reading.

Signal contract
Detected = observation crosses a declared threshold

Reliable detection additionally requires:
source identity + channel integrity + receiver readiness

What was actually observed?

In the selected battery study, the defined near-term event was evaluated within the next 20 cycles. A combined representation ranked that event strongly in three complete cells excluded from development: AUROC 0.9394 across 1,653 final-test records. That is bounded held-out support for detection of the defined event, not proof of a distinct mechanism.

Observed support

Combined representation: AUROC 0.9394 in the named cell-disjoint evaluation.

Competing signal

Burden alone reached AUROC 0.9704, leaving conventional aging or accumulated use open.

Negative component result

Raw plus H* reached 0.7770 versus raw 0.7850; incremental delta −0.0080.

Threshold failure

The hard MQ conjunction produced zero sensitivity and balanced accuracy 0.4994.

Claim boundary

A detected signal may predict or correspond to change without establishing cause, mechanism, universal transfer, or operational readiness. The battery example remains limited to its named three-cell held-out evaluation; independent external replication and deployment calibration are incomplete.
