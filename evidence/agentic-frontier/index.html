01 · Discovery

What we are learning

Three questions sit at the front of the program: can hidden loss of options reveal fragility, can coherence be made precise enough to challenge, and can the research machinery itself be tested?

A promising result, a formal result, and an agentic comparison are not one result. Each keeps its own evidence class.

G11Meaning

Seeing fragility before failure

IT STILL LOOKS HEALTHY. BUT ITS ROOM TO RECOVER IS SHRINKING. Can that loss be measured before failure becomes obvious?

This research asks whether losing future options can improve prediction before ordinary signs of failure dominate; on one frozen power-system benchmark, it did improve prediction.

BoundaryStill unresolved: whether the same signal works beyond that setting, and why.

Why this matters

A system can look healthy while quietly losing room to recover. Measuring that remaining freedom may reveal fragility earlier than ordinary state measurements alone.

02 · Research method

How the work is done

Diamond Gate separates reasoning, construction, challenge, authority, evidence custody, and publication so that a convincing answer does not automatically become an accepted one.

The architecture is operating. Its measurable advantage over simpler research workflows has not yet been established.
