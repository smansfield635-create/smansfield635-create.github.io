# Compass Awards-Provenance Redesign Audit

Date: 2026-08-22
Scope: current Diamond Gate Bridge public estate, with emphasis on the Compass successor.

## Finding

The estate already contains an award-significant body of work. The Compass successor must therefore operate as the orientation instrument for an existing integrated platform, not as a generic landing page or simplified demo.

## Installed provenance

1. Experience design: the estate uses spatial navigation, carousels, worlds, characters, progressive disclosure and return-state continuity rather than a conventional sitemap model.
2. Browser-native technical craft: H-Earth and related world surfaces use repository-owned rendering, geometry, runtime state and direct manipulation.
3. Governed construction: Governance exposes the intent → protection → construction → verification → delivery journey and deliberately separates clear human interaction from bounded engineering authority.
4. Evidence discipline: Evidence uses claim family → practical meaning → engineering → proof → boundary, and explicitly refuses to convert presentation into certification.
5. Integrated platform: Compass, worlds, diagnostics, products, governance, evidence and construction now form an estate with shared interaction and continuity expectations.
6. Laws interaction precedent: the Laws chamber demonstrates the strongest established interaction grammar—fixed center, controlled spherical geometry, cardinal/secondary hierarchy, one readable label after settlement, progressive disclosure and reversible state transitions.
7. Public-legitimacy parity: Developer, Governance and Evidence share a one-active-card carousel grammar rather than stacked documentation.

## Award significance re-audit

Against the Webby 2025/2026 website criteria—Content, Structure & Navigation, Visual Design, Functionality, Interactivity, Innovation and Overall Experience—the estate's strongest current correspondences are:

- Content: original worlds, diagnostics, governance, evidence, research and construction narrative.
- Structure & Navigation: Compass-as-place, persistent return paths and cross-page estate architecture.
- Visual Design: atmospheric dark-space language, crystalline geometry, native world rendering and differentiated chamber identities.
- Functionality: real browser-native environments, controller-owned interaction states, bounded deployment and recovery systems.
- Interactivity: spatial Compass manipulation, nested clusters, carousels, portals, world controls and situated guides.
- Innovation: AI-assisted governed construction, explicit public evidence boundaries, stateful recovery and estate-scale integration.
- Overall Experience: strongest when these systems feel like one place rather than unrelated pages.

Against Awwwards-style weighting, the estate is strongest in creativity and technical design potential but can lose heavily on usability when routes, state transitions, labels or publication surfaces fail. Against CSS Design Awards categories, Innovation is already a credible strength; UI and UX require the Compass to visibly inherit the estate's best interaction standards.

## Rejection of previous clone direction

The prior isolated clone was rejected because it reduced the estate into large static editorial blocks, failed to make secondary cluster semantic ownership visible, did not consistently expose established state transitions, and presented one polished Mirrorland surface as if that represented a complete successor.

## Gen346 design law

The successor is governed by:

**FULL-FIDELITY SYSTEM + FORWARD PROGRESS**

Required architecture:

1. Estate-oriented hero with two-state perspective carousel.
2. Awards-provenance field presenting the five installed achievement pillars as a spatial carousel.
3. Progressive New Here entry rather than an explanatory wall.
4. Cinematic introduction treated as proof of the estate, not decoration.
5. Canonical Compass geometry as the visual and interaction center.
6. Four cardinal stars preserved with exactly one readable semantic owner after settlement.
7. Secondary clusters with exactly one readable room identity at a time and controller-owned selection/return.
8. Mirrorland as a reversible state branch of the Compass.
9. Brain / Awards / House spatial capability orbit.
10. TRL / TRA readiness as a stage carousel, not a grid dump.
11. Destinations as the final release surface after orientation, not a substitute for the Compass.

## Acceptance boundary

No successor is acceptable because files changed or a deployment passed. Acceptance requires rendered inspection proving that the page visibly expresses the above architecture, that secondary labels and state transitions function, that return paths restore prior Compass state, and that the result materially exceeds the rejected clone.
