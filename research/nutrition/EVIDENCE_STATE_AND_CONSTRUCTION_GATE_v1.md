# EVIDENCE_STATE_AND_CONSTRUCTION_GATE_v1

## Purpose

Preserve the distinction between architectural maturity, engineering completion, and empirical scientific validation before any nutrition construction begins.

## Evidence-state vocabulary

Every meaningful claim, component, formulation choice, algorithmic rule, matrix entry, manufacturing assumption, and commercial statement must carry one of these states:

- **LOCKED** — canonical program law or architecture.
- **ENGINEERED** — designed to an implementation-ready specification, but not necessarily empirically validated.
- **PROVISIONAL** — working candidate subject to revision.
- **REQUIRES_LITERATURE_VALIDATION** — needs formal reconciliation against current scientific literature.
- **REQUIRES_BENCH_TEST** — requires physical, chemical, manufacturing, stability, or analytical testing.
- **REQUIRES_HUMAN_VALIDATION** — requires ethically governed human study before outcome claims are elevated.

## Construction gate

Construction may proceed only when the relevant object has:

1. Explicit object identity: `OBJECT_A_AMPM` or `OBJECT_B_BASELINE`.
2. Defined formulation role and comparator.
3. Safety review state attached to every active component.
4. Delivery-form feasibility state attached.
5. Interaction/timing state attached.
6. Manufacturability state attached.
7. Outcome/instrument pair defined for every intended empirical claim.
8. Kill criterion defined.
9. Regulatory/claims state defined.
10. No unresolved item silently promoted from PROVISIONAL to LOCKED.

## Non-substitution laws

- A commercially attractive tier does not prove a scientific distinction.
- A manufacturable formula does not prove physiological benefit.
- A plausible mechanism does not prove a clinical outcome.
- User testimony does not substitute for controlled validation.
- Ingredient popularity does not substitute for exact-form evidence.
- "Natural" or "organic" status does not establish universal safety.
- A pediatric formula cannot be obtained by simply scaling an adult formula.
- AI personalization may not override safety gates or invent treatment.
- A historical 256-state Baseline design space does not establish 256 validated formulations.
- A prototype recipe is not a canonical production formula until reconciled through safety, evidence, physical feasibility, and manufacturing gates.

## Object separation

### OBJECT_A_AMPM

Primary empirical questions:

- Can useful micronutrient coverage be compressed into a lower-burden AM/PM architecture?
- Does temporal partitioning add measurable value?
- Does bounded personalization add measurable incremental value beyond a strong common foundation?

### OBJECT_B_BASELINE

Primary empirical question:

- Can a low-burden pre-meal intervention improve defined meal-transition outcomes versus water and/or an appropriate usual-condition comparator?

No evidence from one object automatically transfers to the other.

## Required stop conditions

STOP construction or escalation if:

- dose cannot be reconciled with applicable safety ceilings;
- chemical form remains unresolved where form materially affects dose, absorption, stability, or safety;
- required physical mass defeats the intended burden;
- interactions or medication conflicts are unresolved;
- blend/content uniformity cannot be demonstrated;
- stability is unacceptable;
- GI tolerance is unacceptable;
- the comparator cannot distinguish the hypothesized mechanism/value;
- the proposed outcome cannot be measured reliably;
- personalization complexity lacks a measurable target benefit;
- claim language outruns evidence.

## Pediatric gate

Pediatric/child work remains a separately governed track.

No pediatric construction proceeds without age-specific:
- nutrient reference reconciliation,
- upper-limit/safety review,
- formulation review,
- delivery-form review,
- clinician/regulatory review where applicable.

## Manufacturing gate

The turnkey GMP path is part of scientific engineering.

Before an RFQ is treated as production-ready, the transfer package must include:
- master formula specification,
- exact chemical forms,
- elemental targets,
- raw-material masses,
- dosage/delivery form,
- BOM,
- supplier and COA requirements,
- allergen requirements,
- excipients,
- process constraints,
- pilot specification,
- finished-product specifications,
- stability protocol,
- packaging requirements.

## Empirical hierarchy

Architecture
→ engineering
→ bench validation
→ tolerability
→ controlled human validation
→ scale validation
→ claim eligibility

No layer may be skipped by narrative.
