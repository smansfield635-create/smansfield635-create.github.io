# Whole-Estate Narrative Continuity Development Map v1

Status: `ACTIVE_DEVELOPMENT_STANDARD`

Purpose: provide a universal development map for any new or renewed Diamond Gate Bridge page, frontier, product, world, campaign, evidence surface, governance surface, or narrative room so that local expression remains distinct while its relationship to the whole estate stays legible.

This standard is additive to the existing `WHOLE_ESTATE_NARRATIVE_SPINE_v1`. It does not replace the Compass, change scientific standing, collapse room identities, force a visitor through one sequence, or make a commercial page the center of the estate.

## Master proposition

`PEOPLE_IDEAS_AND_SYSTEMS_OFTEN_CONTAIN_MORE_POSSIBILITY_THAN_THEIR_PRESENT_FORM_REVEALS`

Development translation:

> Diamond Gate does not only display what was created. It is about what becomes possible to create, understand, preserve, test, experience, and carry forward.

The proposition is intentionally broad enough to connect the estate without forcing every room to repeat the same words.

## Universal transformation map

Every public or frontier surface should be able to locate itself somewhere on this continuity:

`LATENT_POSSIBILITY -> PRESSURE_OR_LIMIT -> ORIENTATION -> STRUCTURE -> EXPRESSION -> TEST -> EVIDENCE -> AGENCY -> CONTINUATION`

A page does not need to contain every stage. It must know which stage or stages it serves, what came before conceptually, and what lawful next movement it enables.

### Stage meanings

1. `LATENT_POSSIBILITY` — something contains more capacity, meaning, value, or direction than its present expression reveals.
2. `PRESSURE_OR_LIMIT` — a constraint, unfinished condition, missing resource, uncertainty, fragmentation, or unresolved question prevents that possibility from being fully expressed.
3. `ORIENTATION` — the visitor can locate the question, direction, route, or relevant estate capacity without needing total ontology mastery.
4. `STRUCTURE` — governing relationships, boundaries, laws, methods, architecture, or construction discipline make progress coherent rather than arbitrary.
5. `EXPRESSION` — the possibility becomes a product, world, page, story, model, interaction, campaign, or working system.
6. `TEST` — the expression meets challenge, comparison, qualification, falsification, use, or consequence.
7. `EVIDENCE` — the estate distinguishes what was demonstrated from what remains aspirational, unresolved, contextual, metaphorical, or unclaimed.
8. `AGENCY` — the visitor, user, creator, builder, or community gains a clearer ability to choose, act, understand, build, or continue.
9. `CONTINUATION` — the result can evolve without erasing its history, identity, boundaries, or relationship to the rest of the estate.

## Estate-role crosswalk

The same master proposition appears differently by route. Local identity must remain primary.

- `COMPASS / ORIENTATION`: discover what exists, recognize the question being carried, and choose a direction. Core movement: `CONFUSION_OR_COMPLEXITY -> ORIENTATION`.
- `NARRATIVE / MIRRORLAND / WORLDS / CHARACTERS`: experience possibility, pressure, consequence, identity, choice, and transformation from the inside. Core movement: `UNSEEN_OR_UNFINISHED_CONDITION -> FELT_MEANING -> CHOICE_OR_BECOMING`.
- `FRONTIER / EXPERIMENT / RESEARCH`: test what does not yet have a settled answer. Core movement: `POSSIBILITY -> INVESTIGATION -> POSITIVE_ADVERSE_OR_UNRESOLVED_RESULT`.
- `PRODUCTS`: turn recurring complexity or need into a usable system that increases practical agency. Core movement: `NEED_OR_COMPLEXITY -> USABLE_INSTRUMENT -> AGENCY`.
- `LAWS / METHODS / MODELS`: expose the constraints, relationships, falsifiers, and evidence standing that prevent interpretation from outrunning reality. Core movement: `QUESTION -> ANSWERABLE_STRUCTURE -> BOUNDED_CONCLUSION`.
- `GOVERNANCE`: preserve authority, identity, continuity, recovery, and quality while increasingly capable construction changes the estate. Core movement: `CAPABILITY -> CONTROLLED_CHANGE -> CONTINUATION`.
- `EVIDENCE`: attach claims to inspectable records and preserve the distinction between demonstration, context, inference, uncertainty, and non-claim. Core movement: `CLAIM -> RECEIPT -> BOUNDARY`.
- `AWARDS / RECOGNITION`: narrate why demonstrated work is significant without converting biography, ambition, target categories, or milestone symbolism into proof. Core movement: `BODY_OF_WORK -> SIGNIFICANCE -> TRANSPARENT_CLAIM_BOUNDARY`.
- `BUILD / COMMERCIAL CONSTRUCTION`: offer the estate's constructive capability outward so another person can move from idea to working expression without already possessing a technical team, finished specification, or equal resources. Core movement: `IDEA_WITH_CONSTRAINTS -> GOVERNED_CONSTRUCTION -> SOMETHING_REAL_AND_OWNABLE`.
- `CAMPAIGNS / COMMUNITY`: translate established standing and usable systems into shared public action without turning aspiration into evidence. Core movement: `UNDERSTANDING -> PARTICIPATION -> COMMUNITY_EFFECT`.

## The creator / underdog relation

The creator story is a provenance layer, not the estate's proof standard and not a mandatory autobiographical theme.

Law:

`CREATOR_PROVENANCE_MAY_EXPLAIN_SIGNIFICANCE_BUT_MAY_NOT_SUBSTITUTE_FOR_TECHNICAL_EVIDENCE`

The reusable narrative value is not "the creator was an underdog." It is:

`INITIAL_RESOURCE_OR_BACKGROUND_LIMITS_DO_NOT_DEFINE_FINAL_CREATIVE_CAPACITY`

That principle can legitimately connect Build, Awards, products, and selected narrative experiences when contextually appropriate. It must not be forced onto scientific, evidentiary, or unrelated narrative surfaces.

## Page / frontier development contract

Before a new surface is considered narratively coherent, its developer should be able to answer all of the following:

1. `LOCAL_IDENTITY` — What is this surface actually for if the rest of the estate did not exist?
2. `MASTER_RELATION` — Which part of the universal transformation map does it express?
3. `VISITOR_STATE_IN` — What condition, question, desire, pressure, or level of understanding can reasonably bring a visitor here?
4. `VISITOR_STATE_OUT` — What should be clearer, possible, testable, usable, or actionable after the surface succeeds?
5. `EVIDENCE_CLASS` — Is the content demonstrated fact, bounded claim, interpretation, metaphor, narrative fiction, aspiration, offer, or unresolved frontier?
6. `UPSTREAM_RELATION` — What conceptual route or estate capacity can precede this surface without being required as access control?
7. `DOWNSTREAM_RELATION` — What lawful next route should become intelligible from here?
8. `NO_FLATTENING` — Does the surface retain its own voice instead of becoming an advertisement for the whole estate?
9. `NO_OUTRUNNING` — Does its language stay within the evidence and authority actually established?
10. `CONTINUITY_HOOK` — If the surface evolves later, what identity, route, claim boundary, provenance, or user expectation must survive?

A page may pass with only one or two explicit cross-estate links. Narrative continuity is a relation, not a requirement to mention every room.

## Threshold sentence rule

Cross-estate continuity should usually be carried by a small number of high-leverage threshold sentences, transitions, labels, or next-step controls rather than by repeated exposition.

Preferred pattern:

`LOCAL_PURPOSE -> ONE_SENTENCE_WHOLE_ESTATE_RELATION -> LOCAL_ACTION`

Avoid:

`LOCAL_PAGE -> EXPLAIN_THE_ENTIRE_ESTATE -> RETURN_TO_LOCAL_PAGE`

The relationship should be unmistakable but quiet enough that the local experience remains primary.

## Commercial relevance law

A commercial surface is coherent only when the offer is a genuine outward expression of demonstrated estate capability.

For Build:

`ESTATE_CONSTRUCTION_CAPABILITY -> REPEATABLE_GOVERNED_SERVICE -> ANOTHER_PERSONS_IDEA_CAN_BECOME_WORKING_EXPRESSION`

Prohibited framing:

`IMMERSIVE_SITE_EXISTS -> THEREFORE_BUY_A_WEBSITE`

Preferred framing:

`THE_ESTATE_DEMONSTRATES_A_CONSTRUCTIVE_CAPABILITY -> THAT_CAPABILITY_CAN_NOW_BE_APPLIED_TO_YOUR_PURPOSE`

Pricing, packages, contact routes, and transaction details may exist, but they are subordinate to the product promise and must not replace it as the page's identity.

## Narrative-world independence law

Narrative worlds are not advertisements. Their artistic and experiential integrity is preserved.

`NARRATIVE_RELATION_IS_ARCHITECTURAL_NOT_PROMOTIONAL`

A world may embody themes such as unfinished possibility, pressure, identity, agency, relationship, consequence, or becoming. It does not need to mention Build, Governance, Awards, or commercial services to be coherent with the estate.

## Visual-semantic continuity

Visual standardization must not erase local worlds. Reusable public presentation standards may carry shared cues such as typography hierarchy, navigational clarity, state color, star/cosmic atmosphere where appropriate, responsive density, evidence boundaries, and progressive disclosure. A world or product may deliberately diverge when its local identity requires it.

Law:

`SHARED_VISUAL_LANGUAGE_SUPPORTS_RECOGNITION; LOCAL_VISUAL_IDENTITY_SUPPORTS_MEANING`

## Development disposition

A new or renewed surface is narratively ready when:

`LOCAL_PURPOSE_CLEAR = TRUE`

`MASTER_RELATION_IDENTIFIABLE = TRUE`

`VISITOR_STATE_TRANSFORMATION_IDENTIFIABLE = TRUE`

`CLAIM_AND_METAPHOR_BOUNDARY_PRESERVED = TRUE`

`DIRECT_ENTRY_PRESERVED = TRUE`

`NO_ROOM_FRAMED_AS_WHOLE_ESTATE = TRUE`

`NO_FORCED_CROSS_PROMOTION = TRUE`

`LAWFUL_NEXT_MOVEMENT_AVAILABLE_OR_INTENTIONALLY_TERMINAL = TRUE`

`CONTINUITY_HOOK_DEFINED = TRUE`

## Relationship to existing constitutional narrative spine

This map is a development instrument. The existing `WHOLE_ESTATE_NARRATIVE_SPINE_v1` remains the higher-order explanatory constitution for whole-estate sequencing and scientific/narrative boundaries.

Where the two appear to conflict:

`NARRATIVE_SPINE_CONSTITUTION_PREVAILS`

This map adds a reusable design question:

`HOW_DOES_THIS_LOCAL_SURFACE_PARTICIPATE_IN_THE_ESTATE_WITHOUT_CEASING_TO_BE_ITSELF?`
