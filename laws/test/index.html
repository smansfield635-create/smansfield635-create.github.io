Explain the test before running it.

Test is a supporting method authority. It explains what may enter, how a proposition is constructed, how the same record is audited in reverse, and how the outcome is classified. Completion does not independently establish truth or scientific validation.

Baseline

The record advances through ordered evidence and justification while applying Flow, Integrity, Reality, and Structure.

Reverse Audit

The accepted record is traversed backward to expose baseline drift, contradiction, identity loss, unsupported substitutions, or invented evidence.

Results & Records
