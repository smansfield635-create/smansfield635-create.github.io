Governance Bridge · DGB × LBS conversation

Do we have something worth building together?

Diamond Gate Bridge produced a working governance software core. LBS publicly operates across clients, campaigns, staffing, teams, reporting, metrics and scaling. The question now is whether those two perspectives are complementary enough to justify a real experiment.

We do not replace the systems that run the work. The software checks whether evidence across those systems, teams and steps actually supports the result the business relies on.
