What the visitor should recognize

Pressure changes the route before it changes the promise.

Demand, delay, uncertainty, and competing needs alter what choices remain available. The stable promise is not the same thing as the conditions acting upon it.

Trajectory implication

When unresolved pressure keeps increasing, the practical path bends, narrows, or closes even if the stated commitment remains unchanged.

How the relationship is represented

A bounded field acts on a path.

The scene specification declares the field source, target, distribution, boundary, and status. The renderer consumes that record; it does not infer meaning from color, motion, or shape.

What the evidence permits

One formula record · four synchronized surfaces

Available choices contract when unresolved pressure exceeds capacity.

This is an illustrative definition used only to test whether one structured expression can drive formal notation, plain-language meaning, parameter controls, and spatial change without implying scientific validation.

A is available choice space, C is current capacity, and P is unresolved pressure.
