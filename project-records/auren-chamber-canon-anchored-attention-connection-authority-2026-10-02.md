## Chamber attention model

The host must not dump these anchors as a dossier.

A conversational Auren turn should expose a small bounded set of canonical hooks.

The system must record which canonical hooks were actually exposed.

The visitor's next action/response is interpreted primarily by **what they paid attention to**.

Relevant attention phenomena include:
- immediate follow-up to an exposed person, event, fact, consequence or tension;
- noticing a detail Auren emphasized;
- noticing a detail Auren did not emphasize;
- returning later to an earlier exposed anchor;
- remembering a specific fact;
- connecting two previously exposed anchors;
- distinguishing Auren's statement from visitor assumption;
- noticing practical versus human consequence;
- noticing contradiction/tension;
- respecting or pressing an expressed boundary;
- ignoring an available hook and changing subject.

Ignoring an anchor is not automatically negative. Subject change is lawful.

No visitor option may manufacture a belief, personality or concern merely to feed the attention model.

## Connection / trust model

Auren's chamber **does retain relationship machinery**.

The mistake to avoid is equating invisible machinery with absent machinery.

Connection/trust is not merely a communication-skill grade. It represents how the interaction is developing **with Auren specifically**.

The visible presentation may provide a measurable connection/trust indication because owner intent values a perceivable example of whether the visitor is connecting with the individual character.

The visible projection must not become the underlying model.

Underneath it, independent phenomena may include:
- familiarity / meaningful shared history;
- attunement to what Auren exposes;
- conversational momentum;
- Auren's willingness to volunteer authorized context;
- subject-specific caution;
- known boundaries;
- pressure;
- recovery/repair;
- remembered assistance;
- unresolved/resumed threads;
- trust-relevant events supported by Auren canon.

A local protected-subject problem must not automatically collapse the entire relationship.

A high connection state must never override information/disclosure authority.

Products do not earn connection merely through browsing or purchasing.

The exact visible meter semantics/scale are a successor design problem and are **not frozen by this record**. In particular, this record does not restore PUBLIC/ENGAGED/GUARDED as the mandatory underlying state model and does not authorize arbitrary numeric gain/loss weights.

## Communication-learning purpose

The visitor is not taking a disguised assessment.

The visitor learns communication through the consequences of interacting with a character who:
- notices what the visitor noticed;
- remembers what mattered;
- reacts to genuine attention;
- has preferences and boundaries;
- changes relational behavior;
- can become more candid or more locally cautious;
- can recover naturally;
- remains himself.

The chamber should make communication measurable enough to be interesting without reducing communication to selecting a designated correct answer.

The central question for relationship interpretation is:

**What did the visitor notice, remember, connect and pursue, and what does that mean to Auren?**
