# Preregistration

## Mechanism candidate

For a bounded system whose constitutive relations and required routes can be recovered from independent documentation, a real intervention that removes a required relation should selectively remove the capabilities that depend on that route. Unrelated capacity should not reopen the route. External support may preserve visible output while endogenous reachability remains absent. Endogenous restoration should require repair of the relevant relation or a predeclared lawful substitute. Reversal may fail to return the system to its prior state when the intervention produces hysteresis.

## Primary falsification target

The candidate is materially falsified if preregistered relation-derived predictions fail under admissible real interventions, if unrelated capacity reliably compensates for supposedly required route loss, if supported output cannot be distinguished from intrinsic continuity, or if relation-specific restoration does not outperform unrelated repair.

The candidate is underdefined if independent analysts cannot reproducibly recover system boundaries, constitutive relations, route families, support provenance, or lawful substitutes from the same admissible documentation.

The candidate is redundant if it predicts correctly but does not materially exceed the strongest frozen comparator on the primary endpoints.

## Evidence source rule

Primary relation candidates must be derived from documentation independent of the theory team and independent of held-out outcome labels. Admissible sources include authoritative schematics, architecture documents, service manuals, maintenance procedures, incident records, work orders, change tickets, validated engineering descriptions, and contemporaneous operational logs.

The custodian must freeze source hashes, provenance, dates, redactions, and the exact public documentation package before relation derivation begins.

## Real intervention rule

Primary intervention episodes must be actual documented faults, component removals, replacements, reconfigurations, support activations or withdrawals, repairs, or reversals. Purely simulated perturbations, theory-authored toy systems, and interventions designed after inspecting theory predictions are excluded from the primary result.

## Prediction sequence

1. The custodian seals hidden truth, intervention fidelity evidence, and held-out outcomes.
2. All teams receive identical public documentation and observations.
3. Theory observers and comparator teams submit frozen predictions.
4. Submission hashes and timestamps are committed.
5. Held-out intervention outcomes are unblinded.
6. A separate evaluator scores the frozen predictions.
7. Exactly one primary disposition is assigned.

## Primary portfolio minimum

The initial gauntlet requires at least four admissible systems across at least two materially different domains and at least twenty admissible intervention episodes. Each primary test requires at least four episodes drawn from at least two systems, except hysteresis, which requires at least three complete disruption-reversal sequences drawn from at least two systems.

## No post-outcome rescue

No relation rule, route definition, comparator, threshold, exclusion rule, endpoint, feature-admission rule, or adjudication category may change after the first held-out outcome is exposed. Any repair creates a separately versioned successor and leaves this operation's result intact.
