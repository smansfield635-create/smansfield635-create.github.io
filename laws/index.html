Human Law / Engineering Law / Evidence

The same constraint can be read in two languages.

The human side asks how the law governs practical behavior, accountability, trust, correction, and consequence. The engineering side asks how the same law appears as state, route, interface, metric, validation, or runtime boundary. Evidence is held separately so applied tracks do not become claims by presentation alone.

Human / Practical Law Social thermodynamics reads constraint as behavior under pressure. +

A practical law is not a slogan. It is a repeatable constraint on how people, institutions, groups, and decisions behave when pressure, time, consequence, and accountability are introduced.
