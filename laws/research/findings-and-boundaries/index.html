Investigation readings

The study closes with its limits still attached.

Problem

Can a frozen battery representation rank a defined near-term event in cells excluded from development?

Evidence

1,653 final-test records from three complete cells; supporting, negative, mixed, and competing results remain distinct.

Method

Frozen representation, complete-cell holdout, fixed 80%-within-20-cycles event, ordinary baselines, component challenges.

Result

Strong bounded ranking survived; a distinct mechanism did not.

Limits

Mechanistic specificity, chemistry transfer, external replication, operational calibration, critical-system use, and universal law validation remain unresolved.

Claim ceiling

Strong domain-specific cell-disjoint held-out empirical support for the defined event within the named three-cell evaluation.

Prohibited promotions
