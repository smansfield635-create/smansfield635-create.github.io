# H_EARTH_OCEAN_GRAPHICAL_EXPRESSION_PROOF_CONTRACT_v1

MODE: CONTRACT FREEZE · BOUNDED PROOF · NO PRODUCTION MUTATION
STATUS: FROZEN CANDIDATE CONTRACT
SCOPE: H-Earth Ground Cell 001 coastal-entry ocean expression only

## 1. Purpose

Determine whether the existing canonical H-Earth architecture can support a materially richer, continuous, living ocean presentation while preserving world identity, navigation, topology, shoreline truth, and the H-Earth Experience Anchor.

This contract authorizes no broad H-Earth graphics rewrite. It defines one bounded ocean-expression proof.

## 2. Controlling acceptance law

The pixel/cell substrate may remain structural authority but must cease to be the dominant perceptual language of the proof surface.

The proof passes only if the coastal ocean reads perceptually as one continuous environmental body rather than exposed cells, bands, tiles, or construction primitives.

## 3. Canonical source authorities preserved

The proof MUST consume existing authority and MUST NOT replace it.

- Canonical water truth: `h-earth-3d/environment/h-earth.water-state.js`
- Water presentation adapter: `showroom/globe/h-earth/render/environment-water.js`
- Environment/material descriptors and shared shoreline: `showroom/globe/h-earth/environment.js`
- Admitted geometry/frame authority: `showroom/globe/h-earth/admitted-geometry-frame.js`
- Live Run 8E GPU rendering lineage: `showroom/globe/h-earth/render/persistent-live-renderer.run8e-r3c*.js`
- Experience authority: `h-earth-3d/experience-anchor/H_EARTH_EXPERIENCE_ANCHOR_v1.json`

No proof implementation may create a second water truth, shoreline truth, world coordinate system, camera authority, traversal authority, or parallel H-Earth.

## 4. Bounded proof surface

The only perceptual target is the existing Ground Cell 001 coastal-entry water/shoreline view.

Required semantic objects remain:
- OBJ_005_SHORELINE_FOAM_LINE
- OBJ_006_NEARSHORE_WAVE_BAND
- OBJ_007_WATER_SURFACE_PLANE
- ZONE_002_SHORELINE_CONTACT_ZONE
- ZONE_003_WATER_SURFACE_ZONE

These identifiers remain semantic/world references. They must not become visibly separated graphical bands.

## 5. Required graphical channels

### A. Continuous water body
Open water, nearshore water, and shoreline contact must read as one continuous ocean extending coherently toward the horizon.

### B. Depth expression
Depth, turbidity, and water class may drive continuous optical/color response. Hard visible category boundaries are prohibited.

### C. Surface motion
Use deterministic multi-scale, low-amplitude surface response. A single obvious repeating sine band is insufficient. Motion must remain presentation-only and must not claim fluid-simulation authority.

### D. Optical response
Viewing angle, canonical/environmental light direction, depth, and distance may influence surface response. The proof should produce dimensional water without changing world geometry authority.

### E. Shore interaction
Foam, wetness, shallow-water response, and contact cues must derive from the existing environment-owned shared shoreline boundary. No alternate coastline may be introduced.

### F. Subsurface life cue
One restrained, presentation-only biological/environmental cue may be admitted in appropriate shallow water, such as submerged vegetation or reef-like structure with sparse motion. It must not create ecosystem simulation, gameplay, traversal, survival, or biological-state authority.

## 6. Pixel-exposure correction targets

The proof must explicitly test whether perceptual construction artifacts are reduced by graphical presentation rather than by replacing canonical geometry.

Candidate presentation concerns include:
- simple banded procedural water response;
- visibly repeated wave phase;
- insufficient depth-dependent optical variation;
- visible separation between open/nearshore/foam semantic geometry;
- nearest-neighbor presentation where it materially exposes pixel structure.

Any filtering change must be proven not to corrupt depth correctness, geometry identity, picking, diagnostics, or deterministic qualification.

## 7. Protected surfaces — mutation prohibited

The proof MUST NOT modify:
- canonical world topology;
- terrain coordinates or elevation authority;
- coastline/shoreline truth;
- planetary geometry;
- region definitions;
- camera authority;
- navigation/direct-manipulation authority;
- traversal permissions;
- swimming or fluid simulation;
- survival simulation;
- 256-state/cell architecture;
- H-Earth Experience Anchor;
- Audralia;
- Earth reference route;
- production deployment configuration.

No dashboard, map application, card UI, cinematic replacement, or disconnected scene may substitute for the navigable world.

## 8. Construction ownership

The proof is a renderer/presentation experiment.

Preferred mutation ownership is a new bounded presentation module and the minimum Run 8E renderer integration required to consume it.

Existing truth modules should remain read-only unless a separately proven defect makes a source correction unavoidable. Such a defect ends this admission and requires a fresh contract or explicit expansion.

## 9. Minimum-sufficient mutation law

Implementation must prefer:
1. one new bounded ocean graphical-expression module;
2. minimum live-renderer integration;
3. proof/qualification artifact(s);
4. Experience Anchor acceptance receipt.

Do not refactor unrelated renderer, compositor, controller, environment, terrain, navigation, vegetation, or world-state systems.

## 10. Determinism and performance

All procedural motion must derive from deterministic world position plus explicit render time/state inputs. No uncontrolled randomness.

The proof must:
- preserve persistent renderer/resource behavior;
- avoid per-frame shader/program/buffer/texture creation;
- preserve bounded GPU resource ownership;
- avoid CPU geometry regeneration per frame;
- retain mobile/tablet viability;
- provide a reduced-motion/static-safe expression when required by existing accessibility/runtime policy.

## 11. Qualification gates

Before human perceptual review, the immutable candidate must prove:

1. source-authority preservation;
2. unchanged shoreline/world topology;
3. unchanged navigation and camera authority;
4. unchanged traversal permissions;
5. no fluid/survival/ecosystem simulation claim;
6. deterministic expression inputs;
7. no per-frame resource leak or creation regression;
8. successful renderer compilation/link;
9. successful Ground Cell 001 render;
10. H-Earth Experience Anchor automated invariants remain eligible.

## 12. Perceptual acceptance criteria

The exact candidate proceeds to physical review only after automated qualification.

Human review asks:

- Does the ocean read as continuous rather than segmented?
- Are visible pixel/cell/band artifacts materially reduced?
- Does depth read from open water through shoreline without obvious category seams?
- Does motion look environmental rather than like a repeating shader trick?
- Does the shoreline remain geographically identical?
- Does H-Earth still feel like the same navigable world?
- Is the graphical improvement large enough to justify this rendering direction?

A merely different color palette is NOT a material improvement.

## 15. Kill conditions

Stop and REJECT the rendering approach if any of the following occurs:

- meaningful world/topology mutation is required merely to hide pixels;
- the proof requires a parallel ocean/world authority;
- shoreline continuity is degraded;
- navigation or camera behavior changes;
- mobile viability materially collapses;
- the ocean remains visibly banded/cellular after the bounded proof;
- the improvement is cosmetic rather than materially graphical;
- H-Earth no longer reads as the anchored same world.

- planet-wide rollout;
- terrain overhaul;
- atmosphere overhaul;
- marine ecosystem simulation;
- merge;
- deployment;
- publication.

Any expansion requires a subsequent admission using this proof as evidence.
