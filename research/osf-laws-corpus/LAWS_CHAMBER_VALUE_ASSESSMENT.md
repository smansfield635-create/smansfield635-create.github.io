## Governing boundary

This assessment determines **research value and placement**, not scientific truth. An OSF description can document a proposition, method, boundary, or intended test, but it does not by itself establish empirical confirmation. A project is not admitted as validated evidence unless its public record contains inspectable data, executed analysis, results, and sufficient provenance.

The corpus presently contributes substantial value in four areas:

1. canonical law language and scope boundaries;
2. falsification architecture and methodological discipline;
3. formal models and operational instruments;
4. applied examples and research programs requiring further evidence.

It presently contributes limited direct empirical support because most public resources expose descriptions without datasets, code, executed result tables, or replication artifacts.

## Recommended Laws Chamber structure

### A. Canonical law and custody layer

Use as direct source material for definitions, law statements, nonclaims, version custody, and falsification boundaries. Do not label these sources empirically validated.

### B. Methods and falsification layer

Use to show how claims are reduced, observed, tested, rejected, terminated, and compared. These materials materially strengthen the Laws Chamber because they expose how a law could fail rather than presenting only affirmative claims.

### C. Formalism and instrumentation layer

Use to present bounded mathematical objects, state spaces, quotient operations, diagnostic instruments, and execution gates. Mathematical claims require independent technical review before being described as proofs.

### D. Applied and empirical-candidate layer

Use as case-study designs, domain translations, or future empirical corridors. Do not treat declared pilots, stress tests, or validation statements as results unless the corresponding public evidence is present.

### E. Context or exclusion layer

Keep philosophical interpretation, roadmap material, and promotional claims outside the chamber’s direct evidentiary spine.
