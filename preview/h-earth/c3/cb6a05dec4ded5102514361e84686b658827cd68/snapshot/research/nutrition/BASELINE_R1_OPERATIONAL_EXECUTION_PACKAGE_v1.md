# PART B — PARTICIPANT INFORMATION / CONSENT DRAFT

**DRAFT FOR OVERSIGHT REVIEW — NOT FINAL CONSENT**

## B1. Study purpose

This pilot compares a defined pre-meal formulation called R1 with the same volume of plain water before a standardized breakfast.

The purpose is to determine whether R1 differs from matched water on measures including hunger, thirst, fullness, eating behavior, meal intake, gastrointestinal comfort, and post-meal comfort.

R1 is experimental for this research purpose.

---

## B2. What participation involves

A participant who completes the pilot would attend two controlled sessions.

At one session the participant receives R1 in 350 mL water.

At the other session the participant receives 350 mL plain water.

Order is assigned prospectively.

Both sessions use the same:

- overnight food-free interval,
- morning testing structure,
- beverage volume,
- waiting interval,
- standardized breakfast,
- questionnaires,
- meal measurement procedure.

---

## B3. Foreseeable burdens/risks

Potential burdens include:

- hunger from the overnight food-free interval,
- dislike of beverage taste,
- fullness,
- thirst,
- nausea,
- bloating,
- abdominal discomfort,
- headache,
- dizziness/lightheadedness,
- food intolerance/allergic reaction,
- effects related to sodium/potassium/mineral exposure in susceptible individuals.

The final consent must be revised by the applicable qualified reviewer to reflect the verified materials, study setting, and complete risk assessment.

---

## B4. Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary.

A participant may stop at any time.

Stopping participation does not require completing the meal or beverage.

---

## B5. Privacy

The analytic dataset should use participant codes rather than direct identifiers.

The final consent must state:

- what identifiable information is collected,
- who can access it,
- where it is stored,
- retention period,
- whether deidentified data may be reused/shared,
- applicable privacy protections.

---

## B6. Contacts

# PART E — WEIGHING / VOLUME SOP

## E1. Principle

Milligram quantities must be measured with equipment demonstrably suitable for the target mass and prospectively defined tolerance.

Kitchen-scale approximation is not acceptable.

---

## E2. Before use

- verify equipment identity,
- verify current calibration/verification status,
- place equipment on appropriate stable surface,
- confirm clean weighing vessel,
- zero/tare,
- prevent material cross-contamination,
- document environmental/operational issues affecting measurement.

---

## E3. Mass recording

For each material record:

- target mass,
- actual displayed mass,
- unit,
- operator,
- time,
- deviation if outside frozen tolerance.

### Critical open constant

Exact allowable weighing tolerances must be set after the actual equipment and material-handling system are selected and qualified.

Do not invent a tolerance before equipment capability is known.

---

## E4. Water volume

Use one qualified measurement method for both R1 and comparator.

Record:

- device,
- target 350 mL,
- actual volume,
- tolerance,
- operator.

Exact tolerance remains **OPEN pending device selection**.

# PART I — PILOT SAMPLE-SIZE RATIONALE FRAMEWORK

## I1. Scientific status

The pilot is exploratory and intended to estimate:

- feasibility,
- paired variability,
- response distribution,
- tolerability,
- candidate effect magnitude,
- useful endpoints for later work.

It is not currently supported by a validated R1 effect size or R1-specific minimal important difference.

---

## I2. Required pre-enrollment decision

Before recruitment, prospectively choose a target number of **completed evaluable pairs**.

The rationale should be based on pilot precision/feasibility rather than claiming confirmatory efficacy power.

A qualified statistician/methodologist should review the final target if the pilot is intended to support formal scientific conclusions or publication.
