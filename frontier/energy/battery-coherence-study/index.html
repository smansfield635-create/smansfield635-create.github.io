Applied empirical track · Battery degradation

Strong held-out signal. Universal proof withheld.

The frozen B/P/E/I/V representation was evaluated across 1,653 cell-disjoint held-out cycles from three unseen batteries: CS2_34, CS2_36, and CS2_38. The target was crossing below 80% retained capacity within the next 20 cycles.

Supported finding

The B/P/E/I/V representation contained strong transferable information about near-term physical degradation in batteries excluded from development. This is a nontrivial domain-specific held-out result.

Interpretation boundary

Burden was the strongest individual predictor. If burden largely represents cumulative cycle exposure or throughput, part of the performance may reflect conventional battery aging. Cycle number, cumulative throughput, current capacity, and recent degradation slope must be controlled before a distinct coherence mechanism can be claimed.
