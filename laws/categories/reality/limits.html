Required controls

Expose untested regimes, missing controls, transfer gaps, threshold sensitivity, external-validity gaps, confounds, replication status, and deployment prerequisites.

Failure behavior

Limits fail when goalposts move to preserve a preferred conclusion, uncertainty is hidden, or future possibility is written as established capability.

Reading layers

Practical, Engineering, and Empirical claims remain separate.

Practical reading

State what a person, operator, or organization must not assume from the result.

Engineering reading

Expose untested regimes, missing controls, transfer gaps, threshold sensitivity, and deployment prerequisites.

Empirical reading

Show replication status, external validity, negative findings, confounds, and evidence that remains absent.

Method

For every conclusion, state observed result, evidence-supported claim, unresolved inference, prohibited claim, and evidence required to move the ceiling.

Audit boundary

Certainty without bounds is narrative. A claim remains held at the strongest level directly supported by current evidence.
