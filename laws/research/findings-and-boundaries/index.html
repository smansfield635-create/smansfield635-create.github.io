Can a frozen battery representation rank a defined near-term event in cells excluded from development?

The practical question concerns early indication within a named battery domain. This Laws page interprets the bounded outcome; it does not reproduce the complete study or imply critical-system deployment.

Cell-disjoint held-out evidence was admitted.

The final evaluation contained 1,653 records from three complete cells excluded from development. Supporting, negative, mixed, and competing results remain distinct. Observation volume and provenance do not by themselves establish mechanism or transfer.

The representation and event were evaluated against ordinary baselines and component challenges.

The procedure froze the representation, separated development and final evaluation by complete cell, evaluated a fixed 80%-within-20-cycles event, and compared combined, raw, burden, H*, weakest-axis, and hard-threshold outcomes.

Strong bounded ranking survived; a distinct mechanism did not.

Claim boundary
