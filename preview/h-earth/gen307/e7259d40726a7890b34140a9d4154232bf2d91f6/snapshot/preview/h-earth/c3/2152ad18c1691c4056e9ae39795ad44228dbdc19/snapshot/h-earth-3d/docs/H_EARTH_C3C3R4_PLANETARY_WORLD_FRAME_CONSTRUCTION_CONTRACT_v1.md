## Dominant deficiency

`D4 — PLANAR_RECTANGULAR_WORLD_FRAME_FAILURE`

The prior environment improved local ocean, boundary, and terrain treatments while retaining a macro world frame that could still be perceived as a rectangular planar patch. C3C3R4 changes the visible-world architecture rather than hiding individual edges.

## Governing architecture

`LOCAL_NAVIGABLE_TANGENT_PATCH + SHARED_CURVED_PLANETARY_CONTINUATION + COMPOSED_OCCLUSION_AND_REVEALS`

### A. Protected local tangent patch

The existing H-Earth playable terrain, navigation, collision, canonical shoreline contact, and 16x16 perceptual cell structure remain unwarped. The shared planetary frame begins outside a protected world-space radius that fully contains the accessible local region.

### B. One shared planetary continuation authority

`showroom/globe/h-earth/render/planetary-world-frame.js` is the single curvature authority for visible continuation. OPEN_WATER and distant non-navigable land must both consume its exact contract identity. Separate per-edge curvature formulas are prohibited.

### C. Circular horizon planform

Distant south/west land continuation must approach circular planetary-horizon arcs rather than constant-X or constant-Z terminal lines. Far ocean geometry must descend through shared curvature strongly enough that its finite construction edge is not the perceived horizon.

### D. Composed landscape opportunities

Mountain passes, valleys, headlands, and former box corners are compositional opportunities. The owner-observed mountain/pass ocean reveal must be preserved and developed, not accidentally filled or hidden. A pass may frame distant ocean or curved world continuation while remaining non-authoritative for navigation outside H-Earth.

## Preservation laws

The successor must preserve:

- enlarged H-Earth navigable scale;
- canonical coast morphology and shoreline contact;
- north/east open-ocean identity;
- south/west connected-region identity;
- no navigation or collision expansion;
- world-locked sky/sun behavior;
- terrain palette;
- perceptual 16x16 terrain-cell depth without literal grid overlay;
- owner-positive C3C3 baseline features not contradicted by the later REPAIR_REQUIRED findings.

## Prohibited repairs

The following do not satisfy D4:

- painting or shading a curved horizon over planar geometry;
- viewport-fixed arcs;
- placing mountains or walls in front of rectangular edges;
- independent north/east/south/west terminal formulas;
- expanding the playable region merely to move the box farther away;
- flattening or erasing the local grid-depth language;
- declaring improvement from a camera/perspective change alone.

## Qualification law

`PRESERVED_BASELINE -> DEFINED_OBJECTIVE -> OBJECTIVE_MATERIAL_RENDERED_DIFFERENCE -> PRESERVATION_CHECK`

Perspective change does not count as improvement.

Machine qualification must establish from the actual assembled/rendered geometry:

1. the same planetary-frame contract is consumed by OPEN_WATER and distant land;
2. the accessible local corner and shoreline remain unwarped;
3. far-world sag is materially non-zero;
4. south/west far planforms are circular rather than straight rectangular terminals;
5. no distant-land rectangular terminal is retained as the intended horizon;
6. full-ring browser scanning preserves connected-region and open-ocean states;
7. terrain cell-depth preservation remains active;
8. navigation, collision, shoreline authority, and accessible-region authority do not expand;
9. exact-head evidence is uploaded;
10. an immutable same-domain owner inspection route is produced before any production merge.
