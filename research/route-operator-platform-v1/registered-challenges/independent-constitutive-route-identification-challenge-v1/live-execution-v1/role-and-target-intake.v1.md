# Independent Role and Primary-Target Intake v1

## Required independent roles

The following functions must be filled by separate qualified people or teams before target execution:

1. `SOURCE_ACQUISITION_CUSTODIAN`
2. `OUTCOME_CUSTODIAN`
3. `ANALYST_COORDINATOR`
4. `BLINDED_SCORING_TEAM`
5. At least four `CONSTITUTIVE_ROUTE_ANALYSTS`
6. At least four `GENERIC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH_ANALYSTS`

## Role exclusion rules

A person or team is ineligible for incompatible functions when it would permit access to hidden outcomes, analyst identities, theory-preferred maps, normalization decisions, or target scores before the applicable freeze.

The theory author, protocol author, and any model instance operating under their direction may prepare materials but may not be counted as an independent route analyst, comparator analyst, outcome custodian, or final blinded scorer.

## Primary-target intake

A primary candidate must be supplied by a system owner, authoritative source team, or genuinely independent custodian and must provide:

- system and domain identifier;
- authoritative pre-intervention technical documentation;
- a frozen documentation cutoff;
- observable components, relations, and specified capacities;
- at least one real documented intervention, fault, removal, replacement, or route change;
- affected and unaffected capability outcomes;
- a separate outcome package that analysts cannot access;
- evidence that no essential mechanism is undocumented;
- conflict and prior-knowledge disclosures;
- confirmation that the candidate was not selected because a known result favored IMI.

## Public-posting restriction

Do not post outcome details, repair results, post-cutoff diagrams, retrospective summaries, revealing filenames, proprietary material, credentials, or personal data in the public registration issue.
