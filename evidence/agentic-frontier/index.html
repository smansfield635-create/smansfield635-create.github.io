Meaning · Object 01

Same State. Different Future.

Relational Transition Geometry (RTG) tests exact-present-state, equal-resource intervention twins where only the placement of latent recovery authority changes, asking whether relational placement changes held-out viable future options. C-MAPSS prediction is a separate evidentiary track under the same broader question.

Question

Can present state look the same while the structure of what remains possible carries additional predictive or causal information?

Strongest result · three tracks kept separate

C-MAPSS · PREDICTIVE SIGNAL: FD003 RMSE 39.0971→26.2617 (+32.83%); FD004 50.1103→47.5843 (+5.04%). FD002 was OBJECT_FAILURE with no scientific outcome.

RTG · MECHANISM SUPPORTED: controlled equal-budget RTS24 intervention twin, 30 selected states, 29 nonzero CHALLENGE states, T = 0.5624591503267974, 95% CI [0.3326645453503039, 0.7558825450242793].

RTG · MECHANISM REPLICATED: Gen448 on independent system pglib_opf_case60_c, T = 0.21785206258890463, 95% CI [0.11979333549127599, 0.34360525563157146]; independent full-network recomputation agreed.

The frozen RTG statistic is D_i = sign(Delta_PROBE_i) * Delta_CHALLENGE_i; T = mean_i(D_i) across the 30 sealed states.

Standing

BOUNDED_EMPIRICAL predictive signal · MECHANISM_SUPPORTED · MECHANISM_REPLICATED. Earlier NOT_IDENTIFIABLE history remains preserved.

Why it matters

Present condition alone can miss information carried by the geometry of available transitions. Prediction, mechanism, and replication answer different questions and do not pool.

Same broad question. Separate experimental contracts and claim ceilings.

Meaning · Object 02

What Has to Be True First?

Structural Admissibility Science (SAS) formalizes prerequisite conditions that must hold before downstream ranking, scoring, or interpretation; strength elsewhere cannot compensate for a blocked prerequisite.

Question

Are some conditions gates that must be satisfied before comparison is allowed to begin?

Strongest result

A formal noncompensatory admissibility object and bounded diagnostic program survive, but the corrective physical heldout did not distinguish SAS from the strongest comparator because both had zero false admissions. Net6 endpoint disagreement 0/510; Net3 2/22; Anytown 2–4/17.

Standing

METHODOLOGICAL_DIAGNOSIS + NOT_DISTINGUISHED.

Why it matters

A high score elsewhere cannot repair an unmet prerequisite. The null is part of the result, not a problem to retune away.

The later positive C-MAPSS predictive experiment has no authorized evidentiary binding to the SAS proposition.

Meaning · Object 03

When Structure Holds—or Breaks

Coherence Geometry is a formal program for asking how properties assigned to a whole depend on relational structure and orientation under explicitly defined model classes.

Question

Can individually strong parts fail to produce a coherent whole when required relationships or orientation fail?

Strongest result

Formal proof and counterexample results remain intact. Separately, Orientation v2 selected orientation at N=11 and retained the selected direction on untouched N=12; each retained 34 positive, 4 negative, and 2 zero-margin selected classes, and 4,718,592 observer checks passed.

Standing

FORMAL_PROOF + FORMAL_COUNTEREXAMPLE + FORMAL_SYNTHETIC orientation successor. Gen430 remains NOT_IDENTIFIABLE.

Why it matters

Relational organization can be a distinct formal object, but a selected direction inside a synthetic class is not automatically a physical law.

The v2 successor is a new prospective experiment, not a reinterpretation of Gen430.

Meaning · Object 04

When Is a Claim Allowed to Count?

Scientific Claim Entitlement is a deterministic operating method for distinguishing what an admitted evidence state permits one to claim from stronger conclusions it does not permit. ADMISSIBLE means entitled under the frozen rules, not true.

Question

What exact conclusion is an evidence state allowed to support?

Strongest result

Four frozen external histories/classes were represented with 0/4 normalization disagreements, 0/4 core semantic changes, and 0/4 unregistered claim classes. Separately, the whole-audit quotient is countably infinite and the pointed local quotient has five classes with minimum three bits; 256 is sufficient but not minimal.

Standing

OPERATING_METHOD plus separately bounded formal-computation results; EXTERNAL_FOUR_CLASS_AUDIT_SURVIVES.

Why it matters

Overclaim often happens after a valid observation, when mechanism, causation, scope, or generality is silently enlarged.

Entitlement limits a conclusion; it does not manufacture truth.

Meaning · Object 05

Can the Agent Actually Do the Work?

Agentic Frontier evaluates completed, independently checked task work under frozen configurations rather than verbal plausibility alone.

Question

Can an agent complete verified work under a known definition of done, not merely describe how it might be done?

Strongest result

Frozen 24-task Study 1: Diamond Gate verified completions 10/24; tested OpenHands configuration 0/24. DG-only completions occurred across all four preregistered strata. Comparator end-to-end tool-loop health remained unresolved.

Standing

Study 1 CONFIGURATION_BOUNDED. Study 2 PREREGISTERED_NOT_EXECUTED with zero opened outcomes.

Why it matters

Tools, environment, verification, and task definition can materially affect observed completion, so the result belongs to the tested configuration.

Pre-experimental transport failure is not counted as an OpenHands scientific failure.

Meaning · Object 06

Do the Safeguards Earn Their Cost?

Canonical Control Kernel is a repository-operational control architecture testing whether particular state, sequencing, and recovery safeguards measurably improve bounded operational control.

Question

Which safeguards actually change an operational result, and which merely add ceremony?

Strongest result

Within one bounded repository-operation profile, 256, NEWS, and ACK_PACK were OPERATIONALLY_DISTINGUISHED; PSALM was NOT_DISTINGUISHED; Fibonacci host utility was UNEVALUABLE. The composed operational architecture was distinguished.

Standing

OPERATING_METHOD with mixed component dispositions.

Why it matters

More controls can add both protection and burden. Complexity should not receive credit merely for existing.

Operational distinction is not scientific validation.

Meaning · Object 07

Can Someone Else Pick Up the Work?

Transfer / succession / reproduction frontier asks whether exact state, evidence boundaries, failures, unresolved questions, and lawful continuation can be recovered without the creator's private conversational memory.

Question

Can another operator recover what happened, what evidence exists, what failed, what remains unresolved, and what may happen next?

Strongest result

Bounded internal workflows preserve exact-head identities, durable receipts, negative/null/unresolved states, frozen task/protocol identities, and enough durable state for exact-state succession and recovery.

Standing

OPERATING_METHOD · internal evidence only.

Why it matters

Dependence on one operator's private memory is a fragile continuity model and makes scientific and operational custody harder to audit.

Internal succession is evidence of custody mechanics, not external reproduction.

Evidence · Object 01

Same State. Different Future.

Evidence class

C-MAPSS: BOUNDED_EMPIRICAL predictive signal. RTG: controlled mechanism support plus independent-system prospective replication. Earlier NOT_IDENTIFIABLE history remains separate.

Claim ceiling

C-MAPSS establishes bounded predictive utility under its frozen heterogeneity attack. RTG establishes a bounded controlled relational effect and independent-system replication under frozen power-system semantics. No pooled or universal claim is authorized.

Novelty / prior art

The exact operationalization and bounded observations may be distinctive. Transition systems, resilience, intervention design, graph structure, prediction, and causal controls have substantial prior art. Broader priority remains unresolved.

Provenance

Recovered RTG/LVTG lineage documents development from state and trajectory through constraint, admissibility, viability, and finite relational geometry. Carries forward: conceptual development. Does not carry forward: later empirical support, exact identity, or priority.

Proof path

METHOD / PUBLIC CLAIM RECORD · CURRENT RESULT RECORD · INSPECT CROSSWALK

Independent-laboratory reproduction is not claimed. Current public exits support inspection of the bounded record and method lineage, not a claim that every execution artifact is publicly recomputable.

Prediction ≠ mechanism ≠ replication. Provenance ≠ evidence.
