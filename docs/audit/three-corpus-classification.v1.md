# Three-Corpus Classification Standard

Repository audit and migration material must be classified into three linked but nonmerged domains.

## 1. Legacy Evidence Corpus

Use for what existed, was observed, was deployed, was specified, or was recovered from the legacy diagnostic track.

Recovered semantic content and verified original physical bytes are separate evidence statuses. Recovery of content does not prove original bytes. Failure to verify original bytes does not erase recovered content.

Generalized legacy incompleteness is nonblocking. A blocking claim requires the exact missing artifact/evidence, exact unresolved dependency, affected replacement surface, reason available evidence cannot resolve it, minimum recovery/authority decision required, and proper specialist/governing support.

## 2. Governing Invariant and Transition Corpus

Use for what must survive, may change, must be eliminated, must be migrated, or must be decided.

Completed specialist determinations, noncompensation laws, claim ceilings, semantic bindings, transition obligations, and supersession history belong here.

## 3. Replacement Target Corpus

Use for new target contracts, source artifacts, fixtures, execution evidence, repository state, deployment state, and release evidence.

Replacement artifacts are distinct from legacy artifacts by default.

## Identity and lineage

Equality of name, path, digest, role, function, or authority family does not establish institutional identity. Identity continuity requires an explicit authority decision or explicit lineage edge.

Useful lineage relations include `REPLACED_BY`, `IMPLEMENTS_INVARIANT_OF`, `PRESERVES_BEHAVIOR_OF`, `PRESERVES_PUBLIC_CONTRACT_OF`, `MIGRATES_STATE_FROM`, `INTENTIONALLY_DIVERGES_FROM`, `CONSOLIDATES`, `SPLITS`, `DEPRECATES`, `RETAINS_HISTORY_OF`, `SUPERSEDES`, `SUPERSEDED_BY`, `DERIVED_FROM`, `EMBEDS`, `PROJECTS`, `HASH_OF`, `CORRECTS`, `CANONICAL_ENCODING_OF`, `PHYSICAL_INSTANCE_OF`, `EXECUTION_OF`, and `DEPLOYMENT_OF`.

## Nonreopening rule

Reclassification does not reopen or readjudicate a completed substantive determination. Prior classifications remain retained history when later evidence justifies reclassification.

## Authority boundary

Classification alone creates no construction, repository mutation, fixture execution, conformance, production, publication, or release authority.
