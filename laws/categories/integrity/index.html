Track map

  • Consistency: same rule, comparable condition, comparable result.
  • Accountability: traceable ownership and consequence.
  • Continuity: preserved identity across time and handoff.
  • Coherence: claims and records do not contradict one another.

Relationship boundary

The battery relationship is admitted for Consistency, Continuity, and Coherence. Accountability remains a conceptual track and gains no study evidence merely by belonging to the family.

Reading layers

Practical, Engineering, and Empirical readings

Practical reading

Ask whether the warning means the same thing when the held-out cell changes.

Engineering reading

Preserve cell-disjoint separation, feature definitions, observation ordering, custody, and comparator identity.

Empirical reading

Inspect whether performance survives held-out cells and whether conventional aging burden explains more than the combined model.

Procedure

Cell identity, held-out separation, feature definitions, observation order, comparator identity, and custody must remain stable before cross-cell consistency is interpreted.

Failure behavior

Changing identity or measurement rules across cells breaks consistency.

Failure behavior

Untraceable substitutions break continuity and coherence.

Audit method

Integrity closes only when the same declared procedure can be traced across the separated records without silently changing identity, criteria, or evidence standing.
