# Scientific boundary

## What this package can establish

- Whether the theory's operational distinction survives contact with externally authored software implementations.
- Whether relation-aware features improve held-out prediction on selected third-party industrial datasets under a frozen analysis.
- Whether output continuity and additive capacity are empirically inadequate comparators in these bounded systems.
- Whether the same analysis is reproducible from an exact repository head.

## What this package cannot establish

- Independent human recovery of constitutive relations.
- Independence of the analysis team from the theory team.
- Universal validity across natural and social systems.
- A discovery claim from the exploratory software bridge.
- Causal constitutive structure from observational UCI datasets alone.

The UCI phase is predictive and discriminative. It is not a direct intervention experiment and may return a negative or mixed result without falsifying the full parent theory.
