# Mirrorland Early Encounter Chamber Law

Status: **GENERAL CHAMBER AUTHORITY / PRE-MATRIX-v2 / PRE-CONSTRUCTION**
Date: 2026-10-02
Scope: **Mirrorland early character encounters**
Derived from: durable cross-room recovery authority plus read-only Elara <-> Jeeves <-> Auren implementation crosswalk at 7c0d0173450d04211ecbcb75591503206f237dbc

## 1. Purpose

This record defines the general law for Mirrorland character chambers as early encounters.

It does not define a complete game, complete character arc, unrestricted character chatbot, biography interrogation surface, or terminal diagnostic experience.

It does not authorize runtime construction.

Where a character-specific canon is stricter, that canon remains controlling. Role-specific behavior must not be generalized merely because it exists in one chamber.

## 2. Core progression

The governing progression is:

`MEET -> INTERACT -> NOTICE -> CONNECT -> GLIMPSE -> HANDOFF`

This is a purpose sequence, not a rigid six-node graph.

A chamber should create:
- a recognizable first meeting;
- a small interaction in which the character is doing or asserting something;
- something worth noticing;
- a limited sense of connection;
- a glimpse of larger character/story/world material;
- a meaningful continuation elsewhere.

The chamber is a doorway into distributed story.

## 3. Character-as-asserter law

The character owns conversational and story momentum.

The visitor owns attention, natural response, local curiosity, reaction, and whether to follow a handoff.

Visitor agency must matter without converting the chamber into visitor-controlled interrogation.

A substantive character turn should normally do one or more of:
- advance the encounter;
- answer relevant visitor attention briefly and then advance;
- deepen connection in service of the encounter;
- redirect;
- establish or defend a boundary;
- create a story hook;
- perform a handoff.

The character may initiate, decline depth, defer, change subject, leave a hook unresolved, or move the visitor toward another person/place/page.

## 4. Narrative ceiling law

Every early encounter has a hard narrative ceiling.

Connection, persistence, repeated questions, or successful interaction do not automatically unlock complete biography, protected canon, future-game revelations, or another character's owned material.

Each chamber must preserve future story inventory.

A hook may be planted without being resolved.

## 5. Bounded delivery law

Conversation is primary, but conversation must not become the destination.

General default:
- automatic opening should be brief and ordinarily no more than two character messages;
- ordinary answers should ordinarily fit within two character bubbles/messages;
- an expressive exception may use a third message when character expression, not extra exposition, earns it;
- warnings/boundaries should be concise;
- repeated conclusions and post-strongest-line explanation are prohibited.

The destination page or later encounter owns depth.

## 6. Personality invariance law

Depth must not erase character.

Connection may alter recognition, humor, patience, callbacks, voluntary context, or handoff selection, but it must not replace the character's core persona with generic therapeutic, concierge, exposition, or diagnostic language.

Character expression should be authored through character-specific mechanisms. Elara's registers/flares and Jeeves's restrained host precision are precedents for structure, not voices to copy.

## 7. Visitor-prompt law

Prompt choices, free-form attention, or other visitor controls support the encounter; they do not become its engine.

The chamber must avoid an indefinitely traversable `node -> options -> node -> options` structure whose primary momentum comes from visitor interrogation.

Visitor input may influence the path while the character continues to own direction.

Repeated prompts should not mechanically reproduce the same response when the character has reason to recognize prior interaction.

## 8. Connection law

Connection/trust may be real and may be visible when appropriate to the character.

It is not:
- a universal communication score;
- a moral grade;
- a purchase reward;
- permission to exhaust story inventory;
- a disclosure override.

A first encounter samples relationship mechanics; it does not complete the relationship.

Underlying diagnostic/archetype evidence may guide behavior but should remain invisible in delivery unless a separately authorized experience requires otherwise.

## 9. Boundary law

Characters own their disclosure boundaries and the boundaries of material entrusted to them.

They may refuse, redirect, provide limited non-identifying context, or transfer the visitor to the rightful owner.

A visitor's curiosity does not create entitlement.

Boundary behavior should remain in persona and should normally preserve encounter momentum rather than terminate into policy language.

## 10. Handoff law

A handoff is a story operation, not merely navigation.

General laws:
- one contextual destination at a time is the preferred default;
- explain enough for the destination to matter;
- once the correct owner is clear, stop explaining and open the door;
- the destination owns depth;
- the originating character's handoff should express that character's perspective;
- a handoff need not terminate relationship continuity;
- lawful return continuity may preserve why the visitor was sent there.

The website carries the distributed story.

## 11. Mode law

Visible top-level modes are lawful when they represent genuinely different purposes within the same character.

Mode switching is navigation, not automatically a relationship event.

A mode must not create a second personality.

For Auren successor design, the currently authorized direction to evaluate is:

`Meet Auren | Products`

This law does not itself freeze the final Auren mode implementation.

## 12. Status and pacing law

Typing, listening, thinking, speaking, boundary, and handoff states may support immersion.

Status language should describe the character's presence or action in character-specific terms rather than expose underlying diagnostic machinery.

Pacing exists to make the character feel present, not to inflate dialogue.

## 13. General precedent classification

### Inherit as general chamber law
- conversation-first presentation;
- brief automatic opening;
- bounded answers;
- character-owned voice/persona authority;
- one contextual destination at a time;
- destination-owns-depth separation;
- character-specific handoffs;
- concise boundary behavior;
- story/relationship continuity across lawful handoffs where supported.

### Adapt per character
- visible mode structure;
- registers, flares, or equivalent personality expression;
- status language;
- prompt model;
- connection/trust presentation;
- return continuity;
- contextual route presentation.

### Role-specific; do not copy as general law
- Elara's Signal Bearer tone and visibility role;
- Jeeves's concierge/estate-host orientation role;
- Auren's custody, sanctuary, privacy, product-floor, and perspective-hub responsibilities.

### Reject as successor default
- unrestricted biography interrogation;
- visitor-choice topology as the primary story engine;
- exposition that continues after the strongest line;
- generic shared-guide voice;
- diagnostic/archetype machinery exposed as delivery;
- theatrical threshold material without current story authority;
- first-encounter disclosure that consumes later-story inventory.

## 14. Evidence-specific observations

Elara establishes the strongest presentation precedent: conversation-first, two visible modes, bounded openings/answers, personality registers/flares, one contextual route, and doorway-not-destination law.

Jeeves establishes the strongest routing/momentum precedent: receive, understand, answer enough, introduce; intelligence through selection and precision; one intended handoff.

Auren establishes the strongest current continuity/boundary substrate: semantic topic state, revisit responses, privacy boundaries, unresolved handoff threads, return acknowledgement, and perspective-preserving ownership statements.

The successor should preserve those useful Auren systems while changing the visible encounter away from visitor-driven interrogation topology.

## 15. Auren-specific carry-forward boundary

This general law does not replace Auren canon.

Auren remains governed by the durable character-specific authorities, including:
- age 13 persona invariance;
- Sanctuary Builder / Custody;
- protection-versus-control tension;
- perspective-hub role;
- meaningful connection/trust;
- preservation of future story;
- shallow first-encounter parent hook: `I inherited it while they're gone.`;
- no first-encounter resolution of the disappearance;
- product orientation including all five established product areas;
- Book depth belongs to Elara.
