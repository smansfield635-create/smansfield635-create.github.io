# Fort Worth Area Job Fair — NTTA Booth Research
Date: 2026-09-30
Employer: North Texas Tollway Authority (NTTA)
Booth: 16
Status: field research preserved

## Key correction
Do not evaluate NTTA from a short first-page slice of openings. Current research identified roughly 90 openings and materially different opportunities outside the initial maintenance-heavy subset.

## Degree-access finding
NTTA is not universally degree-locked. A current Vendor Manager posting explicitly accepts "Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience" plus related experience. Relevant functions include governance models, performance monitoring/auditing, process improvement, SOPs and KPIs.

## Relevant organizational territory
Enterprise Systems / IT: governance, automation, applications, service management, operational controls, audit readiness and AI.
ITS: hands-on Intelligent Transportation Systems configuration, deployment, monitoring, maintenance, troubleshooting and testing.
QA/QC: current research identified quality-assurance work involving AI and automation.
Project/operations: software projects, risk/issues, change control, security, documentation, business analysis and PMO improvement.

## Credential boundary
Some senior technology roles remain strongly credential-gated. Current examples researched include Assistant Director and IT Project Manager positions requiring bachelor's degrees plus substantial experience. Use these roles to identify the relevant organization, not necessarily as immediate application targets.

## Accessible-rung strategy
Ask the recruiter to search specifically across IT, ITS, QA/QC, systems, project operations and process-improvement roles where a bachelor's degree is not a hard requirement or equivalent experience is accepted.

## Booth language
"I saw you have about 90 openings, and I don't think the maintenance positions on the first page are where I fit. My background is unusual: I've independently built a substantial AI-assisted software platform, and I've spent a lot of time on operational governance, automation, quality control, traceability and project execution.

I also noticed NTTA has positions where equivalent experience can substitute for a degree.

Could we look specifically at your IT, ITS, QA/QC, systems, project operations or process-improvement positions where the bachelor's degree isn't a hard requirement?"

## Material conclusion
NTTA is a worthwhile booth conversation. The relevant question is not whether NTTA has technical departments; it does. The field objective is to identify an accessible rung into those departments based on demonstrated experience and current degree-equivalency rules.
