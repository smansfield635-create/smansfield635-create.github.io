Paths Not Yet Open.

Mirrorland does not end by closing the world. It ends by showing which thresholds come next.

The Glass showed you that questions carry position, pressure, fear, and desire. Now the route answers your desire for access with a sealed threshold. Several paths stand ahead. Their glyphs pulse. Their roads are visible. Some were closed because you were not ready to mistake access for authority. Now Mirrorland does not open every door. It reveals the next unfinished paths and asks you to choose where to continue with memory, restraint, and orientation intact.

Why These Paths Were Closed

Desire is not readiness.

West does not punish curiosity. It tests whether curiosity can survive responsibility.

01 · The Player Needed Orientation

Access would have outpaced form.

The Window gave sight, the Diamond tested form, the Stars taught orientation, and the Glass exposed the asker. The seal waited until those lessons could hold together.

A path should not open faster than the one crossing it can remain whole.
02 · The World Needed Protection

Opening can damage what it reveals.

Audralia remains unfinished. Hearth remains a working facility. H-Earth remains a ground-level approach. Each path can reveal more than the player is prepared to interpret.

A world under development is not obligated to become a doorway.
03 · The System Needed Return

A route must survive its own opening.

Passage is not a line on a map. It is a system event. Entry, memory, consequence, return, authority, and failure path must remain coherent before the threshold can release.

In Audralia, hope is not a plan. Every repair must survive its own failure path.
The Gate’s Correction

You thought the closed paths meant refusal.

The gate does not erase the future. It distinguishes directions that must not be collapsed into one doorway.
