## Method

All 64 live items were re-read individually. This pass does not inherit prior KEEP decisions. Each item was reconsidered against eight checks:

1. prompt grammaticality/naturalness;
2. keyed-answer semantic correctness;
3. distractor grammatical plausibility;
4. distractor semantic plausibility;
5. single-construct focus;
6. English-dominant rather than reasoning-dominant demand;
7. relative difficulty fit inside its assigned D1-D8 band;
8. usefulness for adaptive placement rather than mere classroom practice.

Verdicts: KEEP / REWORD / RELEVEL / REBUILD.

## Difficulty-band findings

### D1
All eight items are fit for purpose. The band is intentionally easy and tests high-frequency lexical/functional recognition.

### D2
Seven items are sound. s1e still gains too much discrimination from obviously malformed alternatives and should be tightened.

### D3
The core morphology is useful. s2b and s2g are not clean usage tests because their alternatives mix sentence use, definitions, and nonsense. s3c/s3g need narrower form-matched distractors.

### D4
This band still contains the most obvious escaped legacy defect: s3b retains fake forms (workedly/workness/workful). s3h is also too easy for the band. Sentence-order items are substantially stronger.

### D5
Grammar items are credible, but the comprehension half is underpowered. Umbrella, pharmacy, refrigerate, and the Tuesday→Thursday schedule change are easier than the grammar items and compress the intended difficulty gradient.

### D6
The band is internally split. s6a, s7a, and s7f are genuine D6-style connected/formal comprehension. Fragile, appointment, refund, before-2-PM, and intervals are substantially easier.

### D7
Upper paraphrase quality is improved, but several items remain transparent enough to belong lower: postponed, damaged/although, extended. s8a is now a credible D7 grammar item.

### D8
This is now the strongest and most internally coherent band. Seven items are defensible at advanced reading/paraphrase demand. s7d (unless/except) is useful but not comparably difficult and should move down or be hardened.

## Systemic findings

1. The bank is not yet psychometrically monotonic. Cardinality is balanced 8×8, but content difficulty is not.
2. Earlier repairs improved wording faster than they improved calibration. Several RELEVEL items were context-expanded without becoming genuinely harder.
3. Distractor quality is much better than the original bank, but four items still need direct distractor repair and three require full rebuild.
4. The strongest placement items use grammatical/register/semantic contrasts where every option is plausible English.
5. The weakest items can be solved through obvious vocabulary, obvious malformed English, or direct extraction rather than level-appropriate comprehension.
6. No evidence from this source alone establishes empirical difficulty. D1-D8 remain expert-calibrated strata until learner-response data exists.
