Engineering Evidence

Make the claim. Show the boundary.

Diamond Gate separates current public evidence from historical public evidence. That distinction became necessary on August 12, 2026, when a P0 retirement adoption intentionally replaced a tranche of public governance implementations with fail-closed retirement handoffs.

Retirement does not erase what was demonstrated. It changes what we are allowed to claim in the present tense.

Proof Levels

Five different things can be true about a technical claim.

A claim also carries a time-state: current or historical. Proof level answers how strongly the mechanism was demonstrated; time-state answers whether that public implementation is still active.

Level 1Observable

The implementation or live behavior can be inspected.

Level 2Enforced

Executable machinery rejects a prohibited state or operation.

Level 3Tested

Automated qualification or self-tests exercise the mechanism.

Level 4Benchmarked

Quantitative results exist under a defined protocol.

Level 5Externally Validated

Independent customers, evaluators, organizations or market outcomes provide evidence.
