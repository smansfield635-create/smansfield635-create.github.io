# Laws Chamber Graphic Engineering Integration

Status: coordinated research prototype. No production Laws mutation is authorized.

This program binds three bounded workstreams:

- **Compass experience** — the real visitor story and the primary delivery target.
- **Graphic-engineering laboratory** — mechanics, rendering, motion, fallback, and lifecycle testing.
- **Governance and verification** — continuous proof that routes, records, evidence status, accessibility, and the protected Compass runtime remain unchanged.

The first vertical slice is `RESEARCH_COMES_F.I.R.S.T._RELATIONSHIP`.

The real content relationship is frozen as follows:

1. Flow asks what moves.
2. Integrity asks what stays true.
3. Reality asks what still holds when exposed to evidence and limits.
4. Structure asks what can hold the relationship together.
5. Test asks what may advance through explicit admission and audit.
6. Research is the examination and support layer. It sources, challenges, records, and preserves status across the complete sequence. It is not a sixth law and does not upgrade evidence.

The laboratory and Compass prototype consume the same shared modules for:

- lens state;
- full, reduced, and static motion modes;
- WebGL2 scene behavior;
- DOM label synchronization;
- responsive resizing;
- offscreen and hidden-document suspension;
- static SVG equivalence;
- evidence-boundary receipts.

The accepted Compass is rendered from `/laws/` in a same-origin isolated frame. The protected runtime files are not copied, modified, or replaced. The prototype hides the duplicate top route directory inside the frame only for research presentation; it does not mutate the production page.

No component may move to `assets/laws-expressive/` until it receives a later explicit admission.
