History

  1. Origins

    Practical
    History is not nostalgia. It is the carried record of how a system formed, changed, crossed thresholds, and preserved or lost its governing constraints. Origins: where the coherence constraint first appears.
    Engineering
    In engineering terms, History maps to version lineage, release history, architecture evolution, migration boundaries, milestone receipts, and constraint inheritance. Track when a constraint was introduced.
    Empirical
    For History requires recoverable records across formation, transition, milestone, and lineage. A story is not enough if the system cannot recover what changed. Strong evidence: version records, milestone artifacts, release notes, source-bound history.
