Reality · Theory, evidence, measure, limits

The result survives holdout. Its strongest rival explanation remains open.

This chamber separates what the battery study proposed, what it observed, how it was measured, and where the inference must stop.

Theory

The B/P/E/I/V representation should contain transferable information about approaching physical degradation. The test allowed individual equations and thresholds to fail.

Evidence

1,653 held-out evaluation cycles from CS2_34, CS2_36, and CS2_38, all excluded from development.

Measure

Axis AUROC 0.9394 against a raw physical baseline of 0.7850. Burden alone reached 0.9704.

Limits

Burden may encode age or accumulated use. External replication and conventional degradation controls remain incomplete.

Theory realization

Near-term target: crossing below 80% retained capacity within 20 cycles. Evaluation was cell-disjoint rather than cycle-random.

Evidence design

The unseen-cell split supports transfer within the tested CS2 battery family. It does not establish chemistry-wide or universal transfer.
