# H-Earth C3C3R5 D11 Macro Composition — Ocean Reveal v1

## Purpose

D11 turns the already-corrected deep spatial stack into deliberate landscape composition. It does not invent another ocean, another world frame, or another boundary patch.

The owner identified an earlier accidental composition in which ocean could be seen between mountain forms. This document converts that missed opportunity into an explicit construction objective.

## Geographic constraint

The canonical open-ocean exposures remain north and east. South and west remain connected-region continuation. Therefore the reveal must not punch a false ocean through the continental-interior boundary.

The selected composition is an **east-facing mountain-pass corridor** through the existing Run 8 successor mountain language, opening visually toward the already-existing eastern ocean.

## Construction authority

The frozen Run 8A mountain law is preserved as predecessor truth.

The bounded composition is applied in the Run 8B successor terrain field:

`h-earth-3d/terrain/h-earth.successor-terrain-field.run8b.js`

Contract:

`H_EARTH_C3C3R5_MACRO_COMPOSITION_EAST_OCEAN_REVEAL_v1`

## Composition law

The existing mountain contribution may be reduced only inside a bounded east-facing corridor centered near z=-254.

The operation:

- begins gradually from x=-112;
- reaches full composition strength around x=-36;
- terminates at the eastern mountain exit near x=64;
- has an 18-world-unit z sigma centered on the existing valley/pass language;
- removes at most 24 world units;
- removes at most 78% of the source mountain contribution at any point;
- never cuts the underlying base terrain below the pre-mountain surface;
- recomputes successor normals, slope and curvature from the post-composition elevation field.

This is a pass/saddle operation, not a trench, tunnel, artificial portal, or detached scenery mask.

## Preservation law

The operation must not:

- mutate Run 6 terrain;
- mutate the frozen Run 8A predecessor law;
- create ocean where ocean does not already exist;
- expand navigation authority;
- expand collision authority;
- alter the local-to-planet transform;
- remove the 16x16 perceptual terrain-depth language;
- reintroduce a fake mountain barricade.

## Objective

From appropriate ordinary owner viewpoints, the eastern mountain/valley structure should deliberately frame existing open ocean beyond the landform, producing depth and world continuity rather than an accidental wall.

The objective is not satisfied merely because a numeric cut exists. D12 must establish a material rendered reveal.

## Corner / edge law

Former rectangular corner sectors remain subject to the planetary-continuation composition law. They must resolve through some combination of coastline wrap, headland, valley, distant silhouette, atmospheric occlusion, or planetary fall-away. Rectangular terminal perception remains a failure.

## Qualification

Machine qualification must establish:

- the corridor exists in the Run 8B successor field;
- the cut is bounded to mountain contribution;
- post-composition normals are recomputed from post-composition elevation;
- predecessor terrain identities remain unchanged;
- D10 current live-package custody includes the distant planetary continuation;
- the exact candidate reaches the live renderer.

Visual qualification must establish:

- an actual ocean reveal is visible between/along mountain forms;
- the reveal points toward the existing eastern ocean rather than a fabricated southern/western water body;
- former corners no longer dominate perception as rectangular limits;
- grid/depth remains legible;
- color remains spatially varied without flattening topology;
- globe perception materially improves against the owner baseline.

## Status

D11 construction is implemented but not visually accepted by this document. D12 remains required before owner inspection or production merge.
