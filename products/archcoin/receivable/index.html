Plane 01 · Identity

Incoming value gains a distinct protocol identity.

ARCR / Overview

Domain role

ARCR identifies the Receivable domain inside the four-asset architecture. It is associated with value expected to arrive, its recognition, its collection path, and the evidence that supports it.

Asset identity

Name: ARCHCOIN Receivable. Symbol: ARCR. Standard: ERC-20 on the Ethereum EVM family, using 18 decimal places.

Present claim boundary

ARCR is implemented and locally qualified, but it is not represented here as a deployed cryptocurrency, a receivable, or a right to collect money. No Sepolia address exists yet.

Plane 02 · Construction

Incoming-value identity without hidden issuance authority.

ARCR / Engineering

Implementation

The shared ArchcoinFixedSupply.sol constructor instantiates ARCR through Solidity 0.8.28 and OpenZeppelin Contracts 5.4.0 ERC20, with a nonzero recipient and positive supply.

Fixed-supply behavior

Supply is minted once during construction. There is no external mint, public burn, owner role, pause control, or privileged balance mutation after deployment.

Qualification

Hardhat 2.28.0 and Toolbox 6.1.0 compile and test ARCR. The local EVM run deploys it, verifies its symbol and supply, signs a transfer, and confirms total supply remains unchanged.

Supply boundary

The one-million-unit local fixture proves behavior only. It is not a frozen Sepolia issuance quantity, and the future recipient wallet remains undecided.

Plane 03 · Traversal

From local receipt proof toward public testnet evidence.

ARCR / Platform

Current surface

ARCR has repository source, compiler configuration, tests, a local deployment harness, and signed-transfer evidence. The current result is locally qualified implementation.

Next network

Ethereum Sepolia, chain ID 11155111, is next. A funded wallet, frozen supply, chosen recipient, and explicit deployment authority are still required.

Evidence after deployment

The platform may display an address only after the deployment transaction and bytecode are retrievable and ERC-20 behavior has been independently qualified on-chain.

Mainnet remains later

Mainnet is not authorized. A locally working receivable-domain token does not prove a production asset or any external right to payment.

Plane 04 · Constraint

Protocol identity is not a claim on someone else.

ARCR / Governance

Immutable token constraints

  • No post-deployment mint.
  • No public burn in version one.
  • No privileged token mutation.
  • No mainnet before testnet qualification.

Holder-rights boundary

ARCR does not create a debt, collection right, payment entitlement, redemption promise, equity position, ownership interest, or claim against a receivable.

Truthful public status

The supported statement is that ARCR’s implementation and local transaction path are qualified. Network deployment and external economic meaning remain unclaimed.
