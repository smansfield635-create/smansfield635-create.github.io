A beginner can enter, select help language, complete adaptive placement, receive a valid starting point, enter real level-appropriate teaching, Learn, Practice, Check, receive feedback/remediation, progress, and use the experience on phone/tablet/desktop without clipping or inaccessible controls.

## Execution law

Once an authorized step begins, continue automatically through every downstream step that is lawful from returned evidence.

Do not stop for:
- successful qualification;
- successful merge;
- successful preflight;
- workflow-dispatch materialization;
- a run expected to complete quickly;
- creation of a subsequent carrier;
- ordinary bounded repair of a failed assertion.

When a workflow is dispatched:
1. resolve the actual workflow run ID;
2. poll/watch it through terminal state during the same execution window when feasible;
3. on PASS, immediately execute the next authorized step;
4. on FAIL, inspect the exact assertion/log, perform the smallest lawful repair, and rerun;
5. repeat until the next material boundary is reached.

A status update is not a material boundary.
