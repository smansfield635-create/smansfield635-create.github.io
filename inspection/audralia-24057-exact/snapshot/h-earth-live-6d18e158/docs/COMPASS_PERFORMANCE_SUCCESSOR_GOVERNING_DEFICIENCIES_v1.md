# Compass Performance Successor — Governing Deficiencies and Acceptance

## Frozen baseline

The live Compass at merge `449949b5be58bf614209668f3a02dccc7ff62023` is the visual and behavioral floor. The successor may reduce runtime and control-plane cost without removing the two opening thoughts, capability orbit, nested House guides, return behavior, anatomical orientation, keyboard access, touch access, or reduced-motion equivalence.

## Admitted deficiencies

1. **Authority concentration:** the 970-line carousel controller owns unrelated statement, capability, House, brain, and input responsibilities.
2. **Render custody:** the brain requests frames while rear, offscreen, or page-hidden.
3. **Anatomical expression:** the procedural 2D sketch lacks genuine volumetric mass and lighting.
4. **Guidance location:** House guidance is detached from the parent decision and nested active guide.

- The statement carousel uses an atomic local index, opacity transition, and single arrival shimmer; it has no blur field, persistent `will-change`, rear spatial staging, or generated sparkle nodes.
- The brain uses a lit depth-tested WebGL mesh with independently modeled hemispheres, cerebellum, and brainstem.
- Brain animation runs only when visible, foregrounded, page-visible, and motion-permitted.
- The House parent names the availability of Jeeves, Elara, and Auren before entry.
- The nested state says: “Swipe for another House guide. Tap the clear guide to talk.”
- Desktop, tablet, phone, keyboard, pointer, reduced-motion, overflow, one-neighbor, return, and suspension gates pass at the exact candidate head.
- Requalification is required only when changed bytes intersect these authorities or their verifier.
