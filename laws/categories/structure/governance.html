01 / 04

Authority

  1. Decision right

    Practical
    Authority is not volume, preference, or proximity. It is the recognized right to decide, preserve, enforce, and answer for a rule. Name who owns the rule.
    Engineering
    In engineering terms, authority maps to ownership records, permission models, administrative roles, maintainers, approval rights, policy owners, and source-of-truth custody. Separate read access from change authority.
    Empirical
    For authority requires more than a title. It requires records showing that decision rights, enforcement rights, and responsibility are recognized and used. Strong evidence: approval records, ownership registry, change authority logs, enforcement history.

  2. Rule custody

    Practical
    Authority is not volume, preference, or proximity. It is the recognized right to decide, preserve, enforce, and answer for a rule. Name who may change the rule.
    Engineering
    In engineering terms, authority maps to ownership records, permission models, administrative roles, maintainers, approval rights, policy owners, and source-of-truth custody. Separate implementation control from governance control.
    Empirical
    For authority requires more than a title. It requires records showing that decision rights, enforcement rights, and responsibility are recognized and used. Useful evidence: role maps, maintainer records, delegation receipts, review trails.

  3. Enforcement role

    Practical
    Authority is not volume, preference, or proximity. It is the recognized right to decide, preserve, enforce, and answer for a rule. Name who enforces the rule when pressure rises.
    Engineering
    In engineering terms, authority maps to ownership records, permission models, administrative roles, maintainers, approval rights, policy owners, and source-of-truth custody. Require explicit approval for rule mutation.
    Empirical
    For authority requires more than a title. It requires records showing that decision rights, enforcement rights, and responsibility are recognized and used. Failure mode: informal influence overrides declared authority.

  4. Responsibility

    Practical
    Authority is not volume, preference, or proximity. It is the recognized right to decide, preserve, enforce, and answer for a rule. Name who is accountable when the rule fails.
    Engineering
    In engineering terms, authority maps to ownership records, permission models, administrative roles, maintainers, approval rights, policy owners, and source-of-truth custody. Preserve audit trails for authority-sensitive changes.
    Empirical
    For authority requires more than a title. It requires records showing that decision rights, enforcement rights, and responsibility are recognized and used. Boundary: authority without accountability becomes power, not governance. Claim boundary: Authority without accountability becomes power, not governance.
