The cockpit principle

Understand the world without confusing observation with control.

The cockpit orients, explains and routes. It does not pretend that inspection is production telemetry, that evidence is certainty, or that looking at the machinery grants authority to change it.

That separation lets the world stay understandable as its systems become more sophisticated.
