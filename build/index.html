01 · Meaning

Operationally demonstrated.

The governed software-construction platform is self-assessed at Software TRL 7 against published NASA software TRL criteria.

The practical meaning: the integrated platform has been demonstrated operating under intended conditions rather than existing only as a design or isolated prototype.

02 · Criteria & integrated system

The claim applies to an integrated platform.

The assessment concerns the bounded software-construction system and its governed operational machinery as an integrated whole. It is not a blanket maturity label for every page, experiment, research claim or future product.

TRL 7 is used here as a structured maturity threshold, not as a marketing superlative.
