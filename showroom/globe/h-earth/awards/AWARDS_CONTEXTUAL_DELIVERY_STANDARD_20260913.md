## 1. Governing editorial law

The Awards Chamber is not a repository explanation surface. It is a judge-facing significance and proof surface.

The governing delivery sequence is:

`DISTINCTIVE CLAIM -> CONCRETE MANIFESTATION -> WHY IT IS EXCEPTIONAL -> CLAIM BOUNDARY -> INSPECT`

Do not lead with internal architecture, repository vocabulary, biography, category metadata, or implementation ceremony. Translate technical sophistication into human significance first; expose deeper evidence on demand.

### Top carousel — achievement layer

Question answered: **What did Diamond Gate Bridge accomplish, and why should a judge care?**

Required sequence:

`MEMORABLE CLAIM -> VISIBLE/LIVE PROOF -> SIGNIFICANCE -> BOUNDARY -> INSPECT LIVE WORK`

The top carousel is not a product catalog, award-category list, chapter library, film library, technical taxonomy, sales surface, or control-plane explainer.

### Bottom carousel — chapter / cinematic layer

Question answered: **How does the award case hold up when examined through a specific judging lens?**

Required sequence:

`CHAPTER PROPOSITION -> CONCRETE EVIDENCE -> FILM WHEN AVAILABLE -> BOUNDED CLAIM -> POTENTIAL CORRESPONDENCE -> INSPECT LIVE WORK`

The lower system is the extensible chapter and film archive. Its current six chapters are not a permanent cardinality limit. Additional chapters must update surrounding cardinality language rather than inheriting stale six-chapter copy.

## 2. Award-facing biases

1. **Make the case before explaining the machinery.** A judge should understand significance before encountering repository or governance vocabulary.
2. **Proof follows immediately after the claim.** Every major proposition must resolve into something visible, interactive, filmed, inspectable, or directly routed.
3. **Concrete manifestation outranks abstraction.** Name Compass, rooms, thresholds, H-Earth, Audralia, characters, products, research, Governance, and other actual surfaces when they are the evidence.
4. **Achievement before biography.** Creator provenance may magnify scale but may not create significance or substitute for evidence.
5. **Scale before explanation.** Establish what was accomplished before explaining how it was produced.
6. **Claim before jargon.** Terms such as exact heads, receipts, qualification, fail-closed, authority, and runtime belong behind OPEN after the plain-language proposition is established.
7. **Top and bottom may share objects, not editorial jobs.** The top states why the work matters. The bottom tests that work through a chapter-specific award lens.
8. **Readers prove; they do not merely describe.** Opened states should answer: what exists, why it matters, what is bounded, and where it can be inspected.
9. **Current state and future ambition stay separate.** No future gameplay, AI, platform, research, award, or product state may be implied as already complete.
10. **Provenance is amplification, not evidence substitution.** “Independently built” changes the scale of the accomplishment, not the standard of proof.
11. **Commercial conversion is tertiary inside the Awards narrative.** The immediate award case should favor inspect, follow, see, enter, and verify language.
12. **Claim boundaries remain visible but subordinate.** Transparency should strengthen credibility without becoming the emotional conclusion of a section.
13. **Integration does not mean homogenization.** Distinct systems retain distinct roles, maturity, authority, and runtime boundaries while participating in one estate.
14. **Category correspondence is metadata, not the story.** Let the visitor experience the case first; show potential award correspondence as a subordinate layer.
15. **The Award page is a significance surface.** Its local role is `BODY_OF_WORK -> SIGNIFICANCE -> EXAMINATION -> PROOF -> TRANSPARENT_BOUNDARY`, not global directory, generic Build landing page, or internal technical manual.
16. **Preserve editorial restraint.** Avoid slogan stacking, repeated “the achievement is” constructions, excessive self-congratulation, or five cards that sound mechanically templated.

### Reader compression rule

A top-card reader should normally fit four beats:

1. **What exists.**
2. **Why that is unusual or significant.**
3. **What is not being claimed.**
4. **Where to inspect it.**

Do not restate the headline in multiple paragraphs. Do not make every reader end with the same syntactic formula.

### Trophy reader grammar

Each lower reader should answer, in order:

- **The proposition:** what this chapter asks the judge to evaluate.
- **The evidence:** two or three concrete things that exist.
- **The film:** visual proof when available.
- **The boundary:** current versus prospective state.
- **Potential correspondence:** category fit as subordinate metadata.
- **Inspect the work:** direct route.

## 5. Section-level delivery

### Hero

Preserve the governing question:

`What happens when an idea becomes a world?`

Deliver scale before biography. The hero should establish that one independent experiment became a connected digital estate spanning browser-native worlds, spatial navigation, original characters, interactive products, research systems and governed engineering.

Creator provenance follows only after the body of work is established. Preferred governing statement:

`The work was developed independently by a single creator. That does not change the standard by which it should be judged. It changes the scale of the accomplishment being judged.`

### Achievement intro

Use:

`One independent digital estate. Five dimensions of the achievement.`

Supporting line:

`Each card isolates one dimension of the accomplishment. Bring it forward, then open it to inspect the work behind the claim.`

### Award-cycle threshold

Preserve:

`The question is no longer whether it can be built. The question is how it stands when placed beside the best.`

This is the correct narrative state change from accomplishment discovery to comparative evaluation.

### Trophy Standard transition

Replace UI-explanatory language with award-facing narrative:

`Five achievements establish the case. Six chapters put it under examination.`

Supporting line:

`Navigation. World construction. Original IP. Situated intelligence. Coherence. Independent craft.`

Do not explain “the first carousel” and “this carousel” unless accessibility or interaction instruction specifically requires it. The content should carry the architecture without narrating the UI.

### Potential correspondence

Keep potential award correspondence visible but visually subordinate. It is useful classification evidence, not primary prose. The judge should experience the proposition and evidence before category metadata.

### Continuation labels

Within the two carousel sections, prefer `inspect`, `follow`, `see`, `enter`, and `verify` language. Preserve existing destinations unless separately authorized. Direct sales language such as `Start your project` may remain only downstream of the award case, not as the immediate resolution of an achievement or chapter.

## 6. Award-facing evaluation matrix

The chamber should be able to support the following judging dimensions without changing its architecture:

- navigation / interaction / usability;
- immersive storytelling / immersive technology / virtual worlds / 3D craft;
- original IP / integrated experience / platform design;
- AI experience / character design / real-time engagement;
- data experience / systems thinking / interactive tools;
- independent technical craft / innovation;
- evidence quality / credibility / transparency;
- emotional and narrative impact;
- premium editorial restraint;
- jury efficiency and rapid comprehension.

No single section needs to say all of these. The page succeeds when the complete sequence makes them inspectable without forcing the judge to decode internal architecture.

## 7. Final delivery sequence

The intended visitor journey is:

`HERO: SCALE AND PREMISE`
`-> ACHIEVEMENT CAROUSEL: WHY THE WORK MATTERS`
`-> AWARD THRESHOLD: HOW DOES IT STAND BESIDE THE BEST?`
`-> TROPHY STANDARD: SIX SPECIFIC CASES`
`-> FILM + LIVE EVIDENCE: PROVE IT`
`-> DIRECT INSPECTION: LET THE JUDGE TEST IT`
`-> INDEPENDENT CONSTRUCTION: AMPLIFY THE ESTABLISHED ACHIEVEMENT`
`-> CONTINUE INTO THE ESTATE`

Independent-construction provenance is strongest after the work has already established significance. It should magnify an achievement the judge has seen, not ask biography to manufacture one.
